Pre-registration: the heterozygosity ceiling, read against a parent-free answer key
====================================================================================

Written 10 November 2026 against commit cc8559b, BEFORE the stage logic is changed. The laboratory's
material carries one parent, reconstructed from the children. So nothing here may depend on how many
parents are loaded: the stage, every refusal of it and every intensity claim must come out the same
with one reconstructed parent as with two measured ones.

WHAT IS ALREADY MEASURED
------------------------
A founder panel's shared homozygotes stand in for a pair of parents that always agree (cc8559b). Over
the 31 GSE148488 cells with a published Table S3 karyotype, each read against a panel built WITHOUT
its own parents:

  drop-in       r = 0.9983 against the drop-in read where both measured parents share a homozygote;
                panel = 0.932 x parents + 0.0424. The offset is real heterozygosity at variants four or
                five founders happen to lack.
  information   the two arrays the parent-based ratio calls noise read 1.063 and 1.282 against the
                panel; every other cell reads 0.318 or under both ways. Same verdict at the shipped
                NO_INFORMATION_MIN on all 31.
  stage         identical with two measured parents and with one father reconstructed from five of his
                pronuclei, on all 31, because nothing in it reads a parent.

THE CHANGE, FIXED NOW
---------------------
Only an array over the heterozygosity ceiling is touched, so no array the ladder accepts today can
change. Over it, with the panel keys supplied, three outcomes, each a physical cause:

  no-genotype-signal  the panel information ratio is at or over NO_INFORMATION_MIN: the calls carry
                      nothing about any genome. Refused, as now, under its real cause.
  second-genome       heterozygosity with drop-in removed, (h - d) / (1 - d), is still over the ceiling,
                      with d the panel drop-in put on the parent scale by the line above. More
                      heterozygous than one genome can be even after drop-in. Refused.
  one genome          otherwise. The array gets a stage, read from the ladder at the heterozygosity it
                      would show at the drop-in the boundaries were measured with (DROP_IN, 0.0435). It
                      is marked as drop-in corrected, with d stated. Drop-in corrected heterozygosity
                      does not separate one copy from two on this material (the consult: haploid
                      pronuclei 0.002 to 0.014, diploid progeny from 0.015), so the haploid rung is not
                      reachable this way: ploidy is diploid only where the BAF band says so, and
                      unknown otherwise.

On an array so marked, intensity claims (whole-chromosome copy number, the copy-number scan, the event
list) are withheld under cause `no-intensity-reference`: a normal majority to normalise against has not
been shown, and the localised-excess test that would show it reads 0.07 to 0.18 on every array over the
ceiling, chaotic or not, against a threshold of 0.50, so it cannot. Allele claims run. Its label says
which is which.

Haploid products for a reconstruction: a product over the ceiling that comes out `one genome` with its
drop-in-corrected heterozygosity in the haploid range is accepted, since the operator asserts it is
haploid and the test is only whether the array contradicts that.

RESULT
------
Census over the 31 published cells, two arms: both measured parents, and the father reconstructed
from five of his pronuclei alone (the laboratory's configuration). Each cell read against a founder
panel built without its own parents.

  P1  HOLDS. GSM4552418 and GSM4552422 are failed as no-genotype-signal in both arms.
  P2  HOLDS. The seven are staged, six as blastomere and GSM4552387 as single-cell, each with its
      drop-in stated. None is second-genome.
  P3  HOLDS. Stage and its refusal identical across the arms on 31 of 31.
  P4  HOLDS. Whole-chromosome copy number identical across the arms, 19 calls each, none on the seven.
  P5  HOLDS. On the 22 arrays under the ceiling, no stage and no claim changed in either arm.
  P6  HOLDS. Two measured parents: 82 confirmed lost parents, 4 contradicted, 1 wrong parent, all as
      before.
  P7  HOLDS, narrowly. In the one-parent arm, 15 allele claims now stand on the seven that had none:
      8 confirmed, 7 contradicted. They include the three hand-called del(6q)(pat) deletions that
      5.42.1 recorded as lost to this refusal on 26 October, and both paternal monosomy 5s. Of the 7
      contradicted, 5 are on GSM4472405, a cell published chaotic, and 2 are chr13 and chr15 on
      embryo-1 biopsies (GSM4552387, GSM4552389), where the over-refusal consult found allele and
      intensity channels agreeing against Table S3 on every sister biopsy: adjudicate, not counted.
  P8  FAILS. GSM4774684 and GSM4774686 are no longer refused as not a genome, but the BAF band called
      them diploid, and they are haploid by micromanipulation: drop-in fills the band exactly as it
      inflates heterozygosity. Fixed before release, so a corrected array's ploidy is unknown however
      its band reads; no claim reads the stage's ploidy, so P1 to P7 are unaffected. They are still
      not accepted as haploid products for a reconstruction, because no test here separates one copy
      from two under drop-in this heavy. On every haploid pronucleus the panel drop-in equals the
      heterozygosity to within 0.011, which is what one copy should read and is the basis of a test
      that could; it is not built. GSM4774687, a haploid pronucleus reading trophectoderm at 0.250,
      and GSM4774681, reading blastomere at 0.121, sit under the ceiling and are not reached.

KNOWN 1:1 GAP, PRE-EXISTING
---------------------------
The copy-number scan skips any chromosome already explained as a whole event, and a chromosome the
alleles name as missing a parent counts as explained. Those names exist only with two measured
parents, so copy-number segments can differ between the arms on exactly those chromosomes. Pinned in
scoreSample.check.ts; not changed here.
