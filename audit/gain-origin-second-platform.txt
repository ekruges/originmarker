THE ALLELE SHARE ON A SECOND PLATFORM AND A SECOND LABORATORY

GEO GSE60909, Zamani Esteki et al. 2015 (Am J Hum Genet, PMID 25983246). Illumina
HumanCytoSNP-12, day-4 blastomeres amplified by MDA, with the mother and father of each
family on the same chip. Every other measurement in this repository is on Affymetrix Axiom.

THE STATISTIC ONLY, NOT THE SHIPPED PIPELINE. The pipeline reports nothing at all on these
cells: their heterozygosity reads haploid or failed to the stage inference and the zygosity
call returns unknown, so the allele scan is gated off on every one of them (audit/
gain-origin-pgd.ts, 16 blastomeres of PGD008, 0 chromosomes scored). What is scored below is
the same arithmetic computed directly: the median paternal share at markers where the
parents are opposite homozygotes, against the median and the median absolute deviation of
the cell's own other chromosomes. It says the statistic transfers; it does not say the tool
reads this platform, and the tool does not.

The answer key is the paper's own, read from its tables, text and Figure S14. Only two of
these origins are independently confirmed, both by STR typing on a sister cell: chromosome
13 of E08_Bl346 and chromosome 22 of E09. The rest are the paper's haplarithmisis calls.
