
MOSAIC LOSS vs UNRESOLVED READING, FROM BAF ALONE
Methods consult, 2026-08-14. Egli lab / Berrie, parental-origin calling from Axiom UK Biobank arrays.

Everything below is measured on GSE148488 (135 arrays x 825,656 markers, GRCh37) already ingested in
this project: 26 blastomere d4, 40-41 TE biopsy d6, 16 ESC line bulk, 4 ESC single-cell WGA, 15
pronuclei, 12 parental bulk gDNA. Both parents are genotyped there, which gives an obligate-het
oracle your production case does not have; that oracle is used to bound what the one-parent method
can possibly achieve. Your own chr16 arrays are not in hand, so verdicts on your two biopsies are
consistency checks against matched material, not measurements of your samples.

Headline: three of the four premises in the write-up are wrong, and correcting them changes what the
method should do. The expectation formula is wrong by the factor (2-f). The claim that mosaicism
lives in the unresolved readings is false at every f. The dispersion/modality statistic is the
weakest of the candidates, not the right one. The guard is miscalibrated in both directions. And the
error mode you most fear is worse than you think: below f=0.3 the dominant failure is not a false
positive, it is a confidently wrong parent, and the null is biased toward one parent.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1. THE SHAPE ARGUMENT AND THE EXPECTED DISTRIBUTION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1a. E[BAF] = 0.5 + 0.5f is wrong. Correct: 1/(2-f).

An array reads POOLED allele dosage over all DNA in the well, not the average of per-cell BAF. The
loss removes template, so it changes the denominator too. At a loaded-parent-hom marker, oriented
with the loaded parent's own allele low, other parent transmitting the alternative allele:

  loaded-parent copy lost in fraction f:  own-allele dose (1-f), other dose 1, total (2-f)
                                          E[BAF] = 1/(2-f),  displacement +f/(4-2f)
  other-parent copy lost in fraction f:   E[BAF] = (1-f)/(2-f), displacement -f/(4-2f)

0.5+0.5f is the mean of per-cell BAFs, which implicitly assumes a monosomic cell contributes as much
DNA as a disomic one. It overstates the displacement by exactly (2-f):

  f          0.05    0.10    0.20    0.30    0.50    0.70    1.00
  1/(2-f)   0.5128  0.5263  0.5556  0.5882  0.6667  0.7692  1.0000
  0.5+0.5f  0.5250  0.5500  0.6000  0.6500  0.7500  0.8500  1.0000
  overstate  1.95x   1.90x   1.80x   1.70x   1.50x   1.30x   1.00x

Max absolute error 0.0858 BAF units at f = 2-sqrt(2) = 0.586. Consequences that bite:
- your 0.65 band edge is crossed at f=0.46, not 0.30; your 0.85 edge at f=0.82, not 0.70
- inverting an observed shift d gives f = 4d/(1+2d), not f = 2d, so the stated inversion
  UNDERSTATES mosaic fraction ~1.8x (d=0.05 -> f=0.182, not 0.100)
- the shift is exactly additive in logit BAF: -log(1-f), independent of baseline. That is the form
  to build the likelihood in.

Note the formula is not wrong in general, it is wrong for the mechanism it was written for. For loss
WITH reduplication of the remaining homologue (copy-neutral LOH, present in this dataset - eysES5
chr9, obligate-het median BAF 0.836 at log2R 0.034 vs genome -0.001) total DNA is unchanged and
E[BAF] = (1+f)/2, algebraically identical to 0.5+0.5f. So simple loss and loss-plus-reduplication
differ ~2x in implied f, and which one chr16 is must be settled from log2R before any f is quoted.

Validation on real data: f=1 endpoint confirmed on haploid pronuclei (maternal PN median oriented
BAF 0.9694, 90.2% above 0.85, 7 arrays; paternal PN 0.0748, 75.1% below 0.15, 8 arrays) and on real
whole-chromosome losses in blastomeres (7 chrom-arrays median 0.9662; 6 at 0.0303). Real trisomies
sit at 0.9993-1.0007 of the pooled-dosage prediction across six stage x direction cells. The
CURVATURE could not be validated - no region of known intermediate f exists in this dataset - so the
mid-range rests on the algebra plus the independent agreement of this project's own
mosaic_titration.csv, whose expected_BAF_CN column already equals 1/(2-f) to 4.9e-5. The correct
values were already in the project; the spec contradicts an artifact you already have.

1b. Mosaicism does NOT show up in the unresolved readings. The premise is false.

Built empirical logit-BAF noise kernels from 109,726-692,417 real obligate-het readings per stage,
then applied the corrected shift. The unresolved zone's share of the total band redistribution never
reaches a majority at any f, at any stage: max 0.478 (TE, call rate>0.90), 0.355 (blastomere,
cr>0.90), 0.499 (ESC bulk), 0.489 (parent bulk gDNA).

Below f=0.3 in a blastomere the unresolved share is 0.021-0.197 while the extremes take 0.570-0.920.
At low f the mosaic shift is expressed as a change in the allele-dropout BALANCE - readings tipping
from one extreme to the other - not as accumulating intermediate readings. The population you are
characterising is the one the signal mostly is not in.

Worse, the low band is nearly invariant: injecting a real loss at f=0.2 changes the low band by
-0.02% to -0.5% and the unresolved fraction by a factor of only 1.005-1.05, because the loss moves
only the ~10-16% of markers that are truly het, and moves them middle -> high, not into the gaps.

1c. "Displaced but still narrow, and noise is wide" - the qualitative half is right, the statistical
conclusion is wrong.

A pooled dosage change is multiplicative in the allele ratio, hence a rigid TRANSLATION in logit BAF
with shape unchanged. Measured: logit SD is invariant to f to machine precision (TE 0.9674 at every
f from 0 to 0.7; blastomere 2.1087 at every f). On the raw BAF scale it moves the wrong way for your
argument - a mosaic shift makes the distribution slightly NARROWER (blastomere BAF SD 0.3172 at f=0
-> 0.2942 at f=0.7). Band-width invariance is independently visible in your own
baf_dosage_bands_measured.csv (per-marker SD euploid/mat_gain/pat_gain 0.3079/0.3005/0.3010
blastomere; 0.1410/0.1334/0.1337 TE).

So there is no dispersion or modality contrast to exploit: a displaced unimodal band is not a
resolvable mixture. What IS informative in the unresolved population is its upper/lower ASYMMETRY -
TE cr>0.90 upper-side fraction 0.530 (f=0), 0.637 (0.1), 0.836 (0.3), 0.940 (0.5).

1d. Discreteness and allelic bias.

f is quantised at 1/n. For n=5 the smallest non-zero step is f=0.2 -> BAF 0.5556 (+0.0556 in one
jump); n=10 gives 0.5263. Under binomial sampling from an F=0.10 embryo with n=5, P(f=0) = 0.5905 -
most biopsies of a genuinely mosaic embryo contain no mosaic cell at all. Because 1/(2-f) is convex,
E[BAF] under the binomial prior slightly exceeds the value at f=F (gap 0.0014-0.0191).

Allelic bias, measured at markers GENETICALLY REQUIRED to be het (both parents informative):
middle-band depletion 1.3% ESC bulk, 3.2% parent bulk, 51.8% ESC single WGA, 58.5% TE, 68.3%
blastomere. On blastomeres 61% of known hets fail to read as het and 36% read as a confident
HOMOZYGOTE. Critically, dropout is near-SYMMETRIC between alleles (median asymmetry -0.008
blastomere, +0.016 TE; median logit bias -0.015 and +0.036 against a 0.357 shift at f=0.3), so it
inflates variance without moving the band centre. Exception: the single 2PN zygote array (+0.115).

Your 4.0% middle-band observation reproduces (87.9% own vs your 88.1; middle 4.19% vs your 4.0) but
your mechanism for it is wrong, and the comparator is wrong. Panel allele frequency should not be
used: both parents are genotyped, so expected child-het among loaded-parent-hom markers is computed
directly and is donor-specific (11.1% to 19.9%) - a ~3-fold mismatch, not an order of magnitude. The
depletion is then fully accounted for by dropout with ~1pp left over (array 4: expected het 11.14% x
measured het recovery 25.78% = 2.87% predicted vs 4.04% observed, residual +1.17pp; blastomere
median residual +1.06pp). True hets are not being "pushed out of the middle" by something acting on
hets; only 25-32% of them are recovered as intermediate readings because the rest dropped an allele.
This matters for the fix: dropout is a per-marker Bernoulli rate measurable on obligate-het markers
of the SAME array, hence correctable, whereas "hets pushed out" would imply recalibrating band edges.

Also: the direction of the middle-band artifact REVERSES with quality. Low-quality arrays INFLATE
the middle band (blastomere rows 109/110/113, call rate 0.565-0.753: observed middle 42.5-44.7%
against 4.9-5.0% predicted). The same statistic moves both ways depending on call rate. And at
markers where both parents are hom for the same allele, so the middle band is genetically
impossible, the false-middle rate runs 0.21% in the highest-intensity bin, 0.83% at normal
intensity, and 29.6% below log2R -1.5 - a 140-fold range driven purely by template amount.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q2. THE RIGHT STATISTIC
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Answer: the signed mean shift of the central mass, self-referenced against the same array's own
clean genome. Not dispersion, not modality, not a count.

Head-to-head at 1% FPR with the null taken from REAL drifting-diploid segments (not simulated
noise), TE whole chromosome:

  statistic                        f=0.10  f=0.20  f=0.30  f=0.50
  mean shift (centroid z)            0.03    0.08    0.17    0.51
  unresolved count (current guard)    0.01    0.01    0.02    0.17
  dispersion of unresolved           <=0.06 everywhere except bulk ESC
  multimodality (dip)                 powerless on the unresolved subset

The dip test is the one candidate carrying information the mean-shift statistics do not - it reaches
0.30 at f=0.50 on blastomere chromosomes when applied to the FULL analysis set rather than the
unresolved subset. Keep it as a secondary flag, not the primary.

Recommended primary, concretely: orient loaded parent's allele low; keep loaded-parent-hom markers;
take the central window 0.20 <= BAF <= 0.80 (median 273 markers per 12 Mb on TE; 1,760-3,187 per
chromosome); compute window centroid MINUS the same quantity on that array's own clean chromosomes;
z = shift / sqrt(VIF*sd_window^2/n_window + tau^2) with stage-specific VIF and drift floor tau.

The self-referencing is not optional. Raw one-parent centroid null medians are -0.0014 (ESC bulk),
-0.0127 (ESC single WGA), -0.0311 (TE), -0.0230 (blastomere), with only 10.7-18.5% of null units
positive at 12 Mb (Wilcoxon p<1e-6). Subtracting the array's own genome-wide centroid brings the
null median to -0.001..+0.006 and centres z (mean -0.06..+0.11, sd 0.41-1.01).

Note your specified "z of the segment mean over markers passing a band threshold" fails outright
(power 0.00 on blastomere and TE at all f): selecting BAF>=0.15 retains only 27-39% true hets, so
the mean is dominated by wherever the contaminating homozygous low tail sits. The central window
plus self-referencing is the fix.

Null width, measured, because it sets everything: VIF 1.04/2.14/1.74/2.31 at 12 Mb and
0.94/3.83/3.34/3.63 per chromosome (ESC bulk / ESC single WGA / TE / blastomere). Bulk ESC is
essentially white; every amplified stage is not. The floor is systematic within-array DRIFT, not
sampling: drift floor tau 0.0011/0.0153/0.0123/0.0193 BAF per chromosome, against a sampling term of
0.0019-0.0102, so drift/sampling is 1.44-1.90 on amplified material. Effective independent markers
saturate at 247-268 per chromosome from a nominal 896-1101 obligate-het markers - adding markers
does not help past that point. BAF autocorrelation integral length is 1.00-2.18 Mb, so a 12 Mb
segment holds only 5.5-12 independent blocks whatever the marker density.

Marker dilution is the other half of the intuition gap. Per 12 Mb: 3,146 markers with a reading ->
2,223 loaded-parent-hom -> 223-365 expected truly het -> 78-115 obligate het with both parents.
"3,345 markers with a reading" is the wrong denominator for any power statement; only the last
number carries the loss signal.

Detection floor at 1% FPR (f50 / f80):

  stage             12 Mb segment        whole chromosome
  ESC line bulk     0.056 / -           0.025 / 0.040
  ESC single WGA    0.560 / -           0.213 / 0.443
  TE biopsy d6      not reached         0.448 / -
  blastomere d4     not reached         not reached (to f=0.70)

12 Mb segments are out of reach on every amplified stage. A one-parent, one-array blastomere has no
floor at 1% FPR at any f up to 0.70. Genotyping the second parent is worth ~3x in detectable f and
is the single largest available improvement without changing statistic: the both-parents oracle
reaches f50 = 0.135 (TE chromosome) and 0.296 (blastomere chromosome) where the one-parent statistic
reaches 0.448 and never, respectively.

Q3. DOES INTENSITY ADD INDEPENDENT INFORMATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Yes, the channels are genuinely independent at the measurement level, so a joint term is sound and
not double counting - but it does not rescue the single blastomere.

Your log2(1-f/2) is CORRECT (total template (1-f)*2 + f*1 = 2-f), so log2R = -0.152 at f=0.20,
-0.322 at f=0.40. Note log2r_mdd_by_stage.csv sets min_mosaic_f_CN1 = mdd directly, which is ~1.37x
optimistic on its own terms; the correct inversion is f = 2(1-2^-mdd).

Independence, measured on technical-replicate DIFFERENCES (52 array pairs from 20 replicate groups,
53 arrays): r = -0.055 (ESC bulk, 24 pairs), -0.007 (TE, 15 pairs), +0.036 (blastomere, 13 pairs).
At |r|<=0.06
quadrature addition is safe. But replicate MEANS correlate up to -0.259 (TE) and -0.33..-0.46 for
unresolved fraction at W=10000, sign indicating a common amplification artefact (poor amplification
lowers intensity and raises unresolved BAF) rather than a common copy-number signal. If TE biopsies
are the main application, fit a residual correlation parameter rather than assuming orthogonality.

Single-array f80 at W=3300 (BAF / intensity / joint):
  ESC line bulk    0.100 / 0.436 / 0.098   (+2.0%)
  TE biopsy d6     0.525 / 0.505 / 0.387   (+26.3%)
  ESC single WGA   0.553 / 0.327 / 0.295   (+46.7%)
  blastomere d4    0.988 / 0.660 / 0.603   (+38.9%)

Two things worth internalising. First, intensity is the STRONGER channel on WGA material -
z(log2R)/z(BAF) at f=0.20 is 0.20 on bulk ESC but 1.16 (TE), 2.03 (ESC single WGA), 2.31
(blastomere) - because WGA destroys allelic balance more than total dosage. Second, intensity is
PARENT-BLIND: on haploid pronuclei (a natural f=1 experiment with known origin) autosomal median
log2R is 0.0837 maternal vs 0.0750 paternal, t=0.64 p=0.536, indistinguishable, while the oriented
high-band fraction at father-hom markers is 0.1113 vs 0.0050, t=85.0 p=1.3e-15. So intensity can
inform the copy-number STATE and never the ORIGIN.

That makes them complementary on the state axis, which is the real argument for combining. At f=0.20
(log2R / oriented BAF shift): loss of loaded copy -0.152/+0.056; loss of other copy -0.152/-0.056;
CN-neutral mosaic UPD 0.000/+0.100; mosaic trisomy +0.138/-0.045; contamination 0.000/0.000.
Intensity separates loss from UPD, which BAF cannot; BAF separates the two losses, which intensity
cannot.

Caveat on the intensity floor: drift dominates there too. Blastomere W=2400 technical same-DNA log2R
SD is 0.0077 against single-array window-to-window SD 0.2209, a factor of 29, of which 0.1848 is
between-chromosome offsets - i.e. whole-chromosome-scale drift indistinguishable from a
whole-chromosome copy change. And it does not average down: 0.2083 -> 0.1400 from W=600 to W=10000
(17x more markers), a factor of 1.49 against the 4.1 white noise predicts.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q4. CAN THIS EVER BE DONE ON A SINGLE CELL
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Your assumption is right in spirit but the guard is right for the wrong reason, and there are real
exceptions.

A single cell cannot be fractionally mosaic, so an intermediate POOLED dosage in one cell is not a
mosaic fraction. But intermediate readings in one cell are not always measurement failure either:
- S-phase replication. A locus that has replicated on one homologue only sits at 2:1 dosage. Cleavage
  stage blastomeres are cycling and biopsied without cell-cycle synchronisation.
- Subchromosomal duplication or unbalanced rearrangement, which genuinely gives 2:1 at CN3.
- Copy-neutral LOH plus a duplication elsewhere in the same interval.
- Two nuclei in one "cell", or a binucleate blastomere - a real pooled mixture in one sample.
The measured signature that distinguishes these from noise is that they are SEGMENTAL and coherent
in position, while dropout noise is per-marker independent. That is where the spatial argument you
wanted actually applies - along the chromosome, not within the reading distribution.

Empirically the single blastomere is hopeless for f-estimation regardless: no detection floor at 1%
FPR at any f to 0.70 for one parent and one array, per-marker BAF SD 0.312, and 61% of obligate hets
failing to read as het. Refusing is correct there. But refuse on measured call rate and dropout rate
directly, not on the unresolved-fraction proxy, which is what the current guard actually keys on.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q5. WHAT THE LITERATURE ALREADY DOES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Verdict: no published statistic solves your problem. Adopt definitions, re-derive floors.

The split is clean. Methods built for EMBRYOS that stratify BAF by parental genotype publish no
mosaic-fraction floor, no error model and no noise-vs-mosaicism test, and all need both parents.
Methods that are statistically mature and publish floors down to 1-4% were validated on bulk
multi-cell gDNA and need either a huge phasing cohort or a complete trio.

- Haplarithmisis (Zamani Esteki 2015). Its stated purpose is to make haplotyping SURVIVE WGA
  distortion of BAF - WGA artefact is absorbed as noise by the parental-category structure, never
  tested against a mosaic hypothesis. Where the current implementation reports a mosaic level
  (Nat Commun 2024) it extracts segmented phased BAF at the logR breakpoint and looks it up against
  the Conlin reference dataset: a lookup, with no null distribution, no CI, no FPR. Resolution
  stated only as ">10%".
- APCAD (Verdyck 2021, 2023). Maternal-contribution BAF at markers where parents are hom for
  different alleles, decision boundary <36.4% or >63.6%. 57/59 meiotic trisomies and 57/57 monosomies
  detected - but 32/7,436 chromosomes called normal by karyomapping showed the same skew and could
  not be adjudicated. Needs BOTH parents by construction.
- GENType (Verdyck 2024), the closest material match - 1,523 TE biopsies, ~102,000 SNPs, mean ADO
  18.6%, drop-in 1.57%. Uses BAF as a BINARY only, and states verbatim that differentiating
  mosaicism degrees through BAF analysis could not be conducted due to technical limitations. Its
  cell-mixing ladder was 10 cells in 30% steps, logR only.
- MoChA (Loh 2018/2020), same Axiom platform family, 607,525 variants, bulk blood from 151,202
  people: cell fractions to 1%, 5,901/8,342 events below 5%. Requires a ~150k-sample phasing
  reference. Two directly reusable items: the sample QC gate "exclude if median SD(BAF) at het sites
  > 0.11", and the precedent that 29% of its events were left UNASSIGNED to loss/CNN-LOH/gain
  because power to detect imbalance exceeded power to resolve copy state - the published response to
  your exact dilemma was to report the event without a class rather than force a call.
- triPOD (trios, ~600k SNPs): BAF deviation <0.04 maps to <8.5% mosaicism for UPD/deletion but <24%
  for amplification. Authors state validating false positives below 5% mosaic is largely infeasible.
- MAD b-deviation (Illumina 1M): near-perfect ROC only when quality good AND f>=20%; simulated het
  BAF SD 0.03 good / 0.10 noisy - against ~19% ADO on WGA few-cell material.
- Jacobs 2012 (bulk): CNN-LOH and loss detectable at f 7-95%, single-copy GAIN only 22-88%.

Two corrections to premises in the write-up. PGT-A labs do not call mosaicism in a validated
analytical sense: ESHRE's 239-centre survey found 58.7% (54/92) of centres quoting a mosaicism range
had NOT validated their technology for detecting mosaicism, rebiopsy concordance for mosaic calls is
42.6% (123/289) vs 93.7% euploid, and the 20/80 and 30/70 figures are REPORTING bands, not detection
limits. And the widely cited "20% detection limit in cell-line mixtures" is not supported by its
source: the mixtures were made at 0/30/50/70/100%, so 20% was never tested.

Also split your floor by event class: gains are ~3x worse than losses in two independent bulk studies,
because a single-copy gain shifts BAF one third as much.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q6. FAILURE MODE AND ITS DIRECTION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This is the answer that should change your practice most.

The dominant error below f=0.3 is not a false positive for mosaicism. It is a confidently WRONG
PARENT. Scored two-sided at total FPR 1%, with loss of the loaded parent's copy as the true event so
the correct sign is positive, fraction of DETECTIONS naming the wrong parent:

  f                        0.05   0.10   0.15   0.20   0.30   0.50
  TE, 12 Mb segment        1.00   0.86   0.71   0.50   0.15   0.03
  blastomere, chromosome   1.00   1.00   1.00   0.33   0.17   0.00

It does not become tolerable until f >= 0.30. And a wrong call can be arbitrarily confident: on the
honest null with NO event present, the wrong-sign tail of the both-parents oracle median z reaches
-15.86 at the 0.1st percentile and -19.98 at the extreme on TE 12 Mb segments, with 0.6% of null
units beyond |z|=10. The single worst null unit has a signed median BAF shift of -0.464 on a
chromosome independently verified diploid. Posterior 1.0000 on noise is not a bug you fixed; it is
reachable whenever the statistic is not self-referenced.

The failure is DIRECTIONAL, not symmetric, and it points at the wrong parent. The raw one-parent
null median is negative at every amplified stage (TE -0.031, blastomere -0.023), meaning the loaded
parent's own allele appears in excess, which the method reads as the OTHER parent having lost its
copy. The -0.031 on TE is the shift a true event of f=0.117 would produce. So an uncorrected
one-parent method has a standing bias toward calling loss of the parent you did NOT genotype.

Mechanism: dropout at hom markers is symmetric, but the ORIENTATION step is not - the loaded
parent's own allele is the one you can always identify, and dropout of the other parent's allele
therefore moves readings toward the loaded parent's allele more often than the reverse.

Which way my recommendation trades off: it refuses more than you currently do at 12 Mb on amplified
material, and it detects less at low f than the current guard appears to. That is the honest
direction, since the current guard's apparent sensitivity is not sensitivity - its power at f<=0.3 is
0.01-0.02.

YOUR THREE CASES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The blastomere chr1. It is a call-rate artefact end to end, and it is not a blastomere. All ten
quoted figures match array 128 (GSM4774681), a paternal PRONUCLEUS: chr1 27.8/7.2/50.4/5.2/37.3
against your 30.7/7.7/49.7/5.4/37.2, rest-of-genome 83.0/89.1/3.7/0.55/6.7 against your
83.7/88.1/4.0/0.9/7.1. Against real verified-euploid chromosomes matched on call rate 0.25-0.37
(n=25) the chr1 profile is unremarkable (own 0.077 vs 0.053, middle 0.497 vs 0.551, unresolved 0.372
vs 0.357; max discrepancy 0.054). The 3x guard refuses it (ratio 5.24) - right verdict, wrong reason,
since it is refusing on a proxy for call rate. And the underlying biology is the opposite of "pure
noise": the paternal chr1 is genuinely ABSENT, only 10.0% of chr1 markers reach log2R>-0.5 vs 77.2%
elsewhere, so BAF there carries no dosage information and collapses toward 0.5, reading apparently
foreign maternal alleles in 74.7-77.5% of readings - impossible in a paternal-only nucleus. A region
can be a genuine COMPLETE loss and still produce a middle-band-rich profile that a band-occupancy
likelihood reads as "both copies present". That is the failure your v1 hit.

The two chr16 TE biopsies. Ratios 4.27 and 3.39, both refused, but only 1.13x and 1.42x over the
guard's threshold and both inside the ordinary range of clean TE material, whose null 99th percentile
ratio is 6.74 and where 3x already fires on 5.5% of genuinely clean 12 Mb segments. On a correctly
calibrated 1% threshold neither would be refused. Their unresolved counts are quantitatively
consistent with a real mosaic loss (f=0.325 and f=0.292 against a high-quality-TE background of
9.18%, close to your ~7%) - but note the estimate is two-valued because the unresolved count is
non-monotone, and the high branch (f=0.87/0.89) is excluded only by your genotype channel's
"both copies present".

Do NOT treat the agreement between the two biopsies as evidence. Under the null with no event
present, this statistic already correlates 0.726-0.739 between replicate arrays of one biopsy, and
per-12Mb unresolved fraction correlates 0.91. The drift is a reproducible property of the DNA and
its amplification, so two biopsies of one embryo are EXPECTED to agree under the null.

Correct output for these two: "not evaluable at this interval width and stage" - a 12 Mb TE interval
carries ~273 central-window markers and has no floor at any f - not a refusal framed as QC failure,
and not a call.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO CHANGE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Replace 0.5+0.5f with 1/(2-f) and (1-f)/(2-f); build the likelihood in logit space where the
   shift is additive, -log(1-f). Decide simple loss vs loss-with-reduplication from log2R first,
   since they differ ~2x in implied f.
2. Replace the unresolved-fraction guard. It is non-monotone in f, unreachable in blastomeres
   (max attainable ratio 2.03x vs a 3x threshold), and refuses 2.1-10.7% of genuinely clean
   segments. Split its two jobs: QC on measured call rate and dropout rate at obligate-het markers;
   evidence on the signed centroid shift.
3. Self-reference every statistic against the same array's own clean genome. Without it the null
   carries a -0.031 (TE) directional bias pointing at the wrong parent.
4. Report a copy-number event without a parental class when |z| clears detection but the sign is not
   secure - the MoChA precedent, 29% of events left unassigned. This is the direct answer to "a
   wrong answer looks exactly like a right one".
5. Do not attempt parental calls on 12 Mb intervals in amplified material, and do not attempt them
   at all on single blastomeres with one parent. Report not-evaluable.
6. Genotype the second parent wherever possible: ~3x in detectable f, the largest single gain
   available.
7. Add the intensity channel as a joint term with a residual correlation parameter on TE material.
   It buys 26-47% on WGA/TE, 2% on bulk, and it is the only channel that separates loss from UPD.
8. Adopt the MoChA sample gate: exclude arrays with median SD(BAF) at het sites > 0.11.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LIMITS OF THIS ANALYSIS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- No f-titrated ground truth exists in GSE148488. The aberrant classes available are constitutive
  single copy (male chrX, haploid pronuclei) and real whole-chromosome losses/trisomies, so the
  f=1 endpoint and the noise kernels are MEASURED while every f-dependent power number is the
  corrected shift imposed on real per-marker noise. The curvature of 1/(2-f) is not empirically
  validated here.
- Your chr16 arrays are not in hand. Those verdicts are consistency checks against matched TE
  material, not measurements of your samples.
- Band-redistribution and power tables assume the mosaic region has the same noise kernel as the
  euploid region. A real mosaic region has less template and hence a wider kernel, so those numbers
  are optimistic.
- The blastomere clean set is ~52% of prior-diploid chromosomes and 42/108 progeny arrays were
  excluded, so blastomere figures come from the cleaner tail of that material and if anything
  understate its noise. ESC single WGA rests on 4 arrays from 2 lines, the 2PN zygote on 1 array -
  indicative only.
- Leave-one-array-out calibration holds 1% FPR on average (0.012-0.045) but individual arrays reach
  0.125-0.136, i.e. 12-14x nominal. Thresholds must be per-array adaptive or the FPR is an average
  over arrays, not a guarantee for one.
- Two inter-track disagreements left standing rather than reconciled. Effective markers per
  blastomere chromosome: ~17 from the intensity autocorrelation route vs 247 from BAF segment-mean
  scatter - different channels, and consistent with BAF being much less spatially structured than
  log2R, but confirm which applies before using either. Within-array log2R SD at W=10000: 0.186 here
  vs 0.069 in log2r_noise_decomposition.csv, likely because fixed consecutive-marker windows retain
  between-chromosome offsets that the earlier artifact removed; the drift-inclusive value is the
  right one for a single-array no-reference call.
- I disagree with mosaic_titration.csv on power (it reports 0.875 at f=0.20 whole-chromosome and
  0.583 at seg1200; I measure 0.06-0.88 whole-chromosome depending on stage and 0.02-0.06 at 12 Mb).
  The difference is the null: its is the clean euploid set, mine is the real drifting-diploid set
  with the threshold calibrated on it, and it pools stages where I stratify. Its own footnote
  (perchrom_z_fails_on_A8_genomewide_deviation = True) is the same failure measured directly.
- Four primary full texts were not accessible and are recorded from abstracts only: Zamani Esteki
  2015, Conlin 2010, GENType 2022 Hum Reprod, APCAD part 1 2021. The Conlin gap matters most - it is
  the empirical BAF-to-mosaic-percentage table that WGS-haplarithmisis uses, its material is
  constitutional DNA with no WGA, and transferring that calibration to MDA-amplified 5-cell biopsies
  is an unvalidated step. Get it through the library before quoting any haplarithmisis-derived level.
  PGDIS 2019/2021 position statements were not retrieved; do not quote their bands from here.
- 31 literature records were checked for retractions and corrections: none retracted, two
  corrections recorded. No source was found applying a phase-based mosaic caller to embryo material,
  or reporting a mosaic detection floor for BAF on WGA few-cell material of any kind. That absence is
  why the measurement tracks above are load-bearing rather than confirmatory.
- All analysis autosomal (803,906 of 825,656 markers), GRCh37 as deposited. probe_class.npy is
  per-array, not a static annotation, and was not used as a marker mask. vendor_cn.npy is unusable
  as a cleanliness gate on amplified material (median frac CN=2 is 0.97 bulk ESC but 0.10-0.22 TE
  and blastomere).
