Pre-registration: nominating gain candidates on intensity, and the specificity it must not cost
================================================================================================

Written 9 November 2026 against 5.48.0 plus the asymmetric gain gate, BEFORE the scan is changed.
Baseline: audit/joy-dieter-baseline-2026-11-08.txt, re-confirmed today at gains 11 of 26, losses
44 of 67, 1 false segment across the 72 arrays with no hand call.

WHAT THE MEASUREMENT ESTABLISHED
--------------------------------
audit/gain-gate-result-2026-11-09.txt: candidate windows are nominated on the no-call indicator,
which an extra copy barely moves. Inside the 11 gains the scan finds, the no-call rate is 0.738 to
0.988 against an array null near 0.40. Inside the 8 it never nominates it is 0.395 to 0.578,
barely over baseline. The intensity gate rejects nothing; nomination is the whole loss.

Probed today at three cuts of this array's own autosomal log R distribution, with the scanner,
null machinery and marker floor unchanged and only the per-marker flag replaced:

  cut      gains nominated   of the 8 blind    losses nominated
  q=0.70        25 of 26          7 of 8          0 of 67
  q=0.80        24 of 26          6 of 8          0 of 67
  q=0.90        24 of 26          6 of 8          0 of 67

q=0.70 is chosen on that table. Zero losses nominated at any cut is structural rather than lucky:
the flag counts markers whose intensity is HIGH, which a lost copy cannot produce.

The 8th blind gain carries an intensity deviation of 0.372, under the 0.585 gain gate, so it has
no signal for this channel to find and is not claimed. 7 of 8 is the ceiling from nomination.

THE CHANGE
----------
A separate scan, nominated on intensity, emitting gains only. scanCopyNumber is not touched, so
the loss arm is identical by construction and not by expectation. A marker counts when its log R
exceeds the q=0.70 quantile of that sample's own autosomal distribution, which keeps the null
self-calibrating per array and introduces no absolute intensity threshold, since a per-chromosome
cohort offset is lab-specific and does not transfer. The null is the median high rate over the
sample's OTHER autosomes, the same externalNull the no-call arm uses. A nominated window must then
still clear the intensity gate at 0.585, which is not circular: the flag fires on a heavy upper
tail, the gate asks that the MEDIAN moved. Windows reciprocally overlapping a segment the no-call
arm already raised are dropped rather than reported twice.

PREDICTIONS, FIXED NOW
----------------------
P1  gain recall reaches AT LEAST 15 of 26. Nomination reaches 7 more and all 7 carry deviations of
    0.660 to 1.158, over the gate, but nomination is not a match: each window must also reach 0.5
    reciprocal overlap with his boundaries, and boundary disagreement already costs 7 nominated
    gains today. 15 is the bar; 18 is the ceiling if every one lands.
    FAILS below 15 of 26.

P2  losses are EXACTLY 44 of 67 and every per-region row is unchanged.
    FAILS on any difference. scanCopyNumber is untouched, so a difference is a wiring bug.

P3  the false arm stays at 2 segments or fewer across the 72 arrays with no hand call.
    THIS IS THE REAL RISK AND IT IS NOT RHETORICAL. The clean-window null reaches 0.53 at 10 Mb
    against a gate of 0.585, over roughly 18,500 null windows per scan, and this change is
    precisely the removal of the requirement that kept noisy regions out. SHIPS at 2 or fewer.
    DOES NOT SHIP at 3 or more, in which case the cut moves to q=0.80 or q=0.90, which cost one
    blind gain each, and if neither holds the channel needs the allele evidence rather than a
    tuned quantile.

P4  the gains this recovers are the biology and not more damage. The newly found gains read a
    homozygous-pole fraction ABOVE 0.60, the undamaged side of the 8 November split, where the 11
    already found sit at a median of 0.340.
    This does not gate the release. It is the test of whether the recall moved toward real third
    copies, which is the entire point of the exercise.

WHAT IS STILL NOT THIS, AND IS NOT CLAIMED
------------------------------------------
Boundary overlap costs 7 gains and 20 losses, more than nomination costs in total. It is a scoring
question, to be reported as a rescoring in its own row and never folded into recall.

No allele evidence is used. These cells have no parent arrayed, so the gain call rests on intensity
alone and names no parent. Which parent supplied an extra copy remains unavailable here.
