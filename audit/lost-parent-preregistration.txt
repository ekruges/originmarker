Pre-registration: naming a lost parent against the cell's own answer keys
==========================================================================

Written 10 November 2026 against 5.49.0 plus the refusal records (commit 6ad8469), BEFORE the rule is
implemented and before any cell's error rate has been computed. Scored on audit/refusal-census.ts over
the 31 GSE148488 cells with a published Table S3 karyotype and both parents.

THE DEFECT
----------
A lost parent is named only when the heterozygous fraction at opposite-homozygote markers is under
HAPLOID_MAX = 0.20 and the surviving parent's allele is at or over SURVIVOR_MIN = 0.70. Drop-in puts
heterozygous calls on a chromosome that has only one copy, so on a cell with drop-in of 0.2 to 0.3 a
genuine loss reads 0.21 to 0.40 and is left uncalled. The census: 14 published monosomies undecided;
on the 8 with counts, the lost parent's allele reads 0.005 to 0.095 and the survivor's 0.57 to 0.78.
Three of the 8 are on cells that pass the stage gate. Separately, the stage gate clears every allele
claim on a cell over the heterozygosity ceiling, although drop-in does not move which parent's allele
is present.

THE RULE, FIXED NOW
-------------------
Two answer keys per cell, both measured on that cell, both requiring two measured bulk parents:

  floor    the wrong-homozygote rate at markers where both parents are the SAME homozygote: the sample
           can only be that homozygote there, so the opposite homozygote is an error, and it is the rate
           at which an allele absent from the template appears as a homozygous call.
  present  the median, over the cell's own biparental autosomes, of the smaller of the two one-parent
           shares: what a chromosome carrying both copies reads on this cell.

Per autosome, x = the smaller one-parent count and y = the larger, of n informative markers. With E =
ln 1000, the same order as SEGMENT_LRT:

  lost      x/n is at or under the floor, or the data do not reject the floor at E; AND they reject
            the present level at E in the downward direction; AND y/n is above the present level at E.
            The lost parent is the one whose allele is the minority.
  partial   the present level is rejected downward at E, the floor is rejected upward at E. Reported as
            `mixed` with its counts and NO parent named: one parent's copy is missing from part of the
            sample, or the sample is a mixture of states.
  otherwise nothing is named, as now.

No fraction threshold appears. HAPLOID_MAX, SURVIVOR_MIN and MIXED_SHARE stop deciding lostParent and
mixed. They still define the contribution verdict, which this does not change.

THE GATE. On a stage refusal, lostParent and mixed are no longer cleared when the information ratio
against a measured parent is under NO_INFORMATION_MIN, which is 5.48.0's measured test for calls that
carry genotype information. Where it is at or over, or cannot be computed, they are cleared and
recorded as now. Intensity-derived claims (aneuploidy, segments) are untouched: intensity on a
disordered genome is a separate question with its own measurements.

AMENDMENT, 10 NOVEMBER, BEFORE ANY REAL CELL WAS SCORED
--------------------------------------------------------
The rule as written compared the RARER parent's share against what a two-copy chromosome's rarer
share reads. On error-free material two-copy chromosomes carry no one-parent calls, so that level is
zero, equal to the floor, and the rule could never fire. The synthetic fixture in
scoreSample.check.ts, which has no dropout, showed it: every loss it builds went unnamed.

Amended: whether anything happened is decided on the DOMINANT parent's share, against the cell's
two-copy chromosomes' dominant share (the median, over its biparental autosomes, of the larger
one-parent share), rejected upward at E. Then lost if the rarer share is at the floor (not rejected
upward at E), partial if it is not. This also keeps an extra copy out of `partial`, since a third
copy barely moves the dominant side.

No census row had been produced under the original rule. P1 to P7 stand unchanged.

RESULT OF THE AMENDED RULE, AND ITS WITHDRAWAL
----------------------------------------------
Scored on audit/refusal-census.ts, 31 published cells:

  P1  2 of 4 named with the published parent (GSM4472422 chr5, GSM4552389 chr5). GSM4472405 chr16 not
      reached: that cell's zygosity reads unknown, which gates the allele channel before this rule.
      GSM4552364 chr16 came out partial.
  P2  3 of 4 partial; GSM4552420 chr15 named maternal, as published.
  P3  HOLDS. 0 wrong parents, against 1 at baseline.
  P4  FAILS. 20 confirmed lost parents against 78 at baseline. 58 correct calls became partial.
  P6  2 named on chromosomes published normal, both GSM4552387 (chr15, chr21), a biopsy of the embryo
      whose sisters the consult found read chr13 and chr15 abnormal by two independent channels.

Two errors in the rule, both visible in the census:

1. The floor is measured where both parents are the same homozygote, which is a TWO-copy template.
   A lost chromosome is a ONE-copy template, on which the absent allele is called far more often,
   because one dropout is enough where two were needed. On clean single cells the floor reads 0.0003
   to 0.002 and genuine monosomies read well above it, so they were called partial.
2. The two-copy level was used as a single binomial rate. Chromosomes vary between themselves far more
   than binomial sampling allows, so with a thousand markers a two-copy chromosome only modestly above
   the cell's median rejects it at 1000 to 1. That produced partial calls on chromosomes published
   normal, six of them on one cell published normal throughout.

The rule is withdrawn. It was committed and never released.

REPLACEMENT, DESIGNED AFTER SEEING THIS RESULT
----------------------------------------------
The decision returns to the validated one, HAPLOID_MAX with SURVIVOR_MIN and MIXED_SHARE, with one
change: its inputs are corrected for the drop-in measured on the cell itself. A heterozygous call at a
marker where the cell can only be homozygous is drop-in, so the share of heterozygous calls that
reflects a second copy is (fraction - d) / (1 - d), and each one-parent share is divided by (1 - d).
This is the over-refusal consult's measured recommendation ("heterozygosity must be read as
heterozygosity minus drop-in"), which predates this result. On clean cells d is 0.0003 to 0.02 and
the correction is negligible, so the 78 confirmed calls should stand; on drop-in cells it removes what
drop-in added.

Because it was chosen after seeing this census, its score on the same census is NOT independent
validation. It is reported as such, and it needs confirmation on material it was not designed on
before the claim is made in public.
