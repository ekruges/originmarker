PARENT OF ORIGIN ON REAL LABORATORY ARRAYS: FIRST RUNS
======================================================
The one-parent caller had only ever been exercised on a constructed CEPH trio, where the answer
was known because the losses were constructed from the family's own alleles. These are its first
runs against the laboratory material, through the shipped modules via one_parent_cohort.ts.


EXPERIMENT 14801, MATERNAL REFERENCE
------------------------------------
38 arrays. Reference 14801-05, the one cumulus sample in the whole set, named by the laboratory
rather than chosen by the harness.

  FOUR ARRAYS EXCLUDED AS NOT-A-GENOME, and they are the four PLAN.txt names:

    14801-20   call 57.9%   het 56.2%
    14801-23   call 45.2%   het 52.7%
    14801-27   call 58.0%   het 55.5%
    14801-38   call 59.0%   het 54.7%

  THE EXCLUSION IS THE CEILING, NOT THE CALL-RATE FLOOR, and this is why the ceiling was added.
  Every one of those call rates is above the 40% quality floor, so before it existed all four were
  classified as BULK GENOMIC DNA and handed a dropout of 0.008, the most confident parameter set
  in the module. The agreement with an independently written list of four is the check that the
  ceiling is measuring the right thing.

  SIX ARRAYS ARE BULK-LIKE by call rate and heterozygosity:

    14801-02   call 0.961   het 0.160   ->  bulk
    14801-05   call 0.951   het 0.145   ->  single-cell
    14801-11   call 0.934   het 0.153   ->  trophectoderm
    14801-15   call 0.914   het 0.154   ->  trophectoderm
    14801-35   call 0.904   het 0.137   ->  single-cell
    14801-10   call 0.904   het 0.155   ->  trophectoderm

  THE CUMULUS SAMPLE STAGES AS SINGLE-CELL, WHICH IT IS NOT. 14801-05 is maternal somatic DNA and
  reads 0.951 called, within a thousandth of the bulk sperm gDNA on this platform at 0.954. Its
  heterozygosity is 0.145 against 0.168, and the shortfall estimator reads that gap as dropout.
  It is not dropout: it is a different individual. This is the confound the stage audit quantified
  at +0.155 for an East Asian sample against this European-derived anchor, appearing on a real
  sample, and it is why the reference here is named rather than inferred.

  ONE CHILD CONFIRMED, of 37 candidates. 14801-07, at opposite-homozygote 0.0154 with a second
  parental contribution at 9.1% of 597,714 informative markers. It carries no copy-number regions,
  so it produces no origin call.

  FIVE ARRAYS SIT IN THE PROVISIONAL BAND: 14801-01 at 8.2%, 14801-06 at 7.8%, 14801-12 at 7.2%,
  and others between the 6.5% and 8.5% one-parent boundaries, which is neither haploid nor
  confidently biparental. Those boundaries are wide on purpose, because one parent carries less
  information than two, and the module refuses rather than guessing.

  THE REST ARE NOT RELATED TO THIS REFERENCE at all: 26 arrays at opposite-homozygote 0.05 to 0.11,
  where unrelated adults on this platform sit near 0.07.

  RESULT: ZERO ORIGIN CALLS, and the reason is structural rather than a failure of the method.
  The one array confirmed as this mother's child has no events to assign, and the arrays that do
  have events cannot be confirmed as her children from one parent at this dropout. PLAN.txt
  predicted exactly this for the 2PN-thawed group: one egg donor and one sperm per group, so
  nothing distinguishes a shared paternal haplotype from a shared maternal one, and the group
  needs a real parental array on the OTHER side.

  WHAT WOULD CHANGE IT. A paternal array for this group. The maternal side is anchored and the
  paternal side is not, and the events sit on arrays that cannot be tied to either.


GSE148488: 122 ARRAYS AGAINST A GENOTYPED FATHER
------------------------------------------------
The published series behind the laboratory's own Cas9 work. One sperm donor in four replicates,
four egg donors, and 123 embryo and stem-cell samples. Reference GSM4472397, the sperm donor's
bulk gDNA, which loads through the shipped ingest at call 0.9547 and heterozygosity 0.1626 and
stages as bulk. That is within a thousandth of the call rate PLAN.txt recorded for it.

  40 ARRAYS EXCLUDED as not-a-genome by the ceiling added in 4.8.4, out of 122. Every one has a
  call rate ABOVE the 40% quality floor, between 43% and 59%, with heterozygosity from 26% to 64%.
  Before that ceiling existed all forty were classified as BULK GENOMIC DNA and handed a dropout
  of 0.008. This is a third of the series, not an edge case.

  36 CHILDREN CONFIRMED, at opposite-homozygote 0.0030 to 0.0192 with a second parental
  contribution of 9.5% to 14.9% over 590,000 to 630,000 informative markers. The linkage is
  unambiguous: unrelated adults on this platform sit near 0.07.

  THREE EVENTS ACROSS THIRTY-SIX CHILDREN.

    GSM4552365  TE biopsy    chr16:47,677,040-59,753,701   copy loss   both present
    GSM4552366  TE biopsy    chr16:47,566,785-59,747,742   copy loss   both present
    GSM4774681  blastomere   chr1 whole chromosome         loss        REFUSED

  THE TWO chr16 CALLS ARE THE SAME EVENT SEEN TWICE, in two biopsies of one embryo, and they
  agree: 216 exclusive markers in both, 11.7% of 1,853 and 11.3% of 1,909. Two independent
  amplifications landing on the same count is the internal check that the statistic is reading
  something real. Both say the father's copy is PRESENT, so the lost copy is the mother's or the
  loss is mosaic within the biopsy, which a trophectoderm biopsy of five to ten cells permits.

  THE chr1 REFUSAL IS THE POINT OF THIS RUN. Before 4.8.5 it returned BOTH COPIES PRESENT AT
  POSTERIOR 1.000. The array carries a call-rate collapse on chromosome 1, 69% no-call, and across
  the 16,120 markers where the father is homozygous it reads:

      heterozygous                                 9,628   59.7%
      homozygous for the allele the father lacks   2,959   18.4%
      homozygous for the father's own allele       3,533   21.9%

  No genome reads that way. Where the loaded parent is homozygous a biparental sample is
  heterozygous only when the other parent transmitted the allele this one lacks, which is the
  panel's allele frequency. The likelihood took the heterozygote count at face value, because a
  heterozygote is near-impossible under either deletion hypothesis, and produced a confident
  verdict from an input that carried no information. It now refuses.


WHAT THESE RUNS ESTABLISH, AND WHAT THEY DO NOT
-----------------------------------------------
ESTABLISHED. The method runs end to end on real material, from three independent directions:
laboratory arrays with a maternal reference, laboratory arrays with a public paternal reference,
and a published series with a genotyped father. Linkage separates children from unrelated adults
and from duplicates cleanly. Three defects were found by running it that inspection had not
found, all of them producing confident wrong answers rather than errors, and all three now
refuse instead.

NOT ESTABLISHED, and not close. No parental asymmetry, in either direction. Three events across
thirty-six confirmed children is not a denominator anything can be said from, and PLAN.txt's
floor stands: nothing below a paternal fraction of 0.60, nothing on one-cell-per-embryo material,
and roughly 20 to 30 oocyte donors before a null means anything. What these runs produce is a
working instrument and a measured event rate, not an answer to the question.

THE BINDING SHORTAGE IS EVENTS, NOT METHOD. 122 arrays yielded three. The material with the
events is the laboratory cohort, where 1,189 regions are already called from the dosage channel,
and what that cohort lacks is a parental anchor for the donors carrying them. One anchor per donor
group is enough, and the EYS donor is an anchor for exactly one group in the local corpus: five
arrays of experiment 32477, four of which are uniparental paternal and therefore answer the
parent question trivially.


BOTH PARENTAL SIDES, GSE148488
------------------------------
  reference          children   events
  sperm donor              36        3
  egg donor B               9        4
  egg donor C              23        0
  egg donor A               5        0
  egg donor D          not usable: call 0.819, heterozygosity 0.345, above the diploid ceiling

  SEVEN EVENTS. FOUR both-copies-present, THREE refused, ZERO naming a parent.

THE REFERENCE GATE CANNOT BE THE STAGE LABEL, and two real parental arrays proved it. Egg donor A
is bulk gDNA at a 0.9837 call rate, the highest in the series, and the heterozygosity ladder calls
it single-cell because it reads 0.1414 against a 0.168 anchor. The cumulus sample in experiment
14801 fails the same way at 0.951. What the obligate-het statistic needs from a reference is
complete, accurate genotypes, which is dropout, which call rate measures directly. Heterozygosity
against a population anchor measures the individual as much as the material. The gate is call rate
now, with a heterozygosity band only wide enough to exclude what is not a diploid genome at all.

THE DETECTOR AND THE CALLER ARE IN TENSION ON WHOLE CHROMOSOMES, and this is the finding worth
carrying forward. Split the seven events by kind:

  segmental copy loss        4 of 4 called      all both-copies-present
  whole-chromosome loss      3 of 3 REFUSED     all for unmeasurable genotypes

That is not a coincidence of three. A whole-chromosome loss is DETECTED by the collapse of its
genotype call rate, and the origin call is MADE from genotypes. The signal that identifies the
event is the destruction of the evidence needed to assign it. On chr1 of GSM4774681 the collapse
is 69% no-call and the surviving calls read 59.7% heterozygous where the father is homozygous.

  WHAT FOLLOWS. Assigning a whole-chromosome loss to a parent probably cannot be done from
  genotype calls at all, and needs allele DOSAGE from the intensity channel, which survives a
  call-rate collapse because intensity is measured whether or not a genotype is emitted. The
  shipped two-parent path already reads dosage. The one-parent path does not, and that is the
  gap. Segmental losses are unaffected: their call rates do not collapse, and all four were
  scored normally.

  n IS 3 AND 4. This is a hypothesis with a mechanism, not a measured law.
