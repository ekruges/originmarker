PARENT OF ORIGIN ON REAL LABORATORY ARRAYS: FIRST RUNS
======================================================
The one-parent caller had only ever been exercised on a constructed CEPH trio, where the answer
was known because the losses were constructed from the family's own alleles. These are its first
runs against the laboratory material, through the shipped modules via one_parent_cohort.ts.


EXPERIMENT 14801, MATERNAL REFERENCE
------------------------------------
38 arrays. Reference 14801-05, the one cumulus sample in the whole set, named by the laboratory
rather than chosen by the harness.

  FOUR ARRAYS EXCLUDED AS NOT-A-GENOME, and they are the four PLAN.txt names:

    14801-20   call 57.9%   het 56.2%
    14801-23   call 45.2%   het 52.7%
    14801-27   call 58.0%   het 55.5%
    14801-38   call 59.0%   het 54.7%

  THE EXCLUSION IS THE CEILING, NOT THE CALL-RATE FLOOR, and this is why the ceiling was added.
  Every one of those call rates is above the 40% quality floor, so before it existed all four were
  classified as BULK GENOMIC DNA and handed a dropout of 0.008, the most confident parameter set
  in the module. The agreement with an independently written list of four is the check that the
  ceiling is measuring the right thing.

  SIX ARRAYS ARE BULK-LIKE by call rate and heterozygosity:

    14801-02   call 0.961   het 0.160   ->  bulk
    14801-05   call 0.951   het 0.145   ->  single-cell
    14801-11   call 0.934   het 0.153   ->  trophectoderm
    14801-15   call 0.914   het 0.154   ->  trophectoderm
    14801-35   call 0.904   het 0.137   ->  single-cell
    14801-10   call 0.904   het 0.155   ->  trophectoderm

  THE CUMULUS SAMPLE STAGES AS SINGLE-CELL, WHICH IT IS NOT. 14801-05 is maternal somatic DNA and
  reads 0.951 called, within a thousandth of the bulk sperm gDNA on this platform at 0.954. Its
  heterozygosity is 0.145 against 0.168, and the shortfall estimator reads that gap as dropout.
  It is not dropout: it is a different individual. This is the confound the stage audit quantified
  at +0.155 for an East Asian sample against this European-derived anchor, appearing on a real
  sample, and it is why the reference here is named rather than inferred.

  ONE CHILD CONFIRMED, of 37 candidates. 14801-07, at opposite-homozygote 0.0154 with a second
  parental contribution at 9.1% of 597,714 informative markers. It carries no copy-number regions,
  so it produces no origin call.

  FIVE ARRAYS SIT IN THE PROVISIONAL BAND: 14801-01 at 8.2%, 14801-06 at 7.8%, 14801-12 at 7.2%,
  and others between the 6.5% and 8.5% one-parent boundaries, which is neither haploid nor
  confidently biparental. Those boundaries are wide on purpose, because one parent carries less
  information than two, and the module refuses rather than guessing.

  THE REST ARE NOT RELATED TO THIS REFERENCE at all: 26 arrays at opposite-homozygote 0.05 to 0.11,
  where unrelated adults on this platform sit near 0.07.

  RESULT: ZERO ORIGIN CALLS, and the reason is structural rather than a failure of the method.
  The one array confirmed as this mother's child has no events to assign, and the arrays that do
  have events cannot be confirmed as her children from one parent at this dropout. PLAN.txt
  predicted exactly this for the 2PN-thawed group: one egg donor and one sperm per group, so
  nothing distinguishes a shared paternal haplotype from a shared maternal one, and the group
  needs a real parental array on the OTHER side.

  WHAT WOULD CHANGE IT. A paternal array for this group. The maternal side is anchored and the
  paternal side is not, and the events sit on arrays that cannot be tied to either.
