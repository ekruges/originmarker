STEP 4, REFORMULATED: RECONSTRUCT THE MISSING PARENT'S OPPOSITE HOMOZYGOTES
Written before the measurement runs. Committed first.

WHY THE FIRST FORMULATION WAS THE WRONG HALF

The het-only rebuild asserts that the missing parent is HETEROZYGOUS where enough diploid siblings
read AB at a marker where the known parent is homozygous. It reached 0.627 precision, no vote rule
improved it, and 5.45.1 pinned a reason prior to precision: the two-parent tally is defined on
OPPOSITE HOMOZYGOTES, one parent AA and the other BB, so a parent asserted only heterozygous
contributes zero informative markers.

That objection names the fix. The siblings prove more than heterozygosity, and the stronger claim is
the one the channel needs.

WHAT THE SIBLINGS ACTUALLY DISTINGUISH

At a marker where the known parent is AA, the missing parent is AA, AB or BB, and each sends a
different share of B to her children:

  her genotype   children truly AB   observed AB at dropout `a`
  BB             all of them         1 - a
  AB             half of them        0.5 (1 - a)
  AA             none                drop-in only

So the VOTE FRACTION separates the three, and near-unanimity is the signature of BB rather than an
artefact. The two-sided window of 5.44.4 discarded the top of the vote range as contamination. It is
where the usable markers are.

At a measured dropout of 0.144 and 30 samples the three vote counts are Binomial(30, 0.856) with
mean 25.7, Binomial(30, 0.428) with mean 12.8, and Binomial(30, 0.0435) with mean 1.3. The first two
are about five standard deviations apart.

WHAT IS MEASURED

Only the known parent and the diploid siblings are used to build the reference. The missing parent's
own array is held out and used solely to score.

  1. PRECISION of the BB assertion, against her real array: of the markers asserted BB, the share
     she genuinely reads BB. Criterion: at or above 0.95. A reference a parental call rests on
     cannot carry the 37 percent error the het-only version did, and 0.95 is the point at which the
     opposite-homozygote statistic's own measured separation, 0.17 to 0.85 percent against 6.5 to
     16.6 percent, still holds with margin.
  2. COMPLETENESS: of her true BB markers at known-parent-homozygous sites, the share recovered,
     and the absolute count. The count is what matters: the opposite-homozygote channel runs on a
     median of 1,034 markers per chromosome, so a reconstruction yielding a comparable count per
     chromosome is usable even at low completeness.
  3. THE THRESHOLD SWEPT, so it is clear whether the choice carries the result.
  4. LEAVE ONE EMBRYO OUT, since drop-in was measured to be a property of the marker and therefore
     correlated across siblings. A per-embryo split says whether one embryo carries the numbers.
  5. THE CHANNEL FED. If precision clears 0.95, the reconstructed opposite homozygotes are passed
     to `addTwoParent` and the resulting per-chromosome calls are scored against the published
     karyotype, next to the same cells scored with her real array. That is the only result that
     says whether step 4 produces a working reference rather than a good-looking table.

FAILURE IS A RESULT. If precision does not clear 0.95 this is recorded as a third negative with its
mechanism, and step 4 closes on the proof already pinned in 5.45.1.


================================================================================
RESULT
================================================================================

CRITERION 1 FAILS, and the measurement says what governs it and what would satisfy it.

ARM 1, VOTING BY ARRAY, at the derived threshold of 22 of 30: precision 0.2038 over 2,291 asserted
markers, completeness 0.0189 of her 24,746 true opposite homozygotes. The wrong ones are mostly AB.

AND PRECISION FALLS AS THE THRESHOLD RISES, which the model cannot produce:

   u  asserted  precision  completeness
  18     6,564     0.3696        0.0980
  22     2,291     0.2038        0.0189
  26       877     0.0775        0.0027
  30       122     0.0656        0.0003

A model whose predictions run backwards has the wrong sample size. These 30 arrays are not 30
children. They are 8, 7, 10, 2, 1 and 2 cells of six embryos, and cells of one embryo carry the same
genome. A heterozygous parent transmits to an EMBRYO and every cell of it then reads the same, so
unanimity across 30 arrays is unanimity across six independent transmissions. THE SAME ERROR IS WHY
THE HET-ONLY REBUILD READ 0.627: its k was derived from n equals 30 when the effective n was six.

ARM 2, VOTING BY EMBRYO, one vote each, and precision now runs the right way:

   u  asserted  precision  completeness
   2   174,375     0.1039        0.7322
   4    60,535     0.2719        0.6652
   6    15,425     0.4985        0.3108

ARM 3, THE PER-EMBRYO LIKELIHOOD, which replaces the hard vote. Two hypotheses per embryo, three per
marker for the missing parent, so a ten-cell embryo and a one-cell embryo are weighted by what they
actually say. Best precision 0.4805 at a posterior of 0.5, 5,005 markers, median 279 per chromosome.
No marker reaches a posterior of 0.8, which is itself the answer: with six embryos the posterior
cannot.

THE CEILING IS ARITHMETIC. At this panel's heterozygosity of 0.1666 the implied minor allele
frequency is 0.0917, so at a marker where the known parent is homozygous the missing parent is AB
with probability 0.1666 and BB with 0.0084, prior odds of 19.8 to 1 against the genotype wanted.
Unanimity across n embryos cuts those odds by 0.5^n. Precision therefore cannot exceed:

  embryos   4       5       6       7       8       9      10      12
  ceiling   0.4468  0.6176  0.7636  0.8660  0.9282  0.9627  0.9810  0.9952

NINE INDEPENDENT EMBRYOS ARE NEEDED FOR 0.95. The public corpus contains exactly one family with a
single unambiguous mother and more than one embryo, and it has six.

THE MEASURED 0.4805 FALLS SHORT OF EVEN THE SIX-EMBRYO CEILING OF 0.7636, by the mechanism already
measured in the het-only round: drop-in is a property of the marker, so a bad probe reads AB in
every cell of every embryo, which is what both the vote and the likelihood assume cannot happen.

SO STEP 4 IS BUILT, IN THE RIGHT FORM, AND ITS CONDITION IS STATED RATHER THAN GUESSED. The
reconstruction asserts the genotype the two-parent tally consumes, its precision is governed by the
count of independent embryos and not of arrays, and it needs nine of them from one couple against
the six that exist. That is a condition a laboratory can meet; it is not met by any material here.


================================================================================
EVERY CONFIGURATION TRIED, AND THE TWO MECHANISMS THAT BOUND THEM
================================================================================

  formulation                                        precision  per chrom  note
  het-only, vote by array                               0.6271          -  5.44.3
  het-only, two-sided window from Mendel                0.6289          -  5.44.4
  opposite-homozygote, vote by array                    0.2038        125  falls as u rises
  opposite-homozygote, vote by embryo, unanimous        0.4985          -
  per-embryo likelihood, genome-wide prior              0.4805        279
  hard vote by embryo, per-marker prior                 0.6611        381
  per-embryo likelihood, per-marker prior               0.7107         58  best

The pre-registered criterion was 0.95. The best configuration reaches 0.7107, and at the posterior
where it does so it yields 58 markers per chromosome against the 200 the channel refuses below. It
fails both criteria at once.

MECHANISM ONE, SIX EMBRYOS. The arrays are cells, not children: 8, 7, 10, 2, 1 and 2 cells of six
embryos. A parent transmits to an embryo, so the information is six independent transmissions and
the likelihood ratio for the wanted homozygote against the heterozygote at an all-heterozygous
reading is 2^6, which is 64. Against genome-wide prior odds of 19.8 to 1 that gives 0.7636, and
nine embryos are needed for 0.95. The corpus holds exactly one family with a single unambiguous
mother and more than one embryo, and it has six. THIS IS WHY THE HET-ONLY REBUILD READ 0.627: its
threshold was derived from a sample size of 30.

MECHANISM TWO, CORRELATED DROP-IN WITHIN AN EMBRYO, which bounds it below even that. Conditioning on
the marker's own allele frequency, estimated from ten unrelated donor arrays with the family's two
held out, improves precision exactly as the prior argument says it should, 0.6017 at a minimum
frequency of 0.10 to 0.6611 at 0.45. But it stops far short of the 0.9716 the prior predicts. The
breakdown of the wrong calls says why: at a minimum frequency of 0.40, 32.9 percent of the asserted
markers are ones where she reads HETEROZYGOUS and only 2.1 percent where she carries none of the
allele. Prior odds of 1.87 with a ratio of 64 should leave 2.9 percent. The realized ratio is 3.7.

An embryo that inherited nothing looks as though it did when drop-in reads heterozygous across its
cells, and drop-in is a property of the marker, so it does. The hard vote makes this worst for a
large embryo: at a drop-in of 0.0435, "any cell reads AB" has a ten-cell embryo voting yes with
probability 0.36 having inherited nothing. Replacing the vote with a per-embryo likelihood recovers
part of it, 0.7107, and no further, because the likelihood's own assumption that cells of one embryo
are independent given its genotype is the assumption the correlation violates.

SO THE PRE-REGISTERED CRITERION IS NOT REACHABLE ON THIS MATERIAL, and the two numbers a laboratory
would need are now stated rather than guessed:

  nine or more independent embryos from one couple, not nine or more arrays
  and a per-probe drop-in estimate, since the residual error is correlated and not independent

Below that, the one-array naming shipped in 5.45.0 is the better route and it works: 53 of 54 named
rows correct against the published karyotype.
