Pre-registration: which parent gave an extra chromosome, with one parent
=========================================================================

Written 14 November 2026 against 5.53.0, BEFORE the code below is built or run.

WHY. With one parent, which is the laboratory's whole configuration, the tool sees an extra copy by
intensity and cannot say whose it is. The two-parent reading (gainOrigin.scanAlleleGains) reads the
father's share of alleles at markers where the parents are opposite homozygotes, against the cell's
own biparental chromosomes. The old one-parent route selected markers by the cell's own
heterozygous calls, which forces the share to one half; it is unwired and stays so.

THE METHOD. The one-parent copy state (oneParentState.ts) reads, at markers where the loaded parent P
is homozygous, the share r of the allele P lacks, without selecting on the cell's calls. On a disomic
chromosome r sits at the cell's disomic level r_d; with an extra copy of P's chromosome the other
parent's alleles are a third, not a half, of the copies, so r = 2/3 r_d; with an extra copy of the
other parent's, r = 4/3 r_d. So P's share s = 1 - r / (2 r_d) is 1/2, 2/3 or 1/3: the units of the
two-parent share. Each autosome's s, with r_d the median r over the cell's disomic chromosomes, is
read by the same scanAlleleGains, against the same cell's disomic chromosomes, with its intensity,
under the same conditions (a genome known to carry both parents; copy number readable). Whole
chromosomes only; a segment's share needs the marker-level reading and is not attempted.

TESTS
  CONSTRUCTED. Five GSE148488 cells Table S3 publishes with a normal karyotype; on six of their
  chromosomes published normal (2, 5, 9, 13, 18, 21), one at a time, an extra copy is added by
  re-weighting the cell's own allele intensities: one copy's worth of intensity added to the father's
  allele where he is homozygous (an extra paternal copy) or the mother's where she is (an extra
  maternal copy), split evenly elsewhere. Scored against the father rebuilt from his eight pronuclei
  alone, and against both measured parents for the two-parent reading. 60 constructions.
  P1  Of the constructed gains the one-parent reading names a parent for, the wrong parent on at
      most 2%.
  P2  It names a parent on at least half of the 60.
  P3  Where both readings name a parent, they name the same one on every constructed gain.
  CENSUS, the 31 Table S3 cells:
  P4  One-parent gain claims on chromosomes published normal: no more than the two-parent arm's 3.
  P5  Where the one-parent and two-parent arms both name a gain's parent, the same parent.
  P6  Nothing else moves: stage, zygosity, copy number and lostParent identical to 5.53.0 in every
      configuration.

A failed prediction is reported as a failure.

PASS 1, SCORED 14 NOVEMBER (the method above, 60 constructions)
  P1  wrong parent on at most 2% of those named: 0 of 9. HOLDS.
  P2  a parent named on at least half: 9 of 60. FAILS.
  P3  the same parent as the two-parent reading wherever both name one: 9 of 9. HOLDS.
  Why P2 fails: the two-parent test detects a gain from the alleles alone, so it needs |z| of 6; the
  one-parent share is noisier, and its z reached 6 on 9. Its direction was right on 55 of the 57
  constructions it scored, and the two it got wrong read |z| 0.40 and 0.51. On the 29 unmodified
  chromosomes of the same cells |z| reached 3.01. Intensity called 38 of the 60 gains.

PASS 2, WRITTEN BEFORE IT IS BUILT OR RUN
  The change: with one parent, intensity detects the gain and the alleles only orient it. Where
  intensity has called a whole-chromosome gain and the one-parent share is at least ONE_PARENT_ORIENT_Z
  = 3 of the cell's own spread from its disomic chromosomes, the parent is named by the direction.
  3 is the conventional three-sigma bar; it was set after seeing pass 1, so it is scored on
  constructions it has not seen.
  HELD OUT: the same five cells, the 16 other autosomes where Table S3 publishes normal, an extra
  paternal and an extra maternal copy on each, scored as before.
  Q1  Of the held-out gains intensity calls, a parent named on at least half.
  Q2  Wrong parent on at most 2% of those named.
  Q3  The same parent as the two-parent reading wherever both name one.
  Q4  No parent named on any chromosome of an unmodified cell, nor on any chromosome but the one
      constructed.
  And on the 31 Table S3 cells (the census), P4 to P6 above.
