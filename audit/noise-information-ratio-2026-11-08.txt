Information ratio R on the Axiom corpus: the run, and two predictions that failed
=================================================================================

Run 8 November 2026 against 5.47.0 / 47e48d4, harness audit/noise-information-ratio.ts.
Per-array and per-autosome tables carry barcodes and stay in audit/local/.

CORPUS. 112 Axiom arrays, every one on this machine's reach at 825,656 markers: the 21 hand-call
arrays, the 72 with no hand call, and 19 from the e5 series. The consult response names a
459-array census; that census is not reconstructible here, and the count is stated as 112 rather
than borrowed. The other array families present are different platforms and the consult's own
caveat forbids applying the cut to them until measured.

REFERENCES, AND THE ONE WAY THIS RUN DEPARTS FROM THE CONSULT. R is measured against three
reference arrays, one per barcode prefix, each the highest-call-rate array of its prefix, and a
sample is only ever scored against prefixes that are not its own. The consult measured against
unrelated BULK arrays. No bulk array exists in this corpus, so every reference here is itself a
single cell carrying its own call errors, which inflates R for every sample. The run therefore
reports R against the worst reference and against the best, and the difference is the size of
that effect.

THE RESULT. Against the best stranger:

  informative       R < 0.30          73 arrays
  unresolved        0.30 to 0.60      14 arrays
  no information    R >= 0.60         25 arrays

The gap the consult found reproduces. Sorted, R jumps 0.381 to 0.591 to 0.701 to 0.966, and the
25 arrays above it run 0.966 to 1.135, which is the value a sample with no information about any
genome takes by construction.

PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTIONS, SCORED.

  P1  every array at R >= 0.60 is already fully withheld by 5.47.0.
      HOLDS. All 25 read heterozygosity 0.566 to 0.673, over the 0.25 diploid ceiling, so the
      shipped path already refuses them in full. 7 of the 25 are also under the call-rate floor.

  P2  arrays between 0.30 and 0.60 are at most 2% of the run and all under 0.50 call rate.
      FAILS, on both halves. 14 of 112 is 12.5%, and their call rates run 0.441 to 0.947.
      The cause is the reference substitution above: single-cell references raise R for every
      sample, which pushes genuinely informative arrays up into the unresolved band. It does not
      touch the 0.60 cut, because the gap below it is 0.21 wide.
      CONSEQUENCE: the 0.60 branch is shippable on this corpus and the 0.30 band is not. R below
      0.30 must not be reported as a positive statement of quality until bulk references exist.

  P3  hetBafSd of the R >= 0.60 arrays lies below the median of informative amplified arrays.
      HOLDS, and more sharply than predicted. Informative amplified arrays read 0.173 to 0.345,
      median 0.245. The 25 failed arrays read 0.158 to 0.168. The two ranges do not overlap, and
      the failures are the LOWER group. Any upper threshold on hetBafSd refuses good single cells
      and passes every failed array, which is the consult's finding reproduced here.

  P4  per platform, HumanCytoSNP-12. NOT APPLICABLE: no such array in this run.

WHAT THE BRANCH BUYS. 31 arrays sit over the heterozygosity ceiling, and 5.47.0 refuses all 31
with a reason that cannot say whether the array failed, the sample is a mixture, or the material
was contaminated. R separates them: 25 carry no genotype information, and 6 still do, at R 0.222,
0.256, 0.372, 0.378, 0.381 and 0.591. Those 6 are arrays the current reason describes no better
than the 25.

A mixture reads LOW on this statistic by construction, because a homozygous call in a mixture
needs every contributor to carry that allele. So the new reason can say what the failure is not,
which is the part the heterozygosity ceiling's own caveat says it cannot do.

WHAT MUST NOT MOVE. The branch changes reason text only. It never touches the predicate that
decides whether a call is made, so no chromosome moves between refused and called. A check pins
that, as 5.47.0 did for the offered-count split.
