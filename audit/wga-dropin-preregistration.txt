DROP-IN ON AMPLIFIED SINGLE CELLS: THE MEASUREMENT THE DIPLOID CEILING IS MISSING

Written and committed before the data is read. One measurement, one decision, and a hard stop
after it: this closes the ceiling question or leaves it closed as it stands.

THE QUESTION

`MAX_DIPLOID_HET = 0.25` refuses an array reading above the heterozygosity a single diploid genome
can reach. It is derived in stage.ts as this panel's measured diploid rate of 0.168 plus drop-in at
the highest rate measured there, 0.0525 over 113 same-genome pairs, giving 0.212 and rounded up.

Drop-in is a heterozygous call where the true genotype is homozygous. The 0.0525 figure was
measured on BULK material. The ceiling is applied unchanged to amplified single cells, and drop-in
on whole-genome-amplified material is a different process: chimeric amplicons and template
switching create alleles that bulk hybridisation does not.

Five cells of GSE148488 that the ceiling refuses read heterozygosity 0.318 to 0.376 at call rates
0.67 to 0.77, and the authors' own karyotype reads two of them "normal for other chromosomes" and
one "46,XX". If amplified single cells of a KNOWN diploid genome read in that same band, the
ceiling is refusing normal genomes and three real hand-called deletions are being lost to it.

THE MATERIAL, AND WHY IT ANSWERS THIS AND NOTHING ELSE

GSE60909, Zamani Esteki et al., "Concurrent whole-genome haplotyping and copy number profiling of
single cells". Illumina HumanCytoSNP-12, GPL13829, 164 samples. Genotype calls for every sample
are in the series matrix itself, keyed by GEO accession.

  20 single cells from Epstein-Barr virus transformed lymphoblastoid lines of two siblings,
     S1 and S2, amplified by MDA (10) or PicoPlex (10), several hybridised twice.
   4 multi-cell control DNA arrays from the same two siblings, `wga: -`, unamplified.

A lymphoblastoid line from a healthy donor is diploid. So every one of those 20 single cells is an
amplified array of a genome known to be diploid without anything having read the array to decide
it, which is the property this corpus has never had. The multi-cell controls give the same
genome unamplified, so drop-in can be measured against the truth rather than modelled.

  THE CAVEAT, STATED BEFORE THE RESULT. The multi-cell control is blood and the single cells are
  a transformed line of the same person. A line can acquire changes in culture, so a locus-level
  disagreement is not automatically drop-in. A genome-wide rate is not sensitive to a handful of
  culture-acquired events, and the hybridisation replicate pairs isolate hybridisation noise from
  amplification noise within one amplified sample. Neither of those makes the line identical to
  the blood and the write-up will say so.

THE MEASUREMENTS

  per single cell, against its own sibling's multi-cell control, over autosomes:
    call rate
    heterozygosity, the quantity the ceiling tests
    DROP-IN     the share of calls reading AB where the control reads AA or BB
    DROP-OUT    the share of calls reading homozygous where the control reads AB
  reported per amplification chemistry and per hybridisation protocol, never pooled into one
  number, because the chemistry is the thing that differs.

THE PREDICTIONS

  R1. The 20 amplified single cells read heterozygosity ABOVE 0.25 on a genome known to be
      diploid. That is the whole question: if they do, the ceiling refuses normal amplified single
      cells.
  R2. Drop-in on amplified material exceeds 0.0525, the bulk figure the ceiling is built on, by
      enough to account for the difference. Claude Science's arithmetic puts the requirement at
      0.22 to 0.30 to reach heterozygosity of 0.32 to 0.38 at these call rates; that figure is
      quoted and not assumed.
  R3. MDA and PicoPlex differ. They are different chemistries and a single ceiling for both would
      be the same mistake as a single ceiling for bulk and amplified.

  FALSIFIED IF: the single cells read at or below 0.25. Then the ceiling is right for amplified
  material, the five refused cells are not ordinary diploids, the three hand-called deletions on
  them stay missed, and the ceiling question is closed against raising it. That outcome is as
  useful as the other and is why this is worth one run.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT, FIXED NOW SO THE RESULT CANNOT CHOOSE IT

  R1 and R2 hold      the ceiling is re-derived from the measured amplified drop-in, per stage,
                      and the five cells are re-scored. No new free parameter: the ceiling stays
                      `diploid rate + (1 - diploid rate) x drop-in` with a drop-in figure measured
                      on the right material instead of the wrong one.
  R1 fails            the ceiling stands unchanged and this file records that it was tested
                      against known-diploid amplified single cells and survived.

  Either way the gate work stops here. A platform difference is a confound in both directions and
  is stated rather than corrected: CytoSNP-12 reads 0.301 to 0.316 heterozygous in bulk against
  Axiom's 0.141 to 0.170, so a rate measured there does not transfer to an Axiom ceiling as a
  number. What transfers is the RATIO of amplified drop-in to bulk drop-in, measured within one
  platform, which is why both are measured here on the same arrays.

THE RESULT, WRITTEN AFTER THE DATA WAS READ

Nothing above this line was changed. Run by audit/wga_dropin.py over 279,998 autosomal probes.

R1 FAILED. R2 FAILED. R3 HELD, and the direction of the whole effect is the opposite of what the
ceiling's logic is built to worry about.

THE UNAMPLIFIED CONTROLS, which are the platform baseline

  GSM1493591  S1 conventional   251,385 called   het 0.314
  GSM1493590  S1 24h            242,196 called   het 0.318
  GSM1493603  S2 conventional   250,956 called   het 0.314
  GSM1493602  S2 24h            243,090 called   het 0.318

CytoSNP-12 bulk diploid DNA reads 0.314 to 0.318. stage.ts already records this as the reason the
ceiling must be scaled by the panel in use, and these four arrays are a direct measurement of it:
an Axiom ceiling of 0.25 applied to CytoSNP material would refuse four undamaged bulk genomes.

THE AMPLIFIED SINGLE CELLS, on a genome known diploid, against their own unamplified control

  chemistry   n    heterozygosity   drop-in         drop-out
  MDA        10    0.048 to 0.121   0.001 to 0.011  0.296 to 0.852
  PicoPlex   10    0.147 to 0.264   0.033 to 0.249  0.108 to 0.740

  single cells reading above 0.25:     2 of 20, at 0.264 and 0.253
  amplified drop-in, median:           0.033
  the bulk figure the ceiling uses:    0.0525
  ratio of median amplified to bulk:   0.62x

WHAT THIS SETTLES

Amplification does not raise heterozygosity. It lowers it. Every one of the 20 single cells reads
BELOW its own unamplified control of the same genome: MDA by a factor of three to six, PicoPlex by
a factor of 1.2 to 2. Drop-out runs 0.30 to 0.85 and drop-in runs 0.001 to 0.249, and the median
amplified drop-in is lower than the bulk figure the ceiling was built on, not higher.

So the hypothesis fails in the direction that matters. The five refused cells of GSE148488 read
0.318 to 0.376 on a panel whose bulk diploid rate is 0.168, which is 1.9 to 2.2 times the
platform baseline. An amplified single cell of a diploid genome cannot reach twice its platform's
bulk rate, because amplification moves the rate the other way. The ceiling stands, unchanged, and
it has now been tested against twenty amplified arrays of a genome known to be diploid without
anything having read an array to decide it.

The three hand-called del(6q)(pat) events on those cells therefore stay missed, and the reason is
on the record rather than open: they are on arrays the ceiling refuses, and this measurement says
the ceiling is refusing them correctly.

WHAT THAT IMPLIES ABOUT THOSE FIVE CELLS, STATED AND NOT ACTED ON

The ceiling and the hand karyotype disagree about them. The karyotype reads two "normal for other
chromosomes" and one "46,XX"; the ceiling reads not one genome. This measurement does not say
which is right, but it removes the explanation that favoured the karyotype. What remains on the
ceiling's side of the list is two individuals mixed at an uneven ratio, or a contaminated
reaction, and a by-eye SNP-array karyotype reads relative copy number across chromosomes, which a
low-level mixture does not disturb. That is consistent with the earlier finding that those cells
are BAF-smeared with roughly 46 percent of heterozygous mass outside all three expected bands.

Not pursued. The gate work stops here as this file fixed in advance.

PLATFORM CONFOUND, AS STATED BEFORE THE RUN

What was measured within one platform is the ratio of amplified drop-in to bulk drop-in, and it is
0.62x. That is the quantity that transfers. The rates themselves do not: CytoSNP bulk reads 0.314
and Axiom bulk 0.168, a factor of 1.87, and both figures are measured rather than assumed.

ONE CAVEAT NOT RESOLVED. The controls are blood and the single cells are a transformed line of
the same person, so a locus-level disagreement is not automatically drop-in. It does not touch the
conclusion: the finding is that amplified heterozygosity is uniformly LOWER than its own control,
and culture-acquired changes in a line cannot produce that on every one of twenty arrays.
