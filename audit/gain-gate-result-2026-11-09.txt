The gain gate: a correct fix that buys nothing, and the measurement that says why
=================================================================================

Run 9 November 2026 against 5.48.0 plus the asymmetric gain gate. Scored against
audit/gain-gate-preregistration.txt, written before the change. Per-region tables carry
laboratory barcodes and stay in audit/local/. Barcodes pseudonymised per audit/local/barcode-map.tsv.

THE PREDICTIONS, SCORED
-----------------------
  P1  the four gains at 0.815, 0.954, 0.994 and 0.998 are recovered; gains reach 15 of 26.
      FAILS. 0 of 4 recovered. Gains are 11 of 26, identical to the baseline.

  P2  losses are exactly 44 of 67, unchanged.
      HOLDS. 44 of 67, and every per-region row matches the baseline.

  P3  the false arm stays at 2 segments or fewer across the 72 arrays with no hand call.
      HOLDS. 1 segment on 1 array, chr2 20.6 to 24.6 Mb, a 4.0 Mb copy-gain. The same single
      call as the baseline, so specificity is untouched as well as recall.

  P4  at least 3 of the 4 recovered gains read a pole fraction above 0.60.
      NOT APPLICABLE. Nothing was recovered.

WHY P1 FAILED, AND WHY THE PREREGISTRATION WAS SCORING THE WRONG STAGE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
All four of those regions report a best reciprocal overlap of 0.00 after the change: the detector
raised no segment anywhere near them, so no gate setting could have mattered.

The cause attribution the baseline supplied is not a diagnosis. joy-dieter.ts classifies a miss as
"under SEGMENT_LRR_SHIFT" when the deviation DIETER reports is below the constant. His number is
measured over his own boundaries and says nothing about whether the scan nominated a window there.
Four regions were filed against a gate that had never been offered them. The preregistration
inherited that error, which is the honest reason P1 was wrong rather than unlucky.

audit/joy-gain-miss-cause.ts measures the two stages apart. For every hand region it reports the
best score any overlapping window can reach from the no-call channel with the threshold removed,
beside the intensity deviation the detector itself measures inside.

WHERE THE 93 REGIONS ARE ACTUALLY LOST
--------------------------------------
                                    gains (26)   losses (67)
  never nominated, no-call < 250          8            3
  nominated, lost on boundary overlap     7           20
  rejected by the intensity gate          0            0
  found at 0.5 reciprocal overlap        11           44

The intensity gate rejects nothing in either direction. The asymmetric gate is still a defect
worth removing, because a symmetric test asks 1.71x as much of a gain as of a loss and the
constructed gain controls cleared it only on a donor block reading +1.60 to +1.95, but on this
corpus it is a no-op and is reported as one.

THE DETECTOR IS A DAMAGE DETECTOR, NOT A GAIN DETECTOR
------------------------------------------------------
                       no-call inside   the array's own null   intensity measured inside
  the 11 gains found     0.738 to 0.988    0.355 to 0.425          +1.570 to +2.971
  the 8 never nominated  0.395 to 0.578    0.326 to 0.447          +0.372 to +1.158

Inside a found gain the array has stopped genotyping almost everything. Inside a gain it never
sees, the no-call rate is barely over that array's own baseline, which is what a real third copy
produces: an extra copy does not stop an array calling. Candidate windows are nominated on the
no-call indicator, so the only regions ever offered to the gate are regions the array failed on.
This is the same conclusion audit/joy-gain-recall-splits-by-pole-2026-11-08.txt reached from the
homozygous poles, now located at the stage responsible for it.

WHAT THIS MAKES AVAILABLE
-------------------------
7 of the 8 never-nominated gains carry an intensity deviation between 0.660 and 1.158, all above
the 0.585 gain gate. The signal is present and measured; nothing reads it, because nothing
nominates the window. Nominating gain candidates on intensity rather than no-call is therefore the
change with the recall in it, and the existing scanner already takes a binary per-marker indicator
against a rate null, so it needs a different indicator rather than new machinery.

The specificity risk is specific and must be measured, not argued: the clean-window null reaches
0.53 at 10 Mb against a gate of 0.585, over roughly 18,500 null windows per scan. The 72 arrays
with no hand call are what that gets measured on, at the same bar.

WHAT IS STILL NOT THIS
----------------------
Boundary overlap loses 7 gains and 20 losses, more than nomination loses in total. That is a
scoring question and is to be reported as a rescoring in its own row, never folded into recall.
