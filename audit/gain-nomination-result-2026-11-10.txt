Gains: intensity nomination works, and the 11 of 26 was never the product's number
==================================================================================

Run 9 and 10 November 2026. Scored against audit/gain-nomination-preregistration.txt, written
before the code. Per-array tables carry barcodes and stay in audit/local/. Barcodes pseudonymised
per audit/local/barcode-map.tsv.

THE PREDICTIONS, SCORED
-----------------------
  P1  gain recall reaches at least 15 of 26.
      HOLDS as measured, 16 of 26, and the measurement is void. See below: the harness was
      scanning arrays the shipped tool refuses, so 15 of those 16 are calls the product never
      makes. Counted on the product's own behaviour the number is 1 of 26.

  P2  losses are exactly 44 of 67.
      HOLDS on the same harness, and is void for the same reason. Product-faithful: 32 of 67.

  P3  at most 2 false segments over the 72 arrays with no hand call.
      FAILS on that arm, 15 segments, 14 of them on one array. On PUBLISHED truth it passes: see
      the public arm below.

  P4  the recovered gains are undamaged material.
      HOLDS, 4 of 4, at homozygous-pole fractions of 0.715 to 0.840 where the 11 gains the old
      scan found sat at a median of 0.340. This is the one prediction whose meaning survives
      intact: when the intensity channel does find a gain, it is a third copy and not damage.

THE FINDING THAT REFRAMES ALL OF IT
-----------------------------------
scoreSample withholds the copy-number scan entirely when inferStage returns 'failed', because the
scan normalises against the sample's own per-chromosome median and a genome where most chromosomes
are affected has no normal majority to be that median. The refusal is correct and it is load
bearing.

joy-common's scanSegments called the scan unconditionally. So every recall and false-call figure
this project has recorded against these hand calls was measured on a code path the product does not
take on this material.

  8 of the 21 cells carrying hand calls stage as 'failed'.
  23 of the 26 hand-called GAINS are on those 8 cells.
  23 of the 67 hand-called LOSSES are on those 8 cells.

So the scoreboard, counted on what the product actually does:

                        old harness    product-faithful    on readable material
  gain                  11 of 26        1 of 26             1 of 3
  loss                  44 of 67       32 of 67            32 of 44

The gains question cannot be answered on this corpus. It is not a threshold, it is not the
nomination channel, and it is not boundary agreement. 23 of 26 hand-called gains sit on cells whose
genomes are too distorted for this method's normalisation, and the tool already declines them by
name. The disagreement with the hand calls is about what is readable, which is the same question
5.46.0 and 5.48.0 were built to answer.

WHAT THE INTENSITY ARM IS WORTH ANYWAY
--------------------------------------
Nominating on no-call cannot find a third copy: inside the gains the old scan found, the array had
stopped calling 74% to 99% of markers against an array baseline near 40%, which is amplification
damage; inside the gains it never nominated, 40% to 58%, barely over baseline, which is what an
extra copy does. Of 26 gains the no-call channel loses 8 to nomination and 0 to the intensity gate.

Nominating on intensity, at the 0.70 quantile of the sample's own autosomal log R, reaches 25 of 26
gains and 0 of 67 losses, the latter by construction since a lost copy cannot read intensity-high.
On readable material it takes gains from 0 of 3 to 1 of 3. That is a small number because readable
material holds only 3 of these gains, not because the channel is weak.

THE PUBLIC ARM, WHICH IS WHERE SPECIFICITY IS NOW MEASURED
----------------------------------------------------------
Zuccaro 2020 Table S3 publishes a per-chromosome karyotype for 31 arrayed cells; 477 autosomes read
`normal`. A segmental call on one of those disagrees with a published, reviewed number rather than
with a local table whose provenance is unsettled.

                                 on normal chromosomes   of those, on cells
                                                         published chaotic   on clean cells
  gain nomination off                   30                      28                 2
  gain nomination on                    34                      31                 3

Intensity nomination adds ONE disagreement on a cell not published chaotic, against a
pre-registered bar of one. It passes, at the bar. The two pre-existing ones are losses on a cell
published 45,XY,-6.

A SEPARATE DEFECT THIS SURFACED, NOT CAUSED BY THIS CHANGE
----------------------------------------------------------
28 of the 30 baseline disagreements are on two cells Zuccaro hand-called chaotic aneuploid. The
tool's own 'failed' stage does not fire on them, so it scans and calls where the published reading
says the genome is chaotic. Whatever the right answer is, the tool and the published hand reading
disagree about whether those two cells are readable, and the tool is the permissive one. Untouched
here and recorded for its own measurement.

AND TWO DEFECTS FIXED ALONG THE WAY
-----------------------------------
A segmental call could claim less than a megabase. MIN_SEGMENT_MARKERS assumes even probe spacing
and this platform clusters: at mean density the marker floor spans about 8.7 Mb, and inside a
cluster the same count fits in a tenth of one. Five such calls appeared on the quiet arm, three at
the SAME chr19 locus on three unrelated arrays. The floor now applies to the span a call REPORTS,
which is the refined interval and not the window searched; gating the window left all three in
place. Smallest hand-called region is 3.0 Mb, so 1 Mb costs no recall.

The gain gate was symmetric. A hemizygous loss is log2(1/2) and a third copy is log2(3/2), so one
gate asked 1.71x as much of a gain as of a loss and sat above the event it existed to admit. The
constructed gain controls cleared it only because the spliced donor block read +1.60 to +1.95.
Fixed, and on this corpus it rejects nothing either way: the gate was never what lost the gains.

ADDENDUM, 10 NOVEMBER: THE HETEROZYGOSITY CEILING OVER-REFUSES, AND ITS OBVIOUS REPLACEMENT FAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All 8 refusals fire on one branch, h > MAX_DIPLOID_HET; none fires on the call-rate floor. Scored
against 5.48.0's information ratio, which is independent of heterozygosity, they split:

  no information, R 1.010 to 1.122      5 cells    12 of the 26 gains
  calls informative, R 0.323 to 0.429   3 cells    11 of the 26 gains

The second group is an over-refusal. One of those three reads R 0.323 while two cells that PASS
read 0.305 and 0.330, so the information content does not separate them and heterozygosity alone is
doing the refusing. The branch's own caveat states that a mixture, a contaminated reaction and a
failed array cannot be told apart; 5.48.0 tells a failed array from a mixture, so that caveat is
out of date.

THE REPLACEMENT TESTED AND REJECTED. The scan's actual requirement is a normal majority, so the
statistic measured was the share of autosomes whose median log R lies within a band of the genome
median, with the majority threshold at 0.5 because that is what majority means. Against Zuccaro
Table S3 at a band of 0.15 it refuses 12 of 12 cells published chaotic and admits 18 of 19 of the
rest, the single error being a cell published 45,XY,-6 at exactly 0.500.

On the hand-call corpus it fails in both directions. It would ADMIT the four cells reading R 1.082
to 1.122, whose calls carry no information and whose intensity happens to be uniform, and REFUSE
eight cells that scan today including one at R 0.220 and heterozygosity 0.136. Intensity
concordance and genotype information are orthogonal: amplification swings per-chromosome intensity
without copy number, and a dead array can read flat.

Conjoining the two tests is defensible and recovers 1 of the 11, not 11: of the three
informative-but-refused cells, one has a normal majority at 1.000 and two have none at 0.000.

NOTHING SHIPPED. The finding is measured and stands. The replacement does not, and a rule that
changes 12 of 21 verdicts on this corpus is not one to adopt against a deadline. What it needs is
a band that scales with each array's own per-chromosome spread, which is circular on a chaotic
genome, and that is the open question rather than a tuning exercise.
