PAIR LIKELIHOOD: THE SPLIT AND THE OPERATING POINT, FIXED BEFORE THE RESULTS

This file is written and committed before the held-out half is scored. Everything in it is a
decision about how to measure, made without knowing what the measurement will say. Anything
changed after the numbers arrive is recorded as a change, with the reason, below the original.

WHY A SPLIT IS NEEDED

callPair weighs the allele share against the intensity over six states and returns a posterior.
On 300 constructed builds across 16 cells it recovers mosaic gains the shipped thresholds cannot
see at all, 52 of 60 at a third of the cells against 0 of 60, and it calls 21 of 120 partial
losses a gain, an error the thresholds hold at zero. Both numbers come from the same builds that
motivated the model, so neither is evidence about an unseen cell. Until a threshold is set on data
the model has not been tuned on, the pair is reported beside each row and decides nothing.

THE SPLIT

By cell, never by chromosome. Chromosomes in one cell share that cell's amplification, its marker
dropout and its intensity baseline spread, so a chromosome-level split leaks the thing that drives
the errors. Cells are sorted by GSM accession and assigned by position: even to tuning, odd to
held-out. No randomisation, no reshuffling, no reassignment after seeing a result.

The real hand-called events are not constructed builds and are not in either half. They are read
once, at the end, after both halves are reported, and they are never used to pick a threshold.

A build enters neither half if the untouched cell already carries an event on that chromosome, if
the hand karyotype lists one there, or if the arm cannot be scored at all. That rule is already in
the harness and is not changed here.

THE TWO STATEMENTS, SCORED SEPARATELY

The tilt is which parent ends up over-represented. The direction is whether that came from an
extra copy or a missing one. An extra copy from one parent and a missing copy from the other are
the same tilt and move the allele share the same way, so the share alone fixes the tilt and only
the intensity separates the direction. The intensity carries a per-chromosome baseline that one
cell cannot measure. The two are therefore not one call and are not scored as one.

  TILT      correct when the named over-represented parent matches the build
  DIRECTION correct when gain versus loss matches the build

A row counts as named when callPair returns a copy-number state. `disomy` and `unexplained` are
refusals and count as not named, never as errors.

THE RULES FOR PICKING THE TWO THRESHOLDS

Both are chosen on the tuning half alone, by these rules, and then applied unchanged to the
held-out half.

  T_tilt   the lowest tilt posterior at which no tilt is wrong on the tuning half.
           If no such value exists, the lowest at which at most 1 percent are wrong.
  T_dir    the lowest state posterior at which at most 5 percent of named rows have the wrong
           direction on the tuning half. Zero is not required because it is not reachable: a
           wrong direction can carry a posterior of 1.00 when the arm's own baseline intensity
           is offset, and that offset is not visible within one cell.

Ties go to the higher threshold, which is the more conservative of two equal choices.

THE PRIMARY ENDPOINT

Tilt detection on held-out cells at a third of the cells: the share of builds at f = 0.3 whose
tilt is both named at T_tilt and correct. The shipped thresholds detect nothing at that fraction,
so this is the number that says whether the likelihood adds anything on cells it was not tuned on.

SECONDARY, ALL ON HELD-OUT CELLS

  tilt detection and tilt error at f = 0.5 and f = 1.0
  direction detection and direction error at T_dir, by fraction and by gain against loss
  the full curve of both, so the operating point can be read against what was available
  whether the tuning and held-out halves differ in their cells' own share and intensity spreads,
    which would mean the split is not comparing like with like

WHAT WOULD MAKE THE PAIR DECIDE A ROW RATHER THAN BE REPORTED BESIDE IT

Stated now so it is not decided by whatever the numbers turn out to be. The pair may take over
the parent on a row only if, on held-out cells, the tilt at T_tilt is wrong on no more than 1
percent of named rows and is named on more builds than the shipped thresholds detect. The
direction may be reported as a call only if its held-out error is under 5 percent. If either
fails, that statement stays reported and not deciding, and this file records that it failed.


================================================================================================
THE RESULT, WRITTEN AFTER THE HELD-OUT HALF WAS SCORED

Nothing above this line was changed. 840 constructed builds over 19 cells, six chromosomes across
the size range, every cell in the trio set the hand karyotype covers and whose arrays are present.
10 cells to tuning, 9 held out, assigned by accession as fixed above. Median cell share spread
0.0242 on the tuning half against 0.0200 on the held-out half, so the halves are comparable.

THRESHOLDS PICKED ON THE TUNING HALF, BY THE RULES ABOVE

  T_tilt = 0.75   the lowest value at which no tilt was wrong on the tuning half
  T_dir  = 0.995  the lowest value at which at most 5 percent of named rows had the wrong
                  direction on the tuning half

APPLIED UNCHANGED TO THE HELD-OUT HALF

  tilt        named 287 of 450   right 283 of 287 (98.6%)   wrong 4 (1.4%)
  direction   named 121 of 450   right 120 of 121 (99.2%)   wrong 1 (0.8%)

PRIMARY ENDPOINT, held-out tilt by built fraction, against what the shipped thresholds detect

                 tilt named     of those right    shipped detects
  f = 0.3 gain   37 of 90          35 of 37        0 of 90
  f = 0.3 loss   40 of 90          39 of 40        0 of 90
  f = 0.5 gain   60 of 90          59 of 60        2 of 90
  f = 0.5 loss   65 of 90          65 of 65        0 of 90
  f = 1.0 gain   85 of 90          85 of 85       55 of 90

At a third of the cells the shipped thresholds detect nothing at all and the likelihood names the
over-represented parent on 77 of 180 builds, right on 74 of those 77. That is the number this
split was run to produce.

AGAINST THE TWO BARS FIXED BEFORE THE SPLIT

  the tilt may decide the parent      NOT MET. 1.4 percent wrong against a 1 percent bar.
  the direction may be read as a call MET.     0.8 percent wrong against a 5 percent bar.

So the direction becomes a call at a posterior of 0.995 and the parent is not taken from the model.
PAIR_DIRECTION_P in web/src/gainOrigin.ts is that threshold and gainPair.check.ts pins it, because
the error rate quoted beside every row that clears it was measured at that value and nowhere else.

The tilt failed by 0.4 of a percentage point, on 4 wrong rows out of 287. It is not promoted. The
bar was fixed in advance precisely so that a near miss could not be argued into a pass, and moving
it now would make every number above unfalsifiable. The tilt stays reported beside each row.

WHAT THE SPLIT ITSELF SHOWED, which is the reason for doing it this way

The tuning half had zero wrong tilts at 0.75 and the held-out half had four. The threshold the
tuning half's own rule chose was therefore optimistic, and reading the tuning half alone would
have reported a perfect tilt. At 0.995, the threshold the direction rule chose, the held-out tilt
is wrong on 0 of 121. That value was available but was not what the pre-registered tilt rule
selected, and it is recorded here rather than adopted.
