ARM B SCORED AS A CALLER, AND THE HET-ONLY REBUILD BUILT AND VALIDATED

Committed before either is run. The previous round measured raw rates and called that a test of
arm (b); it was not. A caller is scored on its verdicts. And the rebuild was deferred on an
argument about the laboratory's material when it is buildable and testable on public data where the
answer is known. Both are done here.

PART 1. ARM B AS A CALLER, NOT A RATE

The question the laboratory asks on a chaotic cell is whether a second parental contribution is
present on a chromosome. That is two hypotheses, and both marker classes can carry it:

  parent HOMOZYGOUS     two contributions  het = q(1 - ado) + d,  q = 0.1217
  parent HETEROZYGOUS   two contributions  het = 0.5(1 - ado) + d
  either class          one contribution   het = d

The same decision rule is applied to both classes so the only difference is the marker set: a
log-likelihood ratio per chromosome under those two predictions, called at the shipped posterior of
0.95, refused below it. Scored against the authors' published karyotype over the 31 cells with both
parents arrayed, with each parent taken as the loaded one in turn.

  X1. The parent-HETEROZYGOUS class scores higher accuracy on the two-contribution class than the
      parent-HOMOZYGOUS class does, because its prediction is 4.1 times larger and does not depend
      on the drop-in parameter.
  X2. Neither class reaches the 0.854 the shipped three-way caller achieves about the loaded
      parent's own copy, because that figure rests on the exclusive-marker channel which neither of
      these two hypotheses uses.
  X3. The refusal rate is reported for both. A class that scores well by refusing most chromosomes
      has not answered the question, and this is where that shows.

  FALSIFIED IF X1 fails: the marker class is then not the answer to the one-parent asymmetry and
  the matter is closed against it.

PART 2. THE HET-ONLY REBUILD, BUILT AND VALIDATED ON PUBLIC DATA

An external review's formulation: assert only that the missing parent is HETEROZYGOUS, never that
she is homozygous. At a father-homozygous marker an embryo reading heterozygous proves she carries
the other allele, because dropout removes alleles and cannot invent one. An embryo reading
homozygous proves nothing and is discarded, so an embryo that lost her copy writes nothing in
either direction and the rebuild cannot drift toward the father.

The validation family is public and carries truth. GSE148488's egg donor D has 30 samples sharing
one sperm donor and is herself arrayed as GSM4472408; egg donor B has 32 samples and four arrays of
her own. So the mother can be rebuilt from her children using only the father, and scored against
her actual genotypes.

THE THRESHOLD IS DERIVED, NOT CHOSEN. k is the smallest integer whose contamination meets the
figure the shipped Experiment-5 rebuilt mother carries on its own banner, 0.02749, under

  contamination = (1-H) P(X_d >= k) / [ (1-H) P(X_d >= k) + H P(X_s >= k) ]
  completeness  = P(X_s >= k),   X_d ~ Bin(n, 0.0435),   X_s ~ Bin(n, 0.5(1-ado)),   H = 0.168

with drop-in at the measured 0.0435 and dropout inferred per sample. Both numbers are reported, and
k must scale with n: a fixed k degrades as embryos are added, because drop-in accumulates with n
exactly as signal does.

  Y1. PRECISION. Of markers the rebuild asserts the mother is heterozygous at, the share she is
      actually heterozygous at in her own array is at or above 1 - contamination for the derived k.
  Y2. COMPLETENESS, reported beside it, because precision alone is gamed by raising k until the
      marker set is empty. Expected to be the modelled P(X_s >= k).
  Y3. LEAVE-ONE-OUT AT THE EMBRYO. A rebuild containing a sample scores that sample at inflated
      agreement, which this project measured at 0.00 percent against 1.06. Siblings share events,
      so all cells of one embryo are held out together.
  Y4. THE DIRECTION THE CIRCULARITY WOULD SHOW. If the one-sided rule works, the rebuild's
      precision does NOT fall as more aneuploid samples enter it, where a majority-vote rebuild
      would. Measured by rebuilding from the cleanest half and from all of them and comparing.

  FALSIFIED IF Y1 fails: the one-sided formulation does not deliver its predicted precision and the
  review's answer to the circularity is wrong in practice whatever its logic.

WHAT NEITHER PART SETTLES

Whether the rebuild helps the laboratory. Its own material is three donors with no group reaching
the floor of five products, measured in the previous round. These parts establish whether the
method works, which is a separate question from whether there is anything to point it at.

THE RESULT, WRITTEN AFTER THE RUNS

Nothing above this line was changed.

PART 1. X1 HOLDS, AND IT OVERTURNS THE PREVIOUS ROUND'S CONCLUSION.

Same two-hypothesis rule on both marker classes, posterior 0.95, scored per chromosome against the
published karyotype with each parent loaded in turn. 1,021 chromosome-observations per class.

  class                  truth    right  wrong  refused   accuracy
  parent-HOM (shipped)   one         50     52        1     0.4902
  parent-HOM (shipped)   two        874     43        1     0.9531
  parent-HOM (shipped)   BOTH       924     95        2     0.9068   answered 99.8%
  parent-HET (excluded)  one         80     23        0     0.7767
  parent-HET (excluded)  two        907     11        0     0.9880
  parent-HET (excluded)  BOTH       987     34        0     0.9667   answered 100.0%

The excluded class is better on BOTH truth classes. On the weak arm, which is the one-parent
asymmetry this was about, accuracy goes from 0.4902 to 0.7767. On the chromosomes the hand reads as
normal it goes from 0.9531 to 0.9880, so the false-call rate falls from 43 in 918 to 11 in 918.

Against the objective fixed for this directory, detections minus twice the calls on hand-normal
chromosomes at k = 2:

  parent-HOM  924 - 2(43) = 838
  parent-HET  987 - 2(11) = 965

and the excluded class answers more chromosomes, not fewer. So it clears the two-term bar on both
arms, which is the first statistic in this directory to do so.

THE PREVIOUS ROUND CONCLUDED THE OPPOSITE AND WAS WRONG. It measured the raw rate distributions,
found they overlap, and closed the question. A likelihood ratio over the same overlapping
distributions does far better, because it uses the marker count as well as the rate. Measuring a
separation is not the same as scoring a caller, and only the second answers the question.

X2 HOLDS. Neither class reaches the 0.854 the shipped three-way caller achieves about the loaded
parent's own copy, because that figure rests on the exclusive-marker channel which neither of these
hypotheses uses. X3 HOLDS: refusals are 2 and 0, so nothing is bought by declining to answer.

NOT SHIPPED HERE. Using this class means replacing a two-boundary rule with a likelihood ratio on a
shipped verdict, and the measured boundaries overlap, which is why the ratio wins. That is a change
to a shipped call and takes its own pre-registration rather than riding on this one.

PART 2. Y1 FAILS, Y3 AND Y4 HOLD, AND THE MECHANISM IS MEASURED.

Built and run on the public family where the mother is herself arrayed: 30 samples over 6 embryos,
one father, mean dropout 0.144, derived k = 6.

  modelled contamination 0.00803, so predicted precision   0.9920
  MEASURED precision                                       0.6271   over 103,188 asserted markers
  modelled completeness                                    0.9977
  MEASURED completeness                                    0.3839   of 168,540 true het markers

Y1 fails by 36 percentage points. Y3 holds: leaving each embryo out in turn gives 0.6024 to 0.6344,
mean 0.6184, which is the all-in figure, so the hold-out is working and nothing is inflated by
sibling leakage. Y4 holds: precision does NOT fall as samples enter, running 0.5454 at n = 4 to
0.6271 at n = 30. The one-sided rule does avoid the majority-vote collapse, which was its purpose.

WHY Y1 FAILS, measured rather than argued. The vote distribution at father-homozygous markers where
the mother is truly HOMOZYGOUS, against what independent drop-in at 0.0435 predicts:

  votes       observed   expected       ratio
      5          7,967      2,618     3.0e+0
      6          5,384        496     1.1e+1
      8          3,084         10     3.0e+2
     10          2,645          0     2.5e+4
     12          2,836    6.4e-04     4.4e+6
     14          2,654    2.2e-06     1.2e+9

  six or more votes: 38,479 observed against 585 expected, a ratio of 66

The observed count does not die away. It flattens at roughly 2,650 markers from ten votes through
fourteen, where independence predicts a fall of nine orders of magnitude. So drop-in is a property
of the MARKER and not of the embryo: a bad probe collects a vote from every sample, and no vote
threshold can suppress what every sample agrees on.

The external review's own caveat predicted exactly this and named the mechanism: the independence
assumption is what the measured agreement excess of 1.24 to 1.42 violates in the haploid case, and
whether the diploid analogue does so was unknown and measurable. It does, and by far more.

A share of the 37 percent may be the REFERENCE being wrong rather than the rebuild: a marker where
fourteen of thirty children read heterozygous and the mother reads homozygous is more likely her
call being wrong than fourteen independent drop-ins. At roughly 2,650 markers per vote bin in the
flat tail that accounts for part of it and not for the mid-range, where votes of six to nine
contribute 14,862 against 584 expected.

SO THE REBUILD IS BUILT, VALIDATED, AND DOES NOT WORK WELL ENOUGH. 0.627 precision is not a
reference a parental call can rest on. The formulation is right, the circularity is genuinely
closed by it, and the limit is the material rather than the logic.
