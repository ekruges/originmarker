Withhold arrays, rebuild, compare with the measured parent
==========================================================

Run 11 November 2026 on tesla. Scored against audit/withholding-preregistration.txt, written before
any part was run. Founder panel: the shipped founders-gse186441.tsv.gz. Laboratory cells are
pseudonymised (S23 = the series whose sperm donor's bulk array is S23-00).

THREE FAULTS IN 5.52.0, FOUND BY THIS RUN AND FIXED BEFORE THE FINAL SCORE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Copy number withheld from every haploid product. Found by the smoke run of the part C scorer on
   S23-07, before any part was scored (see the preregistration's addendum). The normal-majority test
   counted disomic chromosomes only. Now the commonest copy state's share (normalFraction).
2. The mask stopped at window boundaries. Found in part C's first pass (S23-02: parental-absence
   segments on chr1 and chr11 against a rebuild from its five siblings, none against S23-00). The
   stretch ran 20 to 65 Mb on chr1; windows 25 to 60 Mb were masked and the ends were not. Now two
   grids offset half a window, and each masked run widened to the second marker where the products
   disagree (uniformMask).
3. A chaotic diploid admitted as a product. Found in part A's first pass (A3): GSM4472405 admitted in
   78 of 163 rebuilds. First fixed by requiring one copy on 80% of stated chromosomes; part A's second
   pass showed that refusing real products from small groups (A1 83 of 792, A2 66 of 512; on S23 two
   of 36), since rebuild error from four siblings put real products as low as 0.52 one-copy, under the
   chaotic cell's highest 0.58. Replaced by: no chromosome may read at or over PARENT_LOST_LOW 0.675,
   the 5% quantile of where a chromosome missing the parent's copy reads on Table S3 (productVerdict).
   Chosen after that pass, on a threshold measured before it. Over the same cases a withheld
   product's highest chromosome reads at most 0.626 (527 cases) and GSM4472405's at least 0.702 (102).
The first pass of every part ran 5.52.0 with fault 1 fixed; the second, faults 1 to 3 with the share
rule; part A's third pass, the shipped rule.

drop-in cells gave 4 findings and none on a chromosome published normal; the 24 others gave 8 and one.
