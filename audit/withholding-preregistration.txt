Pre-registration: withhold arrays, rebuild automatically, compare with the measured parent
=========================================================================================

Written 11 November 2026 against 5.52.0, BEFORE audit/withholding.ts is run. Founder panel: the
shipped web/public/panel/founders-gse186441.tsv.gz. Thresholds are the shipped ones throughout.

MATERIAL WITH A MEASURED PARENT TO SCORE AGAINST
  GSE148488   8 paternal pronuclei of one sperm donor, his bulk array GSM4472397 (truth, never
              given to a rebuild), 7 maternal pronuclei, 2 egg donors, and the 31 cells with a
              published Table S3 karyotype (his diploid children, and two published failures)
  S23         18 laboratory cells and the sperm donor's bulk array S23-00 (truth, never given to a
              rebuild). Paternal by the sample sheet AND by the consult's test against S23-00:
              S23-02, -04, -05, -09, -11, -13. Maternal by both: S23-01, -03, -06, -07, -08, -10,
              -14, -18. Unsettled: S23-12, -15 (sheet: no usable DNA), S23-16, -17 (sheet: P).

A. PRODUCTS WITHHELD. Every subset S of the paternal products with |S| >= 4 (GSE148488: 163,
   S23: 22) is the group; every other array is judged against it (judgeProducts).
   A1  Every member of every subset reads 'product', judged against the others.
   A2  Every withheld paternal product reads 'product' against every subset.
   A3  No maternal pronucleus, egg donor, sheet-maternal S23 cell, or Table S3 cell reads
       'product' against any subset. (A Table S3 cell that has lost most maternal chromosomes would
       be the exception the test is allowed to find; any such case is reported by name.)
   A4  For every subset of >= 5, the masked rebuild's measured contamination (homozygous calls
       where the truth array is heterozygous) is below the unmasked rebuild's.
   A5  Measured contamination is reported against the model figure. Expected: model understates.

B. ONE-PARENT COPY STATE AGAINST WITHHELD REBUILDS (GSE148488). Variants: the 8 masked and
   unmasked; the 8 leave-one-out rebuilds of 7; 6 random subsets of 6 and 6 of 5, seeded; and the
   5.51.0 five (80, 81, 82, 83, 85) masked and unmasked. Each scored per autosome on the 31 cells
   and the 15 pronuclei, against the measured father GSM4472397 scored the same way.
   B1  Every masked variant agrees with the measured father on at least 630 of the informative
       autosomes of the 31 cells.
   B2  No variant gives a wrong parent beyond GSM4472405 chr4 and GSM4552382 chr2.
   B3  Every masked variant reads at most 4 published-normal chromosomes as having lost a copy
       that the measured father reads disomic.
   B4  The copy-number decision (disomic fraction at or over 0.80) is the measured father's on at
       least 30 of 31 cells for every masked variant.
   B5  Every paternal pronucleus, against every variant WITHOUT it, reads 'other-lost' on at least
       95% of its decided autosomes; every maternal pronucleus reads 'parent-lost' on at least 95%.
   B6  Reported: unmasked against masked agreement, variant by variant.

C. EACH S23 CELL WITHHELD, THE REBUILD AUTOMATIC. For each cell k, om reconstruct over the other
   17 S23 cells (the shipped command: stage sort, grouping, judging, mask), then k is scored by
   scoreSample against that rebuild and against S23-00 measured.
   C1  The rebuild from 17 uses exactly the paternal products other than k, for all 18.
   C2  Stage, ploidy and drop-in correction identical on 18 of 18.
   C3  Every copy-number row (whole-chromosome and segmental, made or withheld) identical on 18 of 18.
   C4  Per-autosome copy state identical on at least 97% of the autosomes decided in both.

D. THE GERMLINE PAGE, end to end on its public example products in a browser: products load, the
   pairs group, the build judges every array and reports the mask, and parental origin is called.
   D1  The build completes with every example product admitted and states a masked count.
   D2  Parental origin is called for every example array, and no console error is raised.

A failed prediction is reported as a failure.

ADDENDUM, BEFORE ANY PART WAS SCORED. The smoke run of withholding-score.ts on one cell (S23-07, a
haploid maternal pronucleus, against S23-00) found 5.52.0 withholding copy number from every haploid
product: the normal-majority test counted disomic chromosomes only, and a haploid product has none.
Fixed first (normalFraction: the commonest copy state's share, disomic or one copy of either parent),
so every part below scores the fixed code. B4's "disomic fraction" reads "normal fraction".
