Sex chromosomes with no parent loaded (plan item 3.12)
=====================================================
Written 16 November 2026, before any code was changed. Built on 6.5.0 (7545a95), branch
sex-no-parent. Open arrays only. Per-array tables are untracked, under the main checkout's
audit/local/sex-no-parent/.

THE FAULT, IN THE CODE
----------------------
1. No chromosome X row with no parent. web/src/parentage.ts, classify(): the first row loop
   (line 1343) builds a row only for a chromosome with parental markers in t.byChrom, which is
   empty with no parent. The second loop, "a chromosome with no parental marker", skips every
   chromosome that is not an autosome (line 1464:
   `if (!isAutosome(c) || t.byChrom.has(c)) continue`). So chrX gets no row, although its call
   rate and intensity are tallied (tallyRow, line 557) and its shift from the autosomes is
   computed (line 1287).
2. The false reason. web/src/scoreSample.ts line 1315 looks up the X row, finds none, passes
   xShift NaN to callSexChromosomes, and web/src/sexChromosome.ts line 107 then writes "chromosome
   X carries no intensity on this file". A missing row is not missing intensity.
3. The header sex. web/src/ingest.ts callSex (line 257): chrX heterozygous calls over autosomal
   ones, under 0.15 "male", over 0.55 "female". It is computed when the file is read, before any
   stage, and printed in the file chip (Syngamy.tsx line 1136), the parse log (line 427), the
   Sample quality table (line 1527) with its "sex call" gate row (ingest.ts line 545), and the PDF
   (syngamyPdf.ts line 903). On a haploid cell every heterozygous call is noise, X and autosomes
   alike, so the ratio sits near 1 and reads "female".
4. Also seen: sexChromosome.ts asks only "diploid or not", and scoreSample.ts (line 1316) counts as
   diploid any stage that is not haploid, unknown or failed. With no parent, a cell whose
   heterozygosity comes from drop-in is staged on a diploid rung (stage.ts: drop-in is removed
   only over the 25% ceiling), so chrX at the autosomes' level would read "XX" on a pronucleus
   once fault 1 is fixed. Its reason for non-diploid material ("not expected to carry two of each
   chromosome") is also false of a diploid array the stage refused.

THE PHYSICS
-----------
  diploid XX       chrX at the autosomes' level, heterozygous like them
  diploid XY       chrX one copy under the autosomes, still genotyped; chrY called where the
                   file genotypes it
  haploid, one X   chrX at the autosomes' level: every chromosome is at one copy
  haploid, Y       no chrX; chrY called
So the level of chrX says one X or two only where the cell is shown to carry two copies of its
autosomes. Heterozygosity says so only once drop-in is removed: drop-in adds heterozygous calls to
one genome. stage.ts measures haploid pronuclei at 0.2% to 1.4% heterozygous with drop-in removed,
and HAPLOID_MAX_HET (10.5%) is the line a diploid reaches at any stage.

Looked at before the rule was written, and recorded so it is not hidden: the stage and ploidy of the
6.4.1 no-parent records against the stated sex. Of 257 stated arrays with a record: stated one X,
2 haploid, 2 ploidy unknown, 1 staged diploid (single cell); stated XX, 10 diploid, 2 ploidy
unknown (trophectoderm, drop-in removed); stated XY, 11 diploid, 1 ploidy unknown. 33 stated arrays
(the lab's pronuclei, mostly) have no no-parent record yet. No sex outcome of the new rule was seen.

THE RULE
--------
R1. chrX row. classify builds a chrX row from the call rate and the intensity wherever no row came
    from parental markers, under the floor every row has (200 chrX markers). Like the parental X
    row it carries no copy-number call: aneuploidy stays autosomal. The tally leaves the
    pseudoautosomal region out only where it knows the assembly, and it takes the assembly from the
    parent, so with no parent the region (about 7% of chrX markers on this array) stays in. That
    lifts a one-X level toward zero by a few hundredths against a 0.40 copy floor; not changed here.
R2. Two copies shown. With NO parent loaded, the sex chromosomes are read as a karyotype only on an
    array that shows two copies of its autosomes:
      - not where the stage failed or is unknown, nor where it is haploid;
      - shown where heterozygosity with the stage's drop-in removed, (h - d) / (1 - d) with d the
        founder-panel drop-in the stage was given, is over HAPLOID_MAX_HET;
      - shown where the stage reads ploidy diploid with heterozygosity at or under HAPLOID_MAX_HET,
        because there it was the BAF band, not heterozygosity, that said two copies;
      - not shown otherwise: the heterozygous calls are what drop-in on one genome gives.
    With a parent loaded nothing changes: the stage reads genome count from the parent's alleles
    there, and gate S3 holds that path fixed.
R3. Sex reading against ploidy. Where two copies are shown, callSexChromosomes as before (chrX
    level against the autosomes, with chrY). Haploid: no sex; the reason says one sex chromosome is
    a haploid cell's normal complement and states where chrX sits (at the autosomes' level: one X;
    under them by COPY_SHIFT_FLOOR or more: no X) and whether chrY is read. Stage unresolved or two
    copies not shown: no sex, and the reason says which.
R4. Every "unresolved" reason true of the file. No chrX row: "fewer than 200 chromosome X markers"
    (plus "outside the pseudoautosomal region" where the assembly is known). A row with no level:
    "fewer than 200 chromosome X markers carry intensity". The stage reasons quote the stage and the
    numbers of R2.
R5. Header sex. The heterozygosity sex (profile.sex) is printed for a sample only where R2 shows two
    copies; elsewhere the page says it is not read and why. Before a run there is no stage, so a
    file in the sample slot shows no sex in its chip and its parse log; parents keep theirs (bulk
    adults, and the parent checks read it). With a parent loaded, the page prints what 6.5.0 does.

DRAFT CHANGELOG PARAGRAPH (not in CHANGELOG.md)
-----------------------------------------------
**With no parent loaded, the sex chromosomes are read against the cell's own ploidy, and the page
no longer prints "female" on a haploid cell.** 6.5.0 built no chromosome X row with no parent, so
the reading was unresolved on every array, with a false reason ("chromosome X carries no
intensity"), while the page printed a sex from heterozygous calls on X: "female" on 26 of 28 cells
stated to carry one X, nearly all pronuclei. Now chrX gets a row from its call rate and intensity
(the pseudoautosomal region of either assembly left out), and the sex is read only on an array that
shows two copies of its autosomes: not haploid, and heterozygous past the 10.5% one genome reaches
once the array's drop-in is removed. Two X needs chrX inside the array's noise and under the copy
step; chrY with chrX short of a one-copy step is left unresolved rather than called 47,XXY. Every
unresolved reading says why, in the Sex chromosomes section. Against stated answers with no
parent: one X printed as XX on 3 of 28 (was female on 26), XX right on 6 of 12 (header was 12),
XY right on 10 of 15 (header was 13); no 45,X, 47,XXX or 47,XXY printed. Drop-in-corrected
heterozygosity overlaps between one-X and XX cells (0.064 to 0.110), so the rest stay unread with
that reason. With a parent loaded every reading is as 6.5.0, and every autosome record is
unchanged.
