A parent's own copy on a short segment
======================================
Scored on 15 November 2026 on open arrays only. Follows audit/truth/one-parent-segments-2026-11-15.txt.
The table is printed by audit/truth/parity.py from the run records.

WHAT WAS MISSING
----------------
The window scan for a parent's own copy needs 2,400 of that parent's homozygous markers in a window.
A father rebuilt from his pronuclei carries fewer than a measured one, and against him 6.2.0 printed 10
of the 13 stated losses of the father's copy on a segment; against the measured father, 13.

THE EXPERIMENT BEFORE THE RULE
------------------------------
Offline, the 31 Table S3 cells against the measured and the rebuilt father. The scoring of 6.2.0
pointed at the loaded parent's own alleles: at markers where that parent is homozygous, is the call
the opposite homozygote. With both copies the rate is e + d x q, fitted on the array's reference
chromosomes; with that parent's copy gone it is e + a x q, the rate the other parent's allele shows
alone. Runs scored by log-likelihood ratio divided by the array's clustering of this indicator.

  Against the rebuilt father all 13 stated losses read 58 or more. The three the tool missed read
  58, 129 and 132.

  Against the measured father 15 runs on stated-normal chromosomes reach the threshold of 15. Six
  are where the window scan already prints a loss. Of the other nine, eight fall well below the
  rest of their chromosome in intensity, seven of them below every same-length stretch of it, and
  one shows no change.

  Under 3 Mb, 4 of 896 runs on stated-normal chromosomes reach the threshold, three of them dense
  probe clusters under 0.2 Mb. A haplotype the parents share does not make an opposite homozygote,
  so this direction has no need of the 3 Mb floor of the other one.

THE RULE, written before it was built
-------------------------------------
  1. For each loaded parent whose genome the array carries, with founder frequencies, on a genome
     read as two parents', on the chromosomes read as carrying both.
  2. Rates e and d from the reference chromosomes, a straight-line fit per chromosome, median over
     chromosomes; a from the fit of 6.2.0.
  3. A run is printed where its divided score is 15 or more, it spans 1 Mb or more, and it is not
     under an intensity gain.
  4. Where the window scan already reports a segment for that parent over the run, that segment
     stands and nothing is added.
  5. No parent loaded, or an adult who is not a parent: nothing changes.

Struck from point 3 before any code was written: "and intensity also falls across it, by the tool's
calibrated bar". The bar was tested on the experiment's regions first. Across the genome, same-length
stretches of a single cell differ by 0.1 to 0.4, so two of the stated losses read 3.7 and 4.4 against
a bar of 4.5 and none of the eight falling stretches clears it; within one chromosome a long run has
fewer than the eight same-length stretches the tool requires before it quotes a figure. A run is
printed on the allele evidence, as every parental-absence segment is, with its intensity change
beside it.

Pass test: the rebuilt father prints the father's stated segment losses on 13 of 13; stated-normal
chromosomes left alone in each one-parent arm at least as in the two-parent arm; at least 9 in 10 of
the runs printed on stated-normal chromosomes either come from the window scan or are supported, by
a fall in intensity or by the same stretch in another array of the embryo; the mother's segment
losses, whole losses and named parents no worse than 6.2.0 and no wrong parent added; records with no
parent or an unrelated adult identical to 6.2.0.

ONE FAULT THE FIRST RUN SHOWED
------------------------------
270 rows. On two chromosomes with a stated gain of the OTHER parent the scan printed alleles absent,
and once more on a stated-normal chromosome against the rebuilt father. An extra copy of the other
parent outnumbers this parent's allele and the calls read as a loss. Intensity rises across those
runs, by 0.24, 0.30 and 0.40, which a lost copy cannot do. The rule refused a run under a gain only
where the intensity scan had called a gain segment, and that scan asks a larger rise than one extra
copy gives a single cell. Change: a run is also refused where its own mean intensity stands above
the median of the rest of its chromosome by 0.20 or more, the tool's gap between two copy states,
in both directions of the scan. The three stated losses the pass test counts fall by 0.6 to 0.8.
The rerun removed those runs, four records, and nothing else.
