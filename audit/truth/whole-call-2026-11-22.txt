A whole-chromosome call needs each arm to carry the change (plan item 3.4c)
==========================================================================

Written 22 November 2026, branch whole-call, built on 6.19.0 (530889a). The rule and its pass test
are written here BEFORE the build is run, and the results are appended under them. Nothing above the
RESULTS line was edited after the run. Open arrays and the lab cohort only. Lab cells are rows of
Table S2 and Table S7; answer-key cells are row indexes of the 330-row (no parent) and 270-row (with
parent) lists. No array is named here.

THE FAULT
---------
The whole-chromosome test (web/src/parentage.ts, classify) reads one median log2R per chromosome
and one call fraction. A change on one arm holding most of the chromosome's markers moves that
median past both gates, and a deep loss on one arm drops the whole chromosome's call fraction under
CALL_COLLAPSE. Either way the change prints as a whole-chromosome loss or gain. The 3.4a reading
(partLost.ts) reaches losses only, needs the loss scan to find the piece first, and asks the rest to
read at the cell's level.

DEFINITIONS (as fixed in the plan item, not moved)
--------------------------------------------------
- Arms: hg19 UCSC cytoBand, the p arm every marker before the boundary between the last p acen band
  and the first q acen band, the q arm every marker at or after it. 22 fixed positions, from
  audit/ucsc-hg19-cytoband.tsv. An array the tool does not read as GRCh37 has no arm table.
- Usable arm: at least 200 markers carrying a log2R (REST_MIN_MARKERS).
- The cell's level: lrrNull.centre, the centre the whole test reads against.
- Arm shift: the arm's upper-median log2R less the cell's level.
- Arm call fraction: the arm's called markers over its markers, over the cell's median autosomal
  call fraction (the callMedian classify uses).
- need: the floor the whole call needed, as classify sets it from the sign of the chromosome's
  shift: a loss 0.40, a gain 0.30; 0.14 for both on material read as bulk with spread under
  BULK_SPREAD_MAX and call rate at least 0.80.

THE RULE
--------
W1. Arms: the hg19 cytoBand boundary between p and q. An arm with fewer than 200 markers carrying a
    log2R is not usable.
W2. Each arm is read on its own, against the same centre as the whole test: its median log2R less
    lrrNull.centre, and its call fraction over the cell's median.
W3. An arm carries a whole loss where its shift is at or under -need/2, or its call fraction is under
    0.60 while its shift is under +need/2 (candidate A with the A2 clause). An arm carries a whole
    gain where its shift is at or over +need/2.
W4. A whole call stands only where both usable arms carry it. Where one does not, the whole call is
    withdrawn and the chromosome goes on to the piece scans like any chromosome not called whole:
    the loss scan and the gain scan both run on it.
W5. Fewer than two usable arms (chr13, 14, 15, 21 and 22 on this chip): the whole call stands as
    today.
W6. Very short arms: the 200-marker bar decides.
W7. 3.4a stays for what it does today. W4 runs first, so a chromosome W4 withdraws is never handed
    to readLostChromosome as a whole loss.
W8. Bars: need/2 is half the floor the whole call already used; 200 and 0.60 are existing constants.
    No new number.
W9. A withdrawn whole call never becomes silence. Where W4 withdraws a whole call and the piece scans
    print no change of that kind overlapping the arm that carries it, the tool prints that arm as the
    change: the same kind as the whole call, spanning the carrying arm from the p/q boundary to the
    chromosome end, marked as an arm-level call, with the edge at the centromere side stated as not
    placed and the extent inside the arm stated as not placed.
    - W9 reads an arm only on a chromosome the whole test has already called. It can narrow or
      remove a call and cannot add one.
    - Where neither arm carries the call, the whole call is withdrawn and W9 prints nothing; the
      piece scans decide.
    - W9 prints nothing for the arm that does not carry the call.

DECISIONS BEFORE THE BUILD (from the plan item, 22 November)
------------------------------------------------------------

  row 145, chr16); A withdraws none.
- The A2 clause goes in, recorded as written after the A count: an arm that reads raised cannot carry
  a loss on its call fraction alone. It uses need/2 and adds no number.
- W4 is in scope: both piece scans run on a chromosome whose whole call is withdrawn.

- An assembly the tool does not read as GRCh37 has no arm table: the whole call stands as today.
- Arm boundaries from audit/ucsc-hg19-cytoband.tsv, as 22 fixed positions.

Decided here, before any run, where the plan item is silent:
- D1. The rule reads the whole calls the intensity and call-rate test made (ChromResult.aneuploidy).
  A chromosome the alleles also read whole (lostParent set, or an allele gain or imbalance) is left
  as it is: those are the whole-alleles and parental-absence calls the plan item keeps out of reach.
  A whole call whose parent alone is named by the Mendelian channel is still the intensity test's
  call and is read by the rule.
- D2. W9's "change of that kind overlapping the arm" is a copy-loss (copy-gain) segment the copy
  scans or 3.4a give on that chromosome overlapping the carrying arm, read before the reporting
  filters. The arm call then goes through the same reporting filters and withholding gates as any
  piece of the copy scans, and is recorded in the same segment shape.
- D3. The arm call's coordinates: q arm from the boundary to the last marker of the chromosome; p arm
  from the first marker to the boundary. It carries no breakpoint interval; its edges are stated as
  not placed.

Owner's ruling on WC6, 22 November
==================================
WC6 as first written above was not met on one call: no-parent row 14, chromosome 19, gain. The result
above stands as it was scored. On 22 November the owner ruled that every gain piece passes one set of
gates, the arm-level call among them, so that a withdrawn whole call either prints a change or is
withheld with its named cause. WC6 reads: "every whole call W4 withdraws where an arm carries it
prints a change of the same kind on that arm, or withholds it with its named cause". Under that
reading WC6 is met. The one withheld call:
  no-parent row 14 chr19, gain; 19q arm call built by W9 and withheld by the drop-in gate on gained
  segments, cause "drop-in.no-alleles" (a drop-in cell with no parent shows no normal majority from
  the alleles); the refusal is recorded on the result.
