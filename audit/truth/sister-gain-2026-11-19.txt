A gain read where a cell of the same embryo shows the loss
==========================================================

Plan item 3.1, the wiring step. The rules and the choice to be made are written here BEFORE the
scoring run, and the results are appended under them. Nothing above the RESULTS line was edited
after the run.

CELLS ARE NAMED BY NUMBER AND NEVER BY THEIR ARRAY. The numbering is this file's own, "cell 1"
upward, and the key from number to array lives untracked at
audit/local/sister-2026-11-19/cell-key.tsv. No laboratory barcode, sample file name or accession
is in this file.

WHAT IS READ
------------
A copy that one daughter cell lacks went to the other, so the loss and the gain sit over one
interval: a whole chromosome that failed to separate, or the piece beyond a break. The loss is the
easy half, because it stops the array genotyping. The gain is the hard half: a third copy moves
intensity by a fraction of what a lost one does, and a genome-wide scan for it must clear a bar set
for every place it might be. Told where to look, it is one test.

For a cell and an interval another cell of the same embryo shows lost, on a chromosome this cell
reports no change on (web/src/sisterGain.ts):

  shift  the median of this cell's 5 Mb window medians inside the interval, less the median over
         the chromosomes sharing its commonest copy state
  z      that shift over the spread of equally long runs of windows on those chromosomes, the
         tested chromosome left out of its own null

Called at shift >= 0.25 and z >= 3. Intervals under two windows, cells under 8 reference
chromosomes and nulls under 20 runs are not read at all.

THE RULE ADDED AT THIS STEP, AND WHY
------------------------------------
The draft trusted the embryo label. On the laboratory's own arrays only 129 of 431 pairs sharing a
label read as one genome, and 29 of the draft's 30 calls came from a cell that was a different
genome. A sister must therefore be measured, not declared.

Two measures can do it with NO parent loaded, which is required because the cells this rule is for
have no parent on disk:

  agree     share of shared called markers that are identical (parentage.agreement). This is
            already the page's definition of a unit of one embryo, at
            abnormalities.SAME_EMBRYO_AGREEMENT = 0.90, strictly above. Measured separation when
            that was set: 95.8% within an embryo against 54.9% for a parent-offspring pair.
  opp rate  share of shared HOMOZYGOUS calls that are opposite (relatedness.oppositeHom). The rule
            as first written in the plan, at 0.02 or under. Measured then: groups reading as one
            genome at 0.000 to 0.012, the closest pair of different genomes at 0.027.

A third measure, otherParentLoss.sameGenomeShare, is not a candidate here: it reads the loaded
parent's homozygous markers and there is no parent.

DECIDED BEFORE THE RUN: both measures are printed for every pair of the 170 arrays
(audit/truth/sister-pairs.ts) and the one with the wider separation between pairs that read as one
genome and pairs that do not is the one that ships. Where they separate equally well, `agree` ships,
because a second measure would let one card call two cells sisters for this rule and a different
embryo for the timing rule, and that class of contradiction is what 6.16.0 existed to remove.

WHY AN OPPOSITE-HOMOZYGOTE RATE IS USABLE ON AMPLIFIED CELLS AT ALL
-------------------------------------------------------------------
relatedness.RELATEDNESS_SEPARABLE_MATERIALS admits bulk only, because dropout manufactures
opposite homozygotes out of a real relative. That holds for a parent against a child, who share
ONE allele at a marker, so a single dropout destroys the signal: first order. Two cells of one
genome share BOTH alleles, so an opposite homozygote needs dropout in both cells in opposite
directions at the same heterozygous marker: second order. Two different genomes differ at about a
quarter of markers outright, which is first order again. So the same quantity can separate one
genome from two on amplified cells while it cannot separate a parent from a stranger there. This is
the reasoning behind reading either measure on single cells and it is a claim the pair table tests.

ARRAYS READ
-----------
170 arrays, every one listed `open` in audit/local/truth/units.tsv, checked before the run. No
sealed and no exposed array was opened. The arrays are those appearing in a sister reading of the
257-cell run of 72 embryos.

WHAT MUST NOT MOVE
------------------
A cell with no sister in the run keeps every call, parent and coordinate it had. The reading is
additive and runs after every cell is scored.

RESULTS
-------

THE PRE-REGISTERED CRITERION COULD NOT BE READ OFF THE DISTRIBUTIONS. Over 14,365 pairs of the 170
arrays neither measure is bimodal at its threshold: identity runs continuously through 0.90 and the
opposite-homozygote rate through 0.02, and the set identity admits is a strict SUBSET of the set the
rate admits, 75 pairs against 220. So "wider separation" had nothing to read, and the question was
settled against truth instead, below.

IDENTITY OF CALLS IS FIRST ORDER IN DROPOUT AND THE RATE IS SECOND ORDER, MEASURED. Among the 624
pairs of the 170 arrays that share a laboratory label, identity correlates +0.772 with the pair's
lower call rate and the opposite-homozygote rate -0.445. Splitting those pairs at their median call
rate, identity reads 0.681 on the low half and 0.848 on the high half: BOTH under
abnormalities.SAME_EMBRYO_AGREEMENT, which is 0.90 because two trophectoderm biopsies concord at
95.8%. So a threshold set on biopsies is unreachable for blastomeres whatever their genome.

A DROPOUT-FREE STATISTIC WAS DERIVED AND IS USELESS. Under one genome with no copy change, at a
heterozygous marker and a dropout rate d, both-heterozygous is (1-d)^2, one-heterozygous is 2d(1-d)
and both-homozygous-opposite is d^2/2, so 8*oo*hh/hx^2 is 1 whatever d and whatever the genome's
heterozygosity. It behaves as predicted, correlating -0.064 with the implied dropout against -0.383
for identity, and it carries no signal about genome sharing at all: median 0.380 on true sister
pairs against 0.345 on the rest. Not used. Recorded so nobody derives it again.

THE CONFOUND THAT DECIDED THE RULE. Two cells of one embryo that lost opposite parental copies of a
region read as opposite homozygotes across that whole region. relatedness.ts had already measured
it: four of 122 same-embryo blastomere pairs do exactly that, at 0.053 to 0.056. That is FIRST
order, and it is the reciprocal event this rule exists to find, so a genome-wide comparison pushes
precisely the pairs worth reading above any gate while two siblings with quiet genomes stay under
it. The comparison is therefore taken off the chromosomes either cell reports a change on. A median
of 3 chromosomes per pair are left out, at most 10.

CALIBRATED AGAINST PUBLISHED EMBRYO MEMBERSHIP, WHICH IS INDEPENDENT OF THE GAIN COUNTS THIS ITEM
IS GATED ON. The 31 cells of the published series carry the paper's own embryo labels, giving 51
true sister pairs and 414 pairs of different embryos, of which 175 share an egg donor and are full
siblings. Numbering as audit/truth/page_says.py sets it, key untracked at
audit/local/page-says-2026-11-17/cell-key.tsv.

  rule                                     admits of 51 sisters   admits of 414 others
  identity > 0.90, genome-wide                       3                      0
  identity > 0.90, off changed chromosomes           4                      0
  opposite homozygotes <= 0.02, genome-wide         19                      5
  opposite homozygotes <= 0.02, off changed         23                      7
  identity > 0.86, off changed chromosomes          16                      0

  No pair of different embryos reaches 0.8577 off the changed chromosomes. That worst pair is
  cell 4 and cell 3, two trophectoderm biopsies of different embryos of ONE egg donor, so full
  siblings of good quality: exactly the case a rate cannot separate. The opposite-homozygote rate
  reads 0.0119 for them, under the 0.02 the plan first wrote, which is the published-trio finding
  of relatedness.ts repeating itself (sibling pairs at 0.0114, under the gate).

  SHIPPED: identity above 0.86, off the chromosomes either cell reports a change on. Perfect
  specificity on 414 pairs at the cost of sensitivity, which is the right way to be wrong: a pair
  not read leaves every call untouched, while a pair wrongly admitted can print a gain that is not
  there. This is a change from the rule the plan first wrote and from the threshold the page uses,
  and both reasons are above.

THE THREE ARMS, on the 31 published cells (S6). 89 readings, 86 rejected as not one genome, 3 read.
Zero calls and zero stated gains in every arm, and the three arms are identical, so no one-parent
arm is behind the two-parent arm. The reading uses no parent: a parent enters only through which
chromosomes a cell reports changed and which its reference is, and on these cells that did not
differ between arms.

THE SEGMENT CASE (S4), measured on the 170-array set under the opposite-homozygote rule that was
current when it was counted: 40 piece readings among sister pairs, 1 stated-gain test, called right,
and 2 stated-normal tests, neither called. Pieces behave better than whole chromosomes generally:
over every pair regardless of genome, 78 of 164 stated-gain piece tests are called against 207 of
682 whole-chromosome tests, with a false rate on stated normals of 2 in 1,633 against 45 in 10,568.

THE POINTER DOES REAL WORK, which is the one place the same-genome rule pays for itself. Putting the
same intervals on cells that are definitely NOT one genome (opposite homozygotes above 0.05), 72,489
readings give 1,693 calls: 14 distinct stated gains bought with 47 calls on stated normals and 66 on
stated losses. Under the same-genome rule the wrong calls are none. So the rule is not bookkeeping;
without it the reading is a low bar applied to arbitrary intervals.

THE 257-ARRAY SET UNDER THE SHIPPED RULE (S2, S3). 73,859 readings on chromosomes the reading cell
reports no change on. 73,557 rejected because the pair does not read as one genome; 302 read, 264
whole chromosomes and 38 pieces. 12 calls. Of the 302 readings only 3 fall on a chromosome any table
states a value for, 2 normal and 1 lost, and none of those three is called. So:

  stated gains added:            0
  calls on a stated normal:      0
  calls on a stated loss:        0
  calls on chromosomes no table states: 12

For comparison, the rule the plan first wrote (opposite homozygotes at 0.02, genome-wide) admitted
221 readings and 12 calls, of which 1 was a stated gain; that pair does not read as one genome under
the shipped rule, which is the same judgement that rejected the published sibling pair.

WHY THE YIELD IS ZERO, WHICH IS NOT THE GATE'S FAULT AND NOT THE RULE'S. The pairing is not the
bottleneck: the shipped rule finds 272 true-sister pairs over these 257 arrays and 140 of the cells
have at least one sister. The missing thing is the reciprocal configuration itself.

  stated gains on these open arrays                                   67
  of those, on a cell that has at least one true sister               11
  of those 11, where a sister reports the same chromosome LOST         0
  of those 11, where a sister reports that chromosome AT ALL           0
  of those 11, already called by the tool                              1 (as a gain)

  Ten of the eleven are whole-chromosome gains on cells whose sister says nothing whatever about
  those chromosomes: chromosomes 3, 4, 5, 7, 10, 11, 13, 14, 15 and 18. A cell carrying ten extra
  whole chromosomes is not ten independent mis-segregations with a reciprocal sister, so the premise
  this reading rests on does not hold there, and no threshold would change that.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE READING. It is correct, it is conservative, and on every array open to it it
adds nothing that any table can confirm or contradict. The 12 calls it makes sit on chromosomes no
table states a value for, so they can be neither credited nor faulted here. The mechanism will fire
on a run that holds a true reciprocal pair; the open set holds none for a published gain.

WHAT IS NOT PRINTED, AND WHY. These readings are recorded on the result and written to the log, and
nothing is printed on a card. A new kind of call may only state how often it is right from
pageSays.MEASURED, and the measured rate here is 0 of 0: there is no stated gain to score it on. A
card claim will wait for a run that supplies one. The reading is therefore additive in the strict
sense, and gate S5 holds by construction as well as by its check: a cell with no sister in the run,
or whose only companion is another genome, reports nothing.
