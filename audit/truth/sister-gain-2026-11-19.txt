A gain read where a cell of the same embryo shows the loss
==========================================================

Plan item 3.1, the wiring step. The rules and the choice to be made are written here BEFORE the
scoring run, and the results are appended under them. Nothing above the RESULTS line was edited
after the run.

CELLS ARE NAMED BY NUMBER AND NEVER BY THEIR ARRAY. The numbering is this file's own, "cell 1"
upward, and the key from number to array lives untracked at
audit/local/sister-2026-11-19/cell-key.tsv. No laboratory barcode, sample file name or accession
is in this file.

WHAT IS READ
------------
A copy that one daughter cell lacks went to the other, so the loss and the gain sit over one
interval: a whole chromosome that failed to separate, or the piece beyond a break. The loss is the
easy half, because it stops the array genotyping. The gain is the hard half: a third copy moves
intensity by a fraction of what a lost one does, and a genome-wide scan for it must clear a bar set
for every place it might be. Told where to look, it is one test.

For a cell and an interval another cell of the same embryo shows lost, on a chromosome this cell
reports no change on (web/src/sisterGain.ts):

  shift  the median of this cell's 5 Mb window medians inside the interval, less the median over
         the chromosomes sharing its commonest copy state
  z      that shift over the spread of equally long runs of windows on those chromosomes, the
         tested chromosome left out of its own null

Called at shift >= 0.25 and z >= 3. Intervals under two windows, cells under 8 reference
chromosomes and nulls under 20 runs are not read at all.

THE RULE ADDED AT THIS STEP, AND WHY
------------------------------------
The draft trusted the embryo label. On the laboratory's own arrays only 129 of 431 pairs sharing a
label read as one genome, and 29 of the draft's 30 calls came from a cell that was a different
genome. A sister must therefore be measured, not declared.

Two measures can do it with NO parent loaded, which is required because the cells this rule is for
have no parent on disk:

  agree     share of shared called markers that are identical (parentage.agreement). This is
            already the page's definition of a unit of one embryo, at
            abnormalities.SAME_EMBRYO_AGREEMENT = 0.90, strictly above. Measured separation when
            that was set: 95.8% within an embryo against 54.9% for a parent-offspring pair.
  opp rate  share of shared HOMOZYGOUS calls that are opposite (relatedness.oppositeHom). The rule
            as first written in the plan, at 0.02 or under. Measured then: groups reading as one
            genome at 0.000 to 0.012, the closest pair of different genomes at 0.027.

A third measure, otherParentLoss.sameGenomeShare, is not a candidate here: it reads the loaded
parent's homozygous markers and there is no parent.

DECIDED BEFORE THE RUN: both measures are printed for every pair of the 170 arrays
(audit/truth/sister-pairs.ts) and the one with the wider separation between pairs that read as one
genome and pairs that do not is the one that ships. Where they separate equally well, `agree` ships,
because a second measure would let one card call two cells sisters for this rule and a different
embryo for the timing rule, and that class of contradiction is what 6.16.0 existed to remove.

WHY AN OPPOSITE-HOMOZYGOTE RATE IS USABLE ON AMPLIFIED CELLS AT ALL
-------------------------------------------------------------------
relatedness.RELATEDNESS_SEPARABLE_MATERIALS admits bulk only, because dropout manufactures
opposite homozygotes out of a real relative. That holds for a parent against a child, who share
ONE allele at a marker, so a single dropout destroys the signal: first order. Two cells of one
genome share BOTH alleles, so an opposite homozygote needs dropout in both cells in opposite
directions at the same heterozygous marker: second order. Two different genomes differ at about a
quarter of markers outright, which is first order again. So the same quantity can separate one
genome from two on amplified cells while it cannot separate a parent from a stranger there. This is
the reasoning behind reading either measure on single cells and it is a claim the pair table tests.

ARRAYS READ
-----------
170 arrays, every one listed `open` in audit/local/truth/units.tsv, checked before the run. No
sealed and no exposed array was opened. The arrays are those appearing in a sister reading of the
257-cell run of 72 embryos.

WHAT MUST NOT MOVE
------------------
A cell with no sister in the run keeps every call, parent and coordinate it had. The reading is
additive and runs after every cell is scored.
