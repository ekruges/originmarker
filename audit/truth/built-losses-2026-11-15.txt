How small a loss the scans find, how exact its edges, and what settles a run under 3 Mb
=======================================================================================
Measured on 15 November 2026 on open arrays only. Follows audit/truth/own-copy-segments-2026-11-15.txt.
Produced by audit/validate/constructed-losses.ts.

WHY BUILT LOSSES
----------------
Table S3 states 20 losses on a segment and one of them is under 3 Mb, so neither the smallest loss
found nor the distance of an edge from the truth can be read off the answer key. A loss is built
instead, as the marker floor of the window scan was titrated: a cell published with a monosomy of a
named parent holds one copy of that chromosome with its own amplification artefact, and its rows over
a stretch replace the same rows of a cell of the same father whose chromosome is published normal.

  Recipients    the 16 cells with 19 or more autosomes published normal: 12 the tool reads without
                drop-in, 4 with it. A chromosome on which the cell as read carries a run is not used.
  Donors        for each chromosome, the cell with that monosomy that has the most chromosomes
                published normal. Two chromosomes for the mother's copy, two for the father's.
  Stretches     1, 1.5, 2, 3, 5 and 10 Mb, starting at 15%, 45% and 75% of the chromosome's markers.
  Arms          the measured father, and the father reconstructed from his pronuclei.
  Controls      a 10 Mb stretch from a cell whose chromosome is published normal.

2,515 scored constructions. Found means a run of the lost parent over the stretch, printed or
withheld.

A RUN UNDER 3 MB
----------------
With one parent and one array, a stretch the two parents share reads the same in the alleles as a
loss of the other parent's copy, so a run under 3 Mb is withheld (6.2.0). Two second lines were
tested on the built losses.

  Intensity. A run is printed where its mean intensity sits 4.5 or more of the array's own spread
  below the centre of stretches of as many markers on its other two-parent chromosomes, the tool's
  calibrated test. Of 108 built losses under 3 Mb that the alleles see against the measured father,
  2 pass; of 40 against the rebuilt father, none. The intensity of the built stretches reads a
  median of 1.0 of the spread below the centre. On single cells it settles almost nothing, which is
  what the rule was written expecting.

  Another array of the same embryo. Arrays of one embryo carry the same two parental copies, so what
  the parents share reads as missing in all of them and a loss in one does not. Another array of the
  run is taken as the same embryo where, at the markers where the loaded parent is homozygous and
  this array shows the allele it lacks, it shows that allele too, on 75% or more of blocks of 500
  markers scored against its own rates; and it settles a run where its own score for the other
  parent's alleles being present across the run is 15 or more.

    Same embryo or not, one array per biopsy of two embryos of one couple: 79% to 100% of blocks
    within an embryo, 31% to 69% between the two. On 25 arrays of three embryos, between arrays read
    without drop-in: 78% to 100% within, 2% to 70% between.

    Built losses with the other biopsies of the recipient's embryo supplied, 8 recipients without
    drop-in, 42 constructions a size:

                    measured father                      rebuilt father
                    printed    seen by the alleles       printed    seen
       1 Mb           24%          24%                      0%        0%
       1.5 Mb         57%          60%                     24%       24%
       2 Mb           69%          69%                     40%       40%
       3 Mb           81%          81%                     79%       79%

    Of the losses built under 3 Mb that the alleles see, 63 of 64 are printed against the measured
    father and 27 of 27 against the rebuilt one. On the 3 recipients with drop-in nothing under 3 Mb
    is seen.

    The shared stretch. One recipient carries, as read, a run of 1.6 Mb that the table states normal
    and that the experiment of 6.2.0 found in several cells of the couple. With four other arrays of
    its embryo supplied it stays withheld: none shows the mother's alleles across it. Arrays of the
    couple's OTHER embryo do show them there, so an array is asked only once it reads as the same
    embryo.

THE CONTROLS
------------
A stretch from a cell whose chromosome is published normal, 10 Mb, at each place.
  With the other arrays of the embryo supplied: 108 of 108 print nothing.
  In the main run: 174 of 177 print nothing against the measured father and 175 of 177 against the
  rebuilt one. The five that print are one recipient with one donor on one chromosome, and that
  donor is an array the tool reads with drop-in and withholds everything on: the harness took an
  array that prints no run for an array with no run. The harness now takes a control stretch only
  from a cell read as two genomes, with no drop-in, that prints no run.
  Rerun with that donor rule, 15 recipients: 165 of 165 print nothing against the measured father
  and 165 of 165 against the rebuilt one.

WHAT IS STATED
--------------
  With one parent loaded and no other array of the embryo, a loss of the other parent's copy is
  reported from 3 Mb, and found in 9 of 10 from 5 Mb against a measured parent and from 10 Mb
  against a parent rebuilt from its haploid cells.
  With another array of the same embryo it is reported under 3 Mb where the alleles see it: about 7
  in 10 at 2 Mb and 6 in 10 at 1.5 Mb against a measured parent.
  On an array read with drop-in a loss under 5 Mb is not found.
