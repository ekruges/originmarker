From the gamete or after fertilisation, across the cells of one embryo
======================================================================

RULE B1 (her method, in words)
------------------------------
Among the units of one embryo:
- Meiotic (from the gamete): every unit carries a change of the same direction on that chromosome,
  overlapping, and where a parent is named on each, the same parent. With fewer than 3 units it is
  "consistent with the gamete", never "meiotic", because two cells can share a mitotic error made at
  the first division.
- Mitotic (after fertilisation), reciprocal: one unit gains what another loses over an overlapping
  interval of the same chromosome.
- Mitotic, not in every cell: some units carry it and at least one unit reads that chromosome
  measured normal (a whole call of normal, no piece on it, and the cell reportable).
- Otherwise not called. One unit: not called.
This differs from 6.19.0 timing.ts in three places: the parent must match, the direction must
match, and "absent" needs a reportable cell that reads the chromosome normal, not just no event
listed.

How the words in the rule are read in the code, fixed here before the run:
- A change is a row of pageSays.defectsForResult, the rows the result card counts from. Gain:
  copy-gain, trisomy, segmental-duplication. Loss: copy-loss, parental-absence, monosomy,
  segmental-deletion. Every other kind (copy-neutral findings, the whole-chromosome imbalance) has
  no direction, carries no origin, and is not "normal" either.
- A whole-chromosome row spans the whole chromosome for overlap.
- "Another unit" for the reciprocal case is a unit other than the one the change is on.
- "Reads that chromosome normal": the chromosome is in the unit's measured list (`chroms`), no row
  of any kind sits on it, and the unit is reportable: stage not failed and no sample-level integrity
  refusal (neither integrity.disturbed nor integrity.uninterpretable).

WORDING, AS DECIDED
-------------------
- Three or more units all carrying it: "from the gamete".
- Two units: "consistent with the gamete (two cells read)".
- Reciprocal, or carried by some units while another reads that chromosome normal: "after
  fertilisation".
- Anything else, and any single cell: no origin word anywhere.
Every origin carries its reason with the units it read: how many carry it, how many read normal.
