3. WHAT 5.54.0 DOES

What the call rate and the intensity compute does not depend on whose array is in the parental
slot: per chromosome it is identical against the true father and against an unrelated adult on 31
of 31 Table S3 cells. So these rows are that part of the tool, read with a stand-in.

Breaks are not yet a fair test: the tool places a break from the parental-absence scan, which needs
a parent. With copy segments alone an edge lies within 5 Mb of 25 of 195 published breaks.


4. WHAT THE STATED GAINS ARE
----------------------------
42 of the 52 stated whole-chromosome gains on fresh arrays are in four cells.

Two cells, 12 stated gains each and no chromosome stated normal. The chromosome medians of log R
form two groups and nothing between: twelve at +0.40 to +0.52 and nine at -1.0 to -1.4. The table
reads the first group as trisomy and the second as lost. Intensity cannot say whether the twelve
are at two copies or three, because the cell holds nothing to compare them with. The tool calls the
nine losses and no gain.

Two cells, 9 stated gains each beside 10 or 11 chromosomes stated normal. The nine sit +0.45 above
the rest, seven times the spread of normal chromosomes elsewhere. The tool sees the shift and does
not call it: with nine of twenty-one chromosomes displaced, the median absolute deviation it scales
by is itself three times too large, and z reads 1.4. intensityNull.ts states that this estimator
holds to three or four affected chromosomes.

The remaining 10 gains are in four cells with at least 13 chromosomes stated normal.

The files carry the array software's own copy-number state per marker, a smoothed state the tool
has never read. Its share of markers in the gain state separates the stated gains from stated-normal
chromosomes almost completely when taken as an absolute number (47 of 52 at a 0.5% false rate) and
no better than log R when taken against the cell's own median (18 of 52). The stated gains follow
that track read on an absolute scale.

Monosomy and intensity. On Turocy arrays a stated monosomy moves log R clearly (16 of 20 found at a
0.5% false rate against a pooled spread). On the Table S3 cells it does not (median shift -0.12,
0 of 108 at the same rate). The two studies' amplifications do not carry dosage alike, so an
intensity rule set on one does not transfer.


5. WHO CARRIES WHOSE GENOME
---------------------------
825 arrays (the sealed ones left out) against nine arrayed adults: the rate of opposite homozygous
calls against each adult, as a fraction of the array's own median rate against all nine. Known
children of the GSE148488 sperm donor read a median of 0.38; unrelated genomes 0.8 to 1.3.

Experiment 1 (S20-10 to -19) was fertilised with the GSE148488 sperm donor. Its three pronuclei
labelled paternal read 0.03 to 0.04 against his public array, the level of the Experiment 2
paternal pronuclei against their own father, and its maternal pronucleus reads 0.02 against the
laboratory's maternal array. Experiment 1 therefore has BOTH parents arrayed.

Sibling embryos: four trophectoderm outgrowths (S18-29 to -32) and one ES line (S24-01) read 0.32
to 0.39 against S20-31, itself one of seven blastomeres of one embryo.

Outside GSE148488, 31 of 578 judged arrays carry the genome of one of the nine. No array of
S01 to S17, S19, S22 or GSE186407 does.

AN UNRELATED ADULT CAN PASS FOR A PARENT. Against the GSE148488 sperm donor, 25 cells of Turocy
embryos Z10 to Z14 read "disomic" on 13 to 22 chromosomes in the one-parent copy state, and were
staged diploid. They are not his children: along the genome their rate against him is at the
unrelated level in every 5 Mb window, with no stretch at the child level (median window rate 0.06
to 0.09 against 0.024 for his known children). Their whole-genome rate against him is lower than
against the other eight adults (ratio 0.63 to 0.86), which is shared ancestry, and that is enough
to cross the thresholds the copy state was set with on one family.

THE OPPOSITE ERROR. With an unrelated adult in the parental slot, 17 of the 31 Table S3 cells,
all carrying two genomes, are staged haploid.

A SHARPER READING, MEASURED BUT NOT YET A RULE (carry.ts). Where an adult is homozygous for an allele
the founder panel has at 0.2 or under, the share of an array's called genotypes that are homozygous
for the other allele, taken per chromosome and then as the median chromosome:

  one of the adult's haploid products          0.03 to 0.09     (8 pairs)
  a child of the adult                         median 0.13      (156 pairs; 5% to 95%: 0.05 to 0.48)
  a pronucleus of the other parent             0.78 to 0.83     (7 pairs)
  a stranger                                   median 0.71      (4,710 pairs; 5%: 0.56)
  a stranger of similar ancestry (Z10 to Z14)  median 0.45      (37 pairs)
  an array whose calls are noise               median 0.18      whoever the adult is

It separates a product, a child and a stranger where the whole-genome rate does not, and it shows
the two things a rule must handle: a noise array reads like a child, so the control class (the
adult homozygous for the COMMON allele) has to be read beside it; and ancestry alone moves a
stranger a third of the way to a child. Children of egg donor D read 0.39 to 0.48 against her
array, which has a third fewer homozygous calls than the other adults' arrays: her array, or the
manifest's assignment of those embryos to her, needs checking before it is used as a parent.

THE FAMILIES IN THE CORPUS. Against all 108 arrays of bulk quality, of 578 judged arrays outside
GSE148488: 244 have a first-degree relative among them and 111 share a genome with one (cells of an
embryo that also has an outgrowth, an ES line or a whole-embryo sample). Series S08, S11, S12, S21
and GSE186407 have none.


6. WHAT FOLLOWS
---------------
In the order the numbers above point to.

  a. Whose genome is this? Before any allele call, test the loaded parent along the genome
     (section 5's window test) and refuse a parent that is not one. Both errors of section 5 come
     from skipping it.
  b. A reading with no parent. Most published series have none. Today the tool cannot run on them
     at all; it should run and say what it can: stage, absent chromosomes, intensity.
  c. The whole-chromosome intensity null must survive a cell with many affected chromosomes.
     Section 4's two nine-gain cells are the test.
  d. Gains need answers that are not read off the same intensity. The reciprocal sister cells are
     the candidate: 5 stated trisomies on disk have a sister stated monosomic on that chromosome,
     and the 77 Turocy arrays not on disk hold more.
  e. The stage label tracks amplification quality, not material: half of all single blastomeres
     read "trophectoderm".
  f. Experiment 1 can now be scored with two parents.
