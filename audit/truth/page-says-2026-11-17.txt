The page says what it found (plan item 3.14)
============================================
17 November 2026. Branch page-says, built on call-plot-3 merged with 6.10.0. Measured on the 31
published cells of Zuccaro 2020 Table S3 (GEO GSE148488), every one a child of both donors, in
three setups: both parents loaded, the father measured, the father rebuilt from his haploid cells.

Sources: run.ts records of 6.10.0 (tags two, father, recon), the change lists the page itself builds
with both parents (cli/cardrun.ts over cli/exampleRun.ts), and the answer key
audit/local/truth/truth.tsv. Produced by:

  python3 audit/truth/page_says.py --truth audit/local/truth --records <h.jsonl> --cards <cards.jsonl>
    --fixture audit/truth/page-says-cells.json

The fixture holds, per accession and setup, what the headline reads and the word expected; the
check web/src/pageSays.check.ts replays it through pageSays.headlineOf and reads the MEASURED lines
below against pageSays.MEASURED.

THE HEADLINE RULE (P1, pageSays.headlineOf)
  Failed array                                   UNCLEAR: the array failed.
  Genome read as one copy, or neither parent     the class the genome-wide ratio gives, as before.
  A loaded parent's check does not carry them    UNCLEAR: that parent's genome is not shown.
  A loaded parent called absent genome-wide      UNCLEAR: two readings disagree.
  Copy number not read                           UNCLEAR: one genome cannot be told from two.
  Otherwise                                      BOTH PARENTS, or FATHER (MOTHER) + SECOND PARENT.
  The check is the per-chromosome parent check (parentGenome.ts). The genome-wide absence ratio is
  pooled over every autosome, the changed ones included, so it decides only where it calls a
  parent absent. The mother's genome-wide verdict is not in the 6.10.0 records; the fixture takes
  it as not absent, and the harness writes it from now on (genomeVerdicts, P6).

THE WORD ON EACH CHANGE (P2, P3, pageSays.kindOf)
  found two ways   a stretch the absence scan names, which the Mendelian test over the same
                   stretch also names for the same parent (both parents loaded)
  consistent       a whole chromosome with one parent's copy missing by the alleles; a stretch the
                   Mendelian test names with one parent loaded; a parent inherited from the
                   genome-level call
  one reading      every other printed stretch: the absence scan alone, the newer allele scans,
                   a stretch naming no parent, copy number from intensity, a parent named for a gain
  also seen        a whole-chromosome allele imbalance or mixed signal: listed, not a change
  Changes found two ways or consistent are counted in the headline. One-reading stretches are
  listed under them as "seen once, not confirmed" and are not counted. The record keeps every call.

  Against the table in the plan (step 1 audit): whole chromosomes read 89 of 96, 93 of 103 and

  intensity loss whose alleles also name the parent now counts as "whole", as the page groups it.

ONE COUNT, ONE SET OF COORDINATES, ONE PAIR OF NUMBERS (P4)
  The card's headline count, its change list, the PDF's summary and change table, and the figure's
  marks are all built from pageSays.cardSays. Pieces of one chromosome, gain or loss, parent and
  kind of call join into one row (the counting rule's unit). Every surface prints the refined
  coordinates (segmentCoords) with their edge intervals; the segment list that printed raw window
  edges is gone. The pair of numbers is the scan's own (rate against null rate), printed at one
  decimal on the card, the PDF and the figure's line; the figure no longer quotes a pair of its own
  where the call has one, and its intensity is the scan's. "0 of N measured at band A or B" is gone;
  the integrity alert that counted segments is no longer shown for a usable (disturbed) genome.

WHERE THE FIGURE SITS (P5)
  Head (headline, one line), the change list, the whole-cell figure, then "How this was read"
  folded: how sure each word is, what each figure row shows, the absence axes, material, the
  scans' explanation, extra copies, both-parent notes, sample detail.

THE HARNESS (P6)
  audit/truth/run.ts writes two fields more: genomeVerdicts (the loaded parent's genome-wide verdict
  and, with both parents, the mother's) and changes (each printed change with its band and the
  Mendelian test's verdict over it). On the Mac, two public cells (three rows) scored on main and on
  this branch are identical in every other field.

  The 600 rows on tesla (270 with a parent, list-h; 330 with none, list-np), scored on this branch
  and compared with the 6.10.0 records (sexnp9): 600 of 600 identical in every field but the two
  added; no call, parent or coordinate moved. With OM_SURFACES=1 the card, the PDF and the figure
  agree on all 600 (one count, refined coordinates, one pair of numbers).

  The fixture's one assumption, checked from the new field on the 31 published cells: the mother's
  genome-wide verdict is present on 12 and unclear on 19, absent on none, so no headline moves. The
  father's is absent on 2 with the measured father (the two cells read UNCLEAR, disagreement) and
  on none with the rebuilt father, as the fixture took it.

FIGURE LABELS (P8)
  Row labels say what the row is: "Father's copy", "Mother's copy" (whatever markers they are
  computed from), "Mother's copy, indirect" where heterozygous calls stand in for her, "Heterozygous
  calls", "DNA present", "Copy number". A parent's row reads "present" at the cell's normal level
  and "missing" at zero. The whole-cell figure's title states the finding from the same change list
  as the headline. The line under a stretch's bars states its own pair in plain words. How a bar is
  computed is in the hover and under "How this was read". No figure string says "letter";
  web/src/plotSeries.check.ts fails if one does, or if a row label names a calculation.

DRAFT CHANGELOG PARAGRAPH
  The result card says what was found. The headline reads each parent's genome chromosome by
  chromosome: on the 31 published cells it reads BOTH PARENTS on 24 with both parents loaded
  (was BIPARENTAL on 10, UNCLEAR on 21) and FATHER + SECOND PARENT on 26 with the father alone
  (was UNCLEAR on 26 and GYNOGENETIC on 2, which the paper contradicts; those 2 now read UNCLEAR
  with the disagreement named). The headline counts changes found two ways or consistent; a stretch
  seen by one reading is listed as seen once, not confirmed. Each change carries one word, with its
  measured agreement with the published karyotype one click away and in the PDF. One count, one
  set of coordinates and one pair of numbers per change on the card, the PDF and the figure. The
  figure sits under the change list, with a title that states the finding and rows named for what
  they show. No call, parent or coordinate changed.
