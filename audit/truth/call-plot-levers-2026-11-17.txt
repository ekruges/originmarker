Levers for the hard cases of the "letters present" figure. Rules written before any count.
17 November 2026. Open cells of the public Zuccaro 2020 series with a published karyotype (the 30 of
the study), stated
stretches as in study.py (band to GRCh37, guard at each edge). Never a sealed array.

THE FIGURE (what is drawn, per setup)
  both parents     P's letters present: share of called markers where the parents are opposite
                   homozygotes that read AB or P's homozygote.
  father alone     father's copy: share AB among father-homozygous markers where the other letter
  (measured or     shows (AB or the opposite homozygote).
  rebuilt)         mother's copy: share of called markers reading AB (two-letter calls), and the
                   share of father-homozygous markers reading the mother's letter alone.
  inferred mother  P's letters present as for both parents, with her genotype read off the run's
                   other embryos (lever 4).

HARD AND EASY (fixed before counting)
  hard: the cell is noisy (drop-in, below, over 0.15), or the setup is father alone or rebuilt
        father, or the stated stretch is under 15 Mb.
  easy: everything else (clean cell, both parents, stretch 15 Mb or more).

MEASURES
  beyond  share of inside bins past every bin on the same cell's normal chromosomes.
  AUC     inside bins against those bins.
  band    share of inside bins below the 10th percentile of those bins (the pale band's floor).
  in/out  median inside, median on normal chromosomes.

LEVER 1  Equal-precision bars: bins of 50, 100 and 200 of the quantity's own markers, against the
         5 Mb baseline. The last bin of a chromosome takes a remainder of half a bin or less.
LEVER 2  The cell's normal range: middle 80% (10th to 90th percentile) of the quantity's bins on its
         normal chromosomes, drawn behind the bars. Adds no separation by construction; measured as
         "band" above and judged on the pictures.
LEVER 3  Drop-in removed. d = the cell's rate of false two-letter calls:
           both parents: AB share of called markers where both parents are the same homozygote
                         (the tool's sameHom answer key).
           father alone: AB share of called father-homozygous markers where the founder panel holds
                         the allele he lacks at frequency 0 with n >= 100 (the tool's panel answer key).
         Both parents: corrected = (raw - d) / (1 - d), clipped to [0, 1]. Monotone within a cell, so
         beyond and AUC cannot change; only the levels change.
         Father alone, father's copy: corrected = (AB - d*N) / (OS - d*N), clipped to [0, 1], where N
         is the bin's called father-homozygous markers and OS those showing the other letter. Not
         monotone, so it can change separation.
LEVER 4  Mother read from the run's other embryos. Units: arrays of the same stated mother whose calls
         agree at 0.90 or more of shared called markers (every 10th marker) are one embryo; one array per
         unit (highest call rate). Draws: every unit but the sample's own; used where 2 or more.
         At a father-homozygous marker, with q the founder frequency of the allele he lacks, her
         posterior of being the opposite homozygote from the draws' shows (1 shows the allele he lacks,
         0 does not, no call skipped): prior q^2 / 2q(1-q) / (1-q)^2 for opposite / heterozygous /
         same; P(show) 0.85 / 0.44 / 0.03. Entered where the posterior is 0.5 or more.
         This approximates rule 4 of motherFromRun.ts with fixed rates instead of per-draw fitted ones.
LEVER 5  The running score. Per marker of the quantity's set, with x = 1 where P's letter is present:
         step = log P(x | copy missing) - log P(x | present), present rate r0 = the share on the cell's
         normal chromosomes, missing rate r1 = max(d, 0.02). Stretch measure: the sum over the inside
         markers against the largest sum over any run of as many consecutive markers on the normal
         chromosomes; "clear" where it exceeds it.

WHAT IS DRAWN, FROM THESE COUNTS
--------------------------------
Bars of a fixed number of the track's own markers (lever 1): 200 on the whole cell, beyond 0.95 easy and
0.47 in noisy cells against 0.85 and 0.30 for 5 Mb bins; on a stretch's own figure the bar shrinks to keep
six bars inside it. The cell's normal range is drawn behind the bars (lever 2): in noisy cells 0.82 to
0.88 of inside bars sit under its floor though few clear every other bar. Drop-in is not removed from the
bars (lever 3): with both parents it changes no ranking by construction, and with the father alone it
destroys the measure (beyond 0.56 to 0.08). The mother read from the run (lever 4) is drawn as her track
where the scoring pass reads her: beyond 0.74 to 0.75 with two draws of her against 0.56 and 0.00 without.
The running score (lever 5) is drawn under a stretch where both parents are measured, the only setup
whose missing rate the cell gives (the false two-letter rate): clear on every paternal stretch, noisy
cells included. With one parent the score needs a missing rate the cell does not give, so it is not drawn.
