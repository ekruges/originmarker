Which parent gave an extra chromosome, one parent loaded, with the other draws of the mother a run holds
=========================================================================================================
Written 16 November 2026 BEFORE the code below is built or run. Open arrays only. Plan item 3.0d, second
build. The experiments behind it (4 and 5, and the two checks asked before building) are recorded outside
the repository; this file carries the rule, every new constant with its origin, the pass tests, the
prediction and, after the run, the scored result.

WHAT IS MISSING AFTER THE FIRST BUILD
-------------------------------------
With one parent loaded the other parent is known only through the founders' frequencies, so a cell alone
names the parent of a constructed gain on 44 of 60 (measured father) and 37 of 60 (rebuilt father), and
on 81 and 74 of 158 held out, against 92 with both parents. A run holds other draws of the same mother:
her other embryos and her haploid products (a maternal pronucleus, a polar body). Each carries one of her
two copies. Read together they give her genotype at the loaded parent's homozygous markers, and the
two-parent test can then run on it.

THE RULE
--------
1. Arrays of a run that read as one genome are one embryo: the tool's same-genome share
   (otherParentLoss.sameGenomeShare, the lower of the two directions) at or over SAME_GENOME_SHARE 0.75,
   joined through shared partners. Only arrays with a rate fit (otherParentLoss.otherRates against the
   loaded parent) and a noise rate b under NO_TEMPLATE_BASE 0.30 are joined; an array outside that is
   not a draw. A pronucleus or a polar body stands alone because it shares no genome with any other
   array. The embryo is formed against whichever parent is loaded.
2. A unit (an embryo, by majority of its called arrays, a tie no call) is a draw of the mother only where
   its own rate fit shows the other parent's alleles (a >= 0.19) and its noise rate b is at or under
   DROPIN_CEILING 0.0525. The sample's own unit has to pass both too; a sample that does not is scored by
   the one-cell rules alone and its limits text says why the run's other arrays were not used.
3. Two units share a mother where the share of 10 Mb windows in which they carry the same copy of hers is
   at or over 0.272 (measured parent) or 0.100 (rebuilt parent). Per window, at the loaded parent's
   homozygous markers with founder frequency q of the allele it lacks from RARE_MIN 0.02 to under 1 that
   both units call: K = sum (sX - eX)(sY - eY) / sum aX aY q(1-q)/2, s = 1 where the unit shows the
   allele, e = b + a q from its fit. A window counts only with MIN_SHARE_MARKERS 100 such markers, and
   reads as sharing her copy where K >= 1. Units are joined through shared partners.
4. With 2 or more draws of her in the sample's group (its own unit never votes), at each loaded-parent
   homozygous marker with 0 < q < 1, the chance she is the opposite homozygote is the posterior over
   {opposite homozygote, heterozygote, his homozygote} (prior q^2, 2q(1-q), (1-q)^2), each draw
   contributing its own likelihood: shows with b + a, b + a/2, b; does not show with one less each.
   A marker enters where that chance is 0.5 or more, weighted by it, and with a rebuilt parent also by
   rule 2 of the first build. Per chromosome: the weighted median of the loaded parent's allele share at
   those markers, and its summed weight.
5. The two-parent test (gainOrigin.scanAlleleGains' bars: |z| >= ALLELE_GAIN_Z, |shift| >=
   ALLELE_GAIN_SHIFT, intensity >= ALLELE_GAIN_LRR_Z spreads; scale floors ALLELE_GAIN_SCALE_FLOOR and
   ALLELE_GAIN_LRR_SCALE_FLOOR; ALLELE_GAIN_MIN_REFERENCE reference chromosomes) then runs on that share,
   only on chromosomes rules 1 to 3 of the first build named no parent on. Its reference:
   a. it does not run where the cell's allele gains are withheld for want of a two-copy majority
      (gate normal-majority);
   b. the reference chromosomes are the copy state's disomic ones plus any it reads as a lost parent whose
      intensity is not below the cell's level (median lrrShift of the disomic chromosomes): a lost copy
      cannot raise intensity;
   c. the share reference and the tested chromosome need ALLELE_GAIN_MIN_INFORMATIVE 200 of summed
      weight (weight, not a count of markers); the intensity reference takes every chromosome of (b),
      with no minimum, because intensity needs no allele markers.
   A chromosome whose copy state reads a lost parent is tested only where the intensity scan calls it
   gained (first build, rule 1); one read as no template or undecided is not tested.
6. Both parents loaded, no parent, the loaded parent's genome not shown in the sample, or a run with fewer
   than 2 draws of the mother: nothing changes. The limits text says how many draws of the mother were
   used, or why none were.

EVERY NEW CONSTANT, AND WHERE IT COMES FROM
------------------------------------------
- 0.19, the fitted signal a above which a unit shows the other parent's alleles: the middle of a measured
  gap (units with no mother's genome, paternal pronuclei and two embryos, read 0.00 to 0.05; every unit
  with one reads 0.32 or more).
- 0.272 and 0.100, the same-mother bars for a measured and a rebuilt parent: 1.9 times the highest window
  share any pair of embryos of different mothers reached (0.143 and 0.052), the placing of 3.0b rule 6.
- 10 Mb, the window: the size the two bars were measured with. Shorter than the stretches over which two
  draws of one mother share one of her copies (her crossovers end them).
- 1, the window score that reads as sharing her copy: the middle of the two levels the arithmetic gives
  (2 sharing, 0 not).
- 0.5, the posterior at which a marker enters: more likely than not.
- 2, the fewest draws: the rule's own statement.
Existing constants by import: SAME_GENOME_SHARE, NO_TEMPLATE_BASE, DROPIN_CEILING, RARE_MIN,
MIN_SHARE_MARKERS, ALLELE_GAIN_* , rebuiltHomWeight's m from the rebuild's banner. The 0.999 cap on a
probability is the one sameGenomeShare uses.
The drop-in gate (DROPIN_CEILING on the fit's b) was added in experiment 4 after the data were seen, and is
on record as such: with noisy units in, no window statistic separated siblings from strangers.

MEASURED BEFORE BUILDING: THE EMBRYOS AGAINST A REBUILT PARENT
-------------------------------------------------------------
On the public run (106 children of the sperm donor), the same share and bar against the rebuilt father
alone give 22 embryos where the measured father gives 23; 18 are identical. Two arrays more pass as usable
(both still over the drop-in ceiling, so neither is a draw). Four merges: two embryos of one mother (label
A) become one unit; three units with no mother's genome merge (all fail rule 2 either way); one noisy array
joins a noisy embryo; one noisy array stands alone. The groups that hold the 60's cells (mothers B and D)
are unchanged. A wrong merge of two embryos of one mother costs a draw; it cannot add a draw from another
mother, and the sample's own unit never votes. Because a merge costs draws, the limits text states how
many draws of the mother were used.

DRAFT CHANGELOG PARAGRAPH (not in CHANGELOG.md)
----------------------------------------------
With one parent loaded, a run's other draws of the other parent now name the parent of an extra
chromosome. The run's other arrays are joined into embryos; embryos and haploid products that carry
one of the same mother's copies are grouped by how often, window by window, they share it; each votes
on her genotype weighted by its own drop-in and dropout, and the two-parent test runs on the result
where the one-cell rules named no parent. Constructed gains with the run's other arrays given: rebuilt
father 44 of 60 and 97 of 158 (was 37 and 74), measured father 47 and 99 (was 44 and 81), both parents
37 and 92, no wrong parent. Nothing changes for a cell read alone, with both parents or with none; the
limits text says how many draws of the other parent were used, or why none were.
