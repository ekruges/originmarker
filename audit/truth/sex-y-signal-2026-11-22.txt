A Y is read from the Y markers, not from the chip's calls (plan item 3.12d)
==========================================================================
Written 22 November 2026, before any code was changed or any run made. Built on 6.19.0 (530889a),
branch sex-y-signal. Open arrays only, plus the laboratory cohort the plan item measured. Lab cells
are named by Table S2 or S7 row; nothing here names an array.

THE FAULT
---------
The chip leaves chrY uncalled on any sample it reads as female from its X heterozygosity, so on this
chip "chrY has calls" and "chrX is heterozygous" are one signal. sexing.ts sexCall() needs the calls
for a Y, and sexChromosome.ts yPresent() takes that answer on this chip, so a cell with two X and a
Y that the chip left uncalled reads "no Y" and prints XX. The same calls-only input feeds the sperm
type (parentage.ts), the paternal-group naming (sexing.ts paternalGroup) and the parent checks
(parentSanity.ts).

THE RULE (plan item 3.12d, candidate B, as the owner ruled on 22 November)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Y present" rests on what the Y markers themselves show: their allele fractions first, their
intensity as a check. On this chip the calls are never evidence of absence. They may only block a
reading where they and the intensity both say the opposite.

R1. Allele fractions readable. The array's own homozygous autosomal calls read at least 0.90
    polarised (BAF under 0.15 or over 0.85).
R2. Y present where R1 holds, the Y BAF share is at least 0.75, and the Y intensity is at least
    Y_LRR_MIN (-0.5, unchanged).
R3. Y absent where R1 holds and the Y BAF share is at most 0.60, unless calls and intensity both say
    present. In that case: not read.
R4. Between (share over 0.60 and under 0.75, or share 0.75 or more with intensity under -0.5):
    today's reading only where calls and intensity agree with each other (both present: Y; no calls
    and intensity under -0.5: no Y). Where they disagree: not read.
R5. Allele fractions not readable: today's reading where calls and intensity agree (both present: Y;
    both absent: no Y). Otherwise not read. The "no call is no Y" default goes.
R6. A Y that is not read blocks every X-only constitution. No XX, "one X" or "XX or one X" is
    printed. The reason names what was missing.
R7. A Y present with a heterozygous X (the X in the female band, or X shown two copies):
    - The sex-chromosome line: "chrY is present: its markers read one allele each (n% of them, where
      this array's own homozygous calls read m%) at male intensity, though the chip left them
      uncalled; chrX reads two different X by its heterozygous calls. A Y with two different X is
      47,XXY or a mix of cells, which one array does not separate."
    - The header and Sample quality sex word does not say "female": "not read: chrY present with a
      heterozygous X".
    - With a parent loaded the same, as 3.12b already holds 47,XXY back on amplified material. On
      bulk DNA the existing 47,XXY rule stands.
R8. The bars come from the measuring pass (0.90, 0.75, 0.60). They are written here before any code
    and are not moved by the exit run.

Owner's rulings, 22 November:
  1. The bars stay as measured: 0.90, 0.75, 0.60. They are never moved, whatever the exit run says.
  2. Candidate B (the rule with R4), not A.
  3. R7 prints a FINDING, "47,XXY or a mix of cells", with the R7 reason beside it, not a bare
     "unresolved". In the records and the scoring harness it is its own state, never counted or
     stored as a 47,XXY constitution, an XX or an XY; so "no 47,XXY is printed" (Y1, Y2) means no
     plain 47,XXY constitution. The header and Sample quality sex word reads "not read: chrY present
     with a heterozygous X", never "female".

Measured as the measuring pass measured them: the Y BAF share over every chrY marker carrying a BAF;
the floor over every tenth autosomal marker that carries a BAF and a homozygous call. Where a
phrase of the R7 line is not true of the file it is not printed: "though the chip left them
uncalled" only where the calls are under Y_CALL_MIN, and the allele-fraction clause only where the Y
was read by R2.

Not touched: every autosome reading; COPY_SHIFT_FLOOR, HAPLOID_MAX_HET, Z_CHROMOSOME; Y_LRR_MIN,
Y_CALL_MIN, Y_MIN_PROBES; the callSex bands; the X readings of 3.12, 3.12b and 3.12c; the
intensity-only path for chips that never genotype chrY (Y_BESIDE_X); the thin-panel null.

HOW IT IS SCORED
----------------
The record runner (audit/truth/run.ts) runs once on 6.19.0 and once on this branch over the same
three lists (330 no-parent rows, 270 with-parent rows, 271 lab rows with no parent). Beside the
existing fields each record carries the Y reading and its reason, the sex-chromosome line's reason,
the sample's sex word as the page prints it, the parent checks and the sperm type, so Y3 and Y4 are
read from both runs rather than inferred. Truth is the plan item's (/root/om-sexy on tesla), joined
by array. The autosome fields are also compared with /root/om-g/second-pass-baseline.

Added after the first exit run (22 November, before any code for it)
=====================================================================
Why: Y3's line "never male where chrY reads absent" was written before the run and is not met by R1
to R8 as written, on 3 with-parent rows (chrX in the male band, chrY read absent by R3). 6.19.0
prints the same. R7 covers only the female side. The cause is one design fault: the sample's sex
word comes from callSex (X heterozygosity alone), a second source beside the sex-chromosome
reading. No bar is moved.

R9. One source for the sample's sex word. The header and Sample quality sex word of a SAMPLE is
    derived in one function from the X band and the Y reading together, the same Y reading the
    sex-chromosome line uses:
    - "male" only where chrY reads present and chrX is in the male band;
    - "female" only where chrY reads absent and chrX is in the female band;
    - chrY present with a heterozygous X: as R7 ("not read: chrY present with a heterozygous X");
    - chrX in the male band and chrY absent: "not read: chrX reads one copy by its calls and chrY is
      absent";
    - chrY not read: "not read: chrY was not read" with the same reason the sex-chromosome line
      gives, never "male" or "female";
    - the ambiguous X band keeps today's word.
    A loaded PARENT's own sex word is untouched (Y3's third line still requires it identical on all
    270 rows). Where two copies of the autosomes are not shown with no parent, the existing "not
    read" words stand (sexMaterial.ts), as today.

Added gate line Y3b: on every record, the sample's sex word and the sex-chromosome line never state
different sexes; counted and listed.
