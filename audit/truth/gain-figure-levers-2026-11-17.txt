Which quantity makes an extra copy show, and which says whose copy it is
=======================================================================

Offline measurement for plan item 3.8b, step 1. No tool code was changed and nothing here is a call.

What was measured
-----------------
Every candidate is binned at 2.5 Mb and 5 Mb (at least 8 markers a bin) and the bins inside a gain are
scored against the bins of the same cell's chromosomes published normal. Three numbers per event:

  out    the event's median sits outside the cell's own 10-90% range on its normal chromosomes
         (this is the band the figure draws, so "out" is what a reader sees)
  allc   the event's median is past every normal chromosome's median in the same cell
  auc    chance a bin inside the gain sits further out than a bin outside it

Event sets, all open arrays, each checked against units.tsv before it was read:
  built      the 60 open constructions (5 cells, chromosomes published normal, an extra copy of the
             measured father or of the mother added by re-weighting that cell's own allele intensities)
  published  53 stated gains on 30 open cells: 8 with both parents measured, 45 with no parent on file
Cells are given by number. The numbers are this study's own and the key is not in the repo.
Setups: both parents, measured father alone, rebuilt father alone, no parent.
"noisy" is a cell whose log R step-to-step noise is above the median of the 30 cells (0.385).

Candidates
----------
  copies (log R against the cell's own level)  how much DNA sits in a bin, drawn as copies
  baf spread at middle markers / at AB calls   how far the allele fractions sit from a half
  spread at the father's homozygous markers    the same, only where he carries one allele twice
  het share                                    share of markers the cell calls as two alleles
  father's share at opposite homozygotes       both parents: his allele's fraction where the parents
                                               differ. Even = 1/2, his extra copy = 2/3
  father's share at his homozygous markers      one parent: the same where only he is known, at markers
                                               read as two alleles (fhom_ab) or any middle marker (fhom_mid)

  noisy cells, no parent, whole      out 1.00  auc 0.96  shift +0.491  (n 13)

Noise in the cell is not what decides this: the noisy half reads better in both splits, because the
events there happen to be larger. What hurts is the reference. The quiet half of the no-parent cells
carries many extra chromosomes at once, so the cell's own level is raised and the band the figure
draws swallows the event. That is a limit of the reference, not of the quantity.

5 Mb bins beat 2.5 Mb slightly on published gains (out 0.78 against 0.72, same auc 0.93).

False alarms
------------
Every chromosome published normal, scored against the cell's other normals, gain side, 2.5 Mb bins:
  copies            out 0.01   (n 179 with parents, 369 with none)
  father's share    out 0.01 to 0.03 either side
  every baf spread  out 0.00 to 0.02
So the band itself is quiet: a stretch that leaves it is not a routine event.

Where the built gains flatter a quantity
----------------------------------------
The construction adds exactly one copy's worth of intensity at every marker and splits the alleles
exactly, so:
  - copies lands on 3.0 with none of the spread a real extra copy shows (built shift +0.588 against
    +0.315 on the real pieces with both parents);
  - the father's share lands on exactly two thirds or one third, where real cells overshoot or fall
    short (real pieces reach +0.33 on the same scale, built +0.167);
  - the genotype calls are not rebuilt, so het share cannot move at all on a built gain. Its real
    behaviour had to be read off the published gains, where it moves the WRONG way.
No picture should be drawn on built gains alone. They are kept here only as the clean case.

Does any quantity show the gains the tool misses?
-------------------------------------------------
Copies does, for most of them. Of the 53 published gains, the tool found 13 and missed 40 (28 under
the bar, 8 with no intensity reference, 3 printed as something else, plus one case below). The copies
median sits outside the cell's own band for 13 of 13 found and for 27 of the 39 missed, so a reader
would see the stretch standing clear on most of what the tool leaves alone. The 12 that stay inside
the band are nearly all in cells carrying many extra chromosomes at once, where the cell's own level
is no longer a flat reference.

One published gain reads the other way on its own array: a stated duplication of a 16q piece whose
intensity in that cell is 0.62 BELOW the cell's own level over the stated stretch, with the tool
printing a copy loss there. The figure would show a loss. That cell's label should not be used as a
missed gain.

What does not separate
----------------------
  het share            flat on built gains by construction, and on real gains it FALLS, the opposite
                       of what an extra copy should do (auc 0.05 to 0.30). Do not draw it.
  baf spread, whole chromosome, no parent   flat (auc 0.49 to 0.51, shift 0.000). In these single
                       cells the allele fractions already sit about 0.3 away from a half on normal
                       chromosomes, so a real one-third/two-thirds split is smaller than the cell's
                       own scatter and vanishes. It only works on the 16q pieces (auc 0.92).
  spread at opposite homozygotes   weak with both parents (auc 0.62 on built, 0.76 on published).
  father's share at middle markers (fhom_mid)   good on published pieces but inverted on built gains
                       (auc 0.19): the markers the extra copy drags into the middle window carry the
                       other parent's allele. Unstable, do not draw it.


COPIES FROM EACH PARENT: the product against its ingredients
============================================================
One quantity for the whole figure: how many copies each parent gave at each place.
  total copies      2 * 2 ** (log R - this cell's own median log R on its normal chromosomes)
  parent's share    the share of the DNA that is that parent's, from the alleles alone
  parent's copies   total copies * that parent's share
Normal is one copy from each parent. Measured on the 28 open cells with both parents measured and at
least three chromosomes published normal: 107 whole losses, 20 loss pieces, 6 published gains and
954 stated-normal tracks. 2.5 Mb bins. "out" is the stretch median leaving the range the cell holds
on its own normal chromosomes; "auc" is a bin inside the stretch against a bin on a normal one.

setup    kind         parent copies      parent's share     total copies
both     loss piece   out 1.00 auc 1.00  out 1.00 auc 1.00  out 0.80 auc 0.96   (n 20)
both     loss whole   out 0.82 auc 0.97  out 0.86 auc 0.99  out 0.42 auc 0.81   (n 107)
both     gain piece   out 0.80 auc 0.98  out 0.80 auc 0.98  out 0.60 auc 0.93   (n 5)
both     gain whole   out 1.00 auc 0.98  out 1.00 auc 0.97  out 1.00 auc 0.87   (n 1)
father   loss whole   out 0.49 auc 0.81  out 0.43 auc 0.76  out 0.42 auc 0.81   (n 107)
father   loss piece   out 0.80 auc 0.97  out 0.75 auc 0.97  out 0.80 auc 0.96   (n 20)
father   gain piece   out 0.60 auc 0.94  out 0.80 auc 0.93  out 0.60 auc 0.93   (n 5)
rebuilt  loss whole   out 0.46 auc 0.80  out 0.52 auc 0.82  out 0.42 auc 0.81   (n 107)

The product holds with both parents: it matches the better ingredient everywhere and never falls
below it. Where it earns its place is the cells carrying many changes, where the level the cell is
measured against is itself shifted: loss pieces in those cells read out 1.00 against 0.43 for the
total alone, gains 1.00 against 0.50. The split between the parents is read off the alleles and
needs no reference level, so only the total depends on one.

LEVEL BANDS, fixed before any picture was drawn
-----------------------------------------------
Boundaries chosen on the labelled stretches (expected none, one, two), weighting the three classes
equally so the 954 normal tracks do not swamp the 6 gains:
    none      below 0.68 copies
    one copy  0.68 to 1.65
    two       above 1.65
On that rule, with both parents: a copy that should be missing lands in "none" 96% of the time
(112 of 117), a normal track lands in "one copy" 92% (986 of 1075), and a doubled copy lands in
"two" 5 times of 6. The levels behind it:
    expected 0   median 0.20  (10-90% 0.10 to 0.61)
    expected 1   median 0.96  (0.77 to 1.18) on normal chromosomes
    expected 2   median 1.76  (0.97 to 3.19)

Two honest failures, both in the picture captions:
  - on a gain, the UNTOUCHED parent's track dips to about half a copy (median 0.46 over the 5 gain
    pieces) instead of holding at one. In one cell the doubled copy takes more than its share of
    what is read. The doubled track is the one to read; the other is not a level there.
  - the surviving copy beside a lost one reads a little high (median 1.32 on whole losses), because
    the cell's own level is pulled down by the losses themselves.

WITH ONE PARENT the two-track form must not be drawn
-----------------------------------------------------
Measured, father alone (and the same for the rebuilt father):
    a copy that should be missing   median 0.74 (10-90% 0.58 to 1.04)
    a normal track                  median 1.00 (0.84 to 1.12)
    a doubled copy                  median 1.29 (0.65 to 2.04)
The levels overlap almost completely, and the inferred other-parent track is no better (0.74 where
it should be none). The reason is in the markers: with both parents the split is read where the
parents carry DIFFERENT alleles, and with one parent that set cannot be built. At his homozygous
markers the mother often carries his allele too, and those markers carry no information but still
count. Restricting to markers the cell reads as two alleles is worse still (auc 0.42 on whole
losses, below chance), because a chromosome down to one copy has almost no two-allele calls left.
So with one parent the figure drops to the single total-copies track and names no parent, which is
the same form as the no-parent case. The total track is the same in every setup
(out 0.42 whole losses, 0.80 loss pieces, 0.60 gain pieces).

HETEROZYGOSITY, measured and left out of the figure
----------------------------------------------------
Taken from the same bins, as the share of markers a cell calls as two alleles, against that cell's
own rate: about 0.5 where one copy is missing, 1.0 on a normal chromosome, 0.8 on a gain. It falls
on a gain instead of rising, so it cannot separate a gain from a normal stretch, and in the
no-parent series it RISES to 1.4 where a copy is missing, because a chromosome with almost no
intensity left returns noisy genotype calls. It is not drawn.


WHAT THE FIGURE TAKES FROM THE HETEROZYGOSITY MEASUREMENT
=========================================================
Measured separately (het-and-copies-2026-11-17.txt) and adopted here, not re-measured:
  - a level of none cannot be read from intensity or from heterozygosity. A stretch with no
    template reads background in both and the caller still returns calls, 0.665 heterozygous at
    zero copies against 0.151 at two. What marks it is the call rate collapsing, 0.438 against
    0.852. The figure therefore draws a dashed outline, not a zero, wherever both tracks read none
    while the markers are still calling at the rate that cell holds on its own normal chromosomes.
    216 of the 1,038 both-none bins across these 28 cells are flagged that way, and 104 of 145 in
    one cell;
  - the boundary between none and one copy rests on the alleles, which this study's own numbers
    agree with (allele split out 0.86 on whole losses against 0.42 for intensity alone);
  - the boundary between one copy and two rests on intensity, where heterozygosity makes the
    comparison worse;
  - drop-out runs 0.203 to 0.509 between the tenth and ninetieth percentile and 0.568 at worst, and
    degrades smoothly with no safe level, so the figure shows each cell's own band as the width of
    its uncertainty and one of the worst cells is drawn rather than left out.
