47,XXY with a parent loaded (plan item 3.12b)
=============================================
Written 17 November 2026, before any code was changed. Built on 6.10.0 (5bc62ab), branch
xxy-with-parent. Open arrays only. Per-array tables are untracked, under the main checkout's
audit/local/sex-no-parent/.

THE FAULT
---------
With a parent loaded, web/src/sexChromosome.ts reads two X wherever chrX is not detected as a copy
step from the autosomes, and adds a Y where one is read: two X with a Y is 47,XXY. On amplified
single cells chrX in an XY cell often sits short of the 0.40 copy step, or past it but inside the
array's noise. Measured on the 6.10.0 records: 26 of the 270 with-parent rows print 47,XXY, 13
public cells (each as a father row and a two-parent row), every one with chrY present:
  - 10 cells amplified, chrX under the 0.40 step (-0.33 to +0.30);
  - 2 cells amplified, chrX past the step but inside the noise (-1.77, -1.78; z -4.5, -5.5);
  - 1 cell staged bulk, chrX at -0.12 (z -2.1).
Heterozygous calls on X read one X on 9 of the 13 (8 amplified, the bulk one) and are between
the bands on the other 4. One of the 4 is the cell an example run uses to show a loss.

THE RULE (plan item 3.12b, wording as corrected 17 November before any code)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
With a parent loaded, where 6.10.0 would print 47,XXY (two X by default, with a Y):
  R1. Heterozygous calls on X read one X (ingest.ts callSex, "male" band, its existing bands) and
      chrX sits less than two copy steps (2 x COPY_SHIFT_FLOOR) under the autosomes: XY. The
      reason names that evidence. Two X and a Y is contradicted by heterozygosity reading one X.
  R2. Otherwise, bulk DNA (the stage reads bulk, the condition 6.10.0 already passes as `bulk`)
      with chrX at the autosomes' level (inside the array's noise and under the 0.40 step):
      47,XXY, as before.
  R3. Otherwise, chrX two copy steps or more under the autosomes and still called: unresolved,
      with the 6.10.0 no-parent reason (further below than one copy, not shown absent either).
  R4. Otherwise: unresolved, with the reason that on amplified material (or, on bulk, with chrX
      not at the autosomes' level) this X level cannot separate one X from two.
Nothing else changes: the XX, XY, 45,X and 47,XXX readings with a parent, and every reading with
no parent (6.10.0's rule C), are untouched. No new constant.

The existing check that bulk DNA with chrX at the autosomes' level and a Y reads 47,XXY
(sexChromosome.check.ts section 3) defines no heterozygosity on X and no material. It is given
the heterozygous calls a two-X genome has (the female band) and bulk material, which is what the
case describes; its expectation, 47,XXY, is unchanged.

THE GATE (X1 to X4, plan item 3.12b, as corrected)
-------------------------------------------------

    and XY readings printed today stay. Every other field of every with-parent row identical to

X2. The check that bulk DNA with X at the autosomes' level and a Y reads 47,XXY passes.

FINDING, not built (for a follow-up item): a woman's bulk array reads chrY present
  With a parent loaded, the egg donor's array prints 47,XXY. On its own it calls 0 of 812 chrY
  markers and sexing.ts reads no Y. sexChromosome.ts yPresent then falls back to the
  intensity-only route written for panels that genotype no chrY (CytoScan): it treats "no chrY
  call at all" as a platform property and, on bulk DNA, compares chrY intensity with chrX (-0.31,
  against Y_BESIDE_X -0.9) and reads a Y. On this array chrY genotypes in men (the sperm donor
  calls 805 of 812), so zero calls in a woman is absence, not the platform. Three public egg
  donors call 0 of 812; their chrY intensity sits -0.12 to +0.70 from the autosomes. The 26 rows of
  this item are not affected: all 13 cells call 702 to 805 of 812 chrY markers.

DRAFT CHANGELOG PARAGRAPH (not in CHANGELOG.md)
-----------------------------------------------
**With a parent loaded, 47,XXY is no longer printed from an X level that cannot carry it.** The
tool read two X wherever chromosome X was not shown a copy step under the autosomes, and added a
Y: on amplified single cells an XY cell often sits short of that step, so 26 of 270 with-parent
rows, 13 public cells, printed 47,XXY, the example's loss cell among them. Now, with a chrY, a
cell whose heterozygous calls on X read one X is XY (unless chrX sits two copy steps down); bulk
DNA with chrX at the autosomes' level and no heterozygosity reading one X is still 47,XXY; every
other case is left unresolved, with the reason that this X level cannot separate one X from two,
or that chrX sits further down than one copy and its calls do not show it absent. On the 270
rows: 18 now read XY, 8 unresolved, none 47,XXY; nothing else moved, and with no parent nothing
changed.
