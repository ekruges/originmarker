Pre-registration: the gain gate's asymmetry, and what admitting it must not cost
================================================================================

Written 9 November 2026 against 5.48.0, BEFORE the change is made or measured. Scored against
audit/joy-dieter-baseline-2026-11-08.txt, which is the fixed baseline: gains 11 of 26, losses
44 of 67, 1 false segment across the 72 arrays with no hand call.

THE DEFECT
----------
scanCopyNumber rejects a candidate window on |delta| < SEGMENT_LRR_SHIFT, one magnitude for both
directions. The magnitudes are not symmetric. A hemizygous loss is log2(1/2) = -1.0 and a third
copy is log2(3/2) = +0.585, so a single gate asks 1.71x as much of a gain as of a loss and sits
above the event it exists to admit. The constructed gain controls in segments.ts passed it only
because the spliced donor block came from an array reading +1.60 to +1.95, which is amplification
distortion rather than a third copy.

THE CHANGE
----------
The gain arm is gated at SEGMENT_LRR_SHIFT x log2(3/2), which is 0.585 while the loss gate is 1.0.
The loss arm is not touched, so the ratio of gate to expected magnitude is the same in both
directions and whatever per-cell scale factor the amplification imposes cancels out of that ratio.
No per-cell estimation is introduced, because none is needed to make the two arms consistent.

PREDICTIONS, FIXED NOW
----------------------
P1  The four gain misses the baseline attributes to this gate, at deviations 0.815, 0.954, 0.994
    and 0.998, are all recovered. Gain recall goes from 11 of 26 to 15 of 26.
    FAILS if fewer than 4 of those 4 are recovered.

P2  Loss recall is EXACTLY 44 of 67, unchanged. The loss gate is untouched and the nomination
    stage is untouched, so any movement at all is a bug in this change and not a result.
    FAILS on any difference.

P3  The false arm does not get materially worse. 1 segment across 72 arrays today.
    SHIPS at 2 or fewer. DOES NOT SHIP at 3 or more, in which case the gate needs a companion
    requirement rather than a lower number: the obvious candidate is a pole-collapse veto, since
    audit/joy-gain-recall-splits-by-pole-2026-11-08.txt shows the damaged regions are separable.

P4  The recovered gains are the biology rather than more damage. At least 3 of the 4 read a
    homozygous-pole fraction above 0.60, which is the collapsed side of the split measured on
    8 November, where the gains the tool already finds sit at a median of 0.340.
    This one does not gate the release. It is the test of whether the change moves recall toward
    real third copies or just lowers a bar.

WHAT THIS CANNOT CLOSE, AND IS NOT CLAIMED TO
---------------------------------------------
The other 11 gain misses are not this gate. 6 are boundary disagreements, 4 of them within 0.06
of the 0.5 reciprocal-overlap bar, which is a scoring question and is to be reported as a
rescoring in its own row, never folded into recall. 2 are under MIN_SEGMENT_MARKERS at 3 Mb. 3 are
unexplained by any gate and stay open. So the ceiling on this change is 15 of 26 and a claim above
that from this commit alone is false.

Candidate windows are still nominated on the no-call indicator, which carries almost no
information about a real gain. That is why the 11 gains already found are mostly pole-collapsed
material. This change does not fix the nomination channel; it stops the gate from rejecting what
the nomination channel did manage to hand it.
