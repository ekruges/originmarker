BREAKPOINT REFINEMENT AND GAIN ORIGIN - ACCURACY AUDIT
======================================================

Status: PARTIAL. Two of four parts ran. The other two need arrays this machine cannot currently
read, and that is stated here rather than left as an absence.

  node --experimental-strip-types --max-old-space-size=8192 \
    audit/breakpoints.ts web/public/examples audit/breakpoint-results-public.json
  AUDIT_FLOOR=200 for the subset run; omit it for full arrays.

WHAT RAN, AND ON WHAT
---------------------
Public arrays shipped in this repo: web/public/examples, GSE145984 / GSE148488, 51,604 markers
per array. Reference parent GSM4472397 (sperm donor bulk gDNA, call 95.4%, het 16.8%). Hosts:
five pMII haploid meiotic products of that donor, all passing the 0.70 call-rate gate.

The lab arrays were NOT audited in this run. macOS denies this process access to the directory
holding them (EPERM on ~/Downloads, and on ~/Documents, while the repo reads normally). Nothing
in the code changed; the host permission did. Until that is granted the parts below marked
BLOCKED have no result, and no number from a previous session is carried forward into this file.


A. CONSTRUCTED EVENTS WITH A KNOWN BREAKPOINT           NOT POSSIBLE ON THIS MATERIAL
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
90 events planted by splicing one real product into another at a chosen marker index, so the
breakpoint is known exactly and the segment carries real amplification artefact. 0 detected.

That is a property of the material, not a failure of the scan, and it is worth stating precisely
because it bounds what public data can prove here. These are 51,604-marker SUBSETS: about 3,000
informative markers per chromosome against a shipped segment floor of 2,400. An event large
enough for the scan to reach would be most of a chromosome. Lowering the floor to 200 for the
run does not rescue it either: the likelihood-ratio threshold of 250 is not reachable by a
200-marker window at any realistic contrast, which agrees with the independently measured power
of 0.117 at 150 markers and 0.562 at 300.

So: the refinement arithmetic cannot be audited against known truth on the public subsets. It
needs full arrays. BLOCKED on the same permission as below.

D. GAIN ORIGIN                                          PASS, WITH ONE CORROBORATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1. Orientation invariance, on real markers rather than constructed ones.

An extra paternal copy pushes raw B-allele frequency DOWN where the father is AA and UP where he
is BB. A statistic that does not orient each marker first either cancels the signal or inverts
the call. Tested by reading the same physical dosage at both marker orientations:

  78,227 of 78,227 real markers invariant        no exceptions

D2. The homologue channel, run on every chromosome reading above twice its external background.

  GSM4774681 (pMII-2)  chr1    59.5%  vs  10.1%   ->  other homologue
  GSM4774685 (pMII-6)  chr4    14.3%  vs   7.0%   ->  other homologue
  GSM4774685 (pMII-6)  chr15   15.2%  vs   7.0%   ->  other homologue

The first of those is a genuine corroboration and the reason this audit exists. GSM4774681 was
described in this repo's own segment calibration as one of five products "present on every
autosome"; an external review found it carries a real chr1 event, and a check on the shipped
subset put chr1 at 9.78% ABSENCE against 1.04% genome-wide. This audit reaches the same
chromosome from a different statistic entirely - heterozygosity at donor-homozygous markers,
59.5% against a 10.1% background - and calls it the parent's other homologue. Two independent
channels, same chromosome, same array.

The other two are near the floor and should be treated as unresolved rather than as findings:
14-15% against a 7% background clears both the doubling and the 10% absolute floor, but sits far
from the 59.5% of the corroborated case.

WHAT D DOES NOT SHOW. There is no confirmed gain with a known parent in this material, so the
parental direction of a gain has never been shown to fire correctly on a true positive - only to
refuse correctly and to be orientation-safe. The same limitation was reached independently by an
external review of 34 trio-usable arrays: every dosage event in that series is a LOSS.


REFUSALS AND LIMITS, STATED
---------------------------
- Iso-duplication (an extra copy of the SAME homologue) is undetectable in every channel this
  array carries and is reported as such rather than as "no gain".
- Parental direction of a gain needs a biparental cell and both parents loaded.
- A focal gain on a WGA single cell is refused: the direction needs 400 informative markers,
  which span a median 47.6 Mb.
- Breakpoint refinement is not reported below a 0.70 call rate, and an event that does not
  localise says so rather than receiving a wide interval.
- Every breakpoint is reported as an interval. A point estimate would claim about 151x more
  precision than the calibration supports.
