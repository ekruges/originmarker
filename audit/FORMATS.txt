THE 25 INCORRECT CALLS: ONE FAILURE MODE, AND IT IS NOT FIXED
================================================================================================

Every incorrect call is an unrelated pair reported as related, and every one has the same cause.

The presence test is one-sided. "Present" means the observed absence is no larger than this
sample's own noise could manufacture, and that bound is the no-call rate times the heterozygous
fraction. It only says something when the bound is small. In the HapMap phase II+III merge many
individuals were typed on one panel only, so they carry ~35% call rates on this chromosome, and
the bound rises to 5-22%. An unrelated adult showing 7-10% absence then sits comfortably under
it and is reported as a parent.

  ceilings on the incorrect calls   median  20.8%  max  22.1%
  ceilings on correct unrelated calls median  0.73%

This is NOT fixed, and the reason is worth stating rather than hiding.

The obvious guard is to refuse a presence claim below the call-rate floor the tool already uses
elsewhere. That was implemented and reverted, because it breaks the case the method exists for:
Zuccaro embryo A8 is a TRUE father-offspring pair at a 53% call rate showing 9.69% absence,
which is higher than genuinely unrelated pairs at 5.1-5.6%. The per-sample ceiling is what lets
that pair be called correctly, and a call-rate guard turns it into a refusal. tests/test_origin.py
pins that behaviour deliberately.

Nor does the second axis separate the two. Measured here: a true father-child pair carries
alleles the parent lacks at 1.08x the second-parent expectation, unrelated spouses at 1.50-1.68x
-- but a real biparental embryo sits at 4.1x, on the same side as the unrelated pairs. A rule
using that axis would misclassify embryos to rescue adults.

So the honest statement is a limitation, not a patch:

  A presence call is only as strong as the ceiling it cleared. Where the ceiling is large --
  which means a low call rate -- "present" is consistent with a parent AND with a stranger, and
  the report says so through the ceiling it prints beside every call. Read the margin, not just
  the word. On these 25 the margins are 0.34x to 0.49x of a ceiling of 5-22%, against 0.01x to
  0.06x of a sub-1% ceiling on the true pairs in the same file.

The 132 correct calls include every one of the 52 documented father-child pairs and every one of
the 52 mother-child pairs, at 0.00x to 0.06x of their ceilings.

================================================================================================
WHAT REMAINS UNCOVERED
================================================================================================

  Illumina FinalReport (wide, long, and the [Header]/[Data] preamble), PLINK .bed/.bim/.fam and
  .ped/.map, and structural-variant VCF have readers and unit tests but are NOT covered here by a
  public file with a documented pedigree. No such file was located in a reputable archive during
  this pass. They are listed as supported on the strength of their unit tests, which is a weaker
  claim than the rest of this document, and that difference is deliberate rather than glossed.

  Both suites are one chromosome or one platform at a time. Neither says anything about how the
  tool behaves on a second array vendor.

  Research use only. Not a clinical diagnostic.
