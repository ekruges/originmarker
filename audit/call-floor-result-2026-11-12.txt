The call-rate floor, replaced by what it stood in for
=====================================================

Run 12 November 2026 on tesla. Scored against audit/call-floor-preregistration.txt, written after the
survey and before the change. Laboratory arrays are pseudonymised; per-array tables stay in audit/local.

WHAT CHANGED
  QC_CALL_FLOOR is gone. The information ratio refuses mostly-random calls at any heterozygosity, not
  only over the ceiling. Where a parent is loaded, the one-parent copy state supplies ploidy
  (stage.allelePloidy): disomic on half the stated chromosomes is two genomes and never haploid; one
  copy of one parent on NORMAL_MAJORITY_MIN of them is one genome, staged haploid where heterozygosity
  with the measured drop-in removed is at the haploid level, over or under the ceiling.
  Along the way the same fault appeared in a second floor. CALL_RATE_FLOOR (0.60) withheld zygosity
  below 60% called, and fires on 113 of the 1,001 surveyed arrays; the three pronuclei it was set on
  are GSE148488's three heaviest drop-in products. Zygosity is now read from the alleles where they
  decide: one genome or two by the copy state; both parents' alleles on two or more autosomes with
  drop-in removed, from the opposite-homozygote reading with two parents or from chromosomes reading
  in the disomic range (DISOMIC_LOW 0.343) with one. Where the band is still read, it is withheld when
  the measured drop-in reaches the band margin, HET_BAND_EXCESS, since drop-in alone fills it. The
  call rate stands in only where no founder panel is loaded.

KNOWN LIMIT, FOUND HERE AND NOT CHANGED
  Without a parent, heterozygosity cannot tell one genome from two on heavily amplified material:
  dropout lowers it and drop-in raises it. Of 708 arrays the stage calls diploid with no parent, 292
  read at the haploid level once their measured drop-in is removed. One is GSM4774687, a paternal
  pronucleus, haploid by micromanipulation, staged trophectoderm. With a parent loaded, as in every
  run of the application, the alleles decide; GSM4774687 is then haploid. Making the parentless
  ploidy of those 292 unknown would be right and has not been validated, so it is left as stated.
