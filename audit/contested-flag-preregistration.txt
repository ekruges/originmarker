THE CONTESTED FLAG AND THE LADDER: FIVE TESTS, WRITTEN BEFORE THEY ARE RUN

Written after an external review of 5.39.0 and committed before any of the five measurements
below is taken. Each test states what it expects and what would falsify it. A failed prediction is
recorded here, not argued away and not used to widen the test.

The review is audit/CONSULT-RESPONSE-segmental-parent-of-origin.md. Its headline claim is that the
contested set is mostly real nullisomy, resting on both counts being six: six cells carry
hand-called nullisomy or a failed karyotype, six cells carry contested pairs. The counts match.
Checking the committed truth table against the measured run, the CELLS do not: the overlap is
three of six, three of the contested cells carry zero nullisomic chromosomes and zero no-calls,
and the cell the argument rests on, GSM4552418, nullisomic on 21 of 22 autosomes, produces no
contested pair at all. So the question these tests answer first is not whether nullisomy explains
the contested set. It is why a cell that is nullisomic almost everywhere produces nothing.

THE DATA, UNCHANGED FROM THE PRE-REGISTRATION THESE TESTS FOLLOW

  arrays  GSE148488, Zuccaro et al., Cell 2020;183(6):1650-1664
  truth   Table S3, the authors' own by-eye karyotype, audit/zuccaro-table-s3.tsv, 682 calls
  cells   the 31 with both parents arrayed and a file on disk
  parents from GEO's own !Sample_source_name_ch1 via audit/geo_trio_manifest.py

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEST A. WHY A CELL THAT IS NULLISOMIC ALMOST EVERYWHERE PRODUCES NO SEGMENT

Two gates in scoreSample.ts could account for it, both deliberate and both documented:

  line 598  `uninterpretable = result.stage?.stage === 'failed'`, and line 826 sets
            `result.segments = []` when it holds. A cell with almost no DNA reads as failed
            amplification on heterozygosity and call rate.
  line 768  `lostByAlleles`, chromosomes the alleles already read as having lost a parent, which
            the absence scan then skips: "a segment found inside it would be the same absence
            described twice."

  A1. GSM4552418 reaches the segment scan with `stage.stage === 'failed'`, so it is gated at
      line 826 and returns zero segments of every kind.
  A2. On the cells that DO carry nullisomy and are not gated, the nullisomic chromosomes are
      absent from the scan because they are in `lostByAlleles`, not because the scan failed on
      them.
  A3. The three contested cells with zero hand-called nullisomy, GSM4552385, GSM4552386 and
      GSM4552420, are not gated: they reach the scan, and their contested windows are therefore
      not nullisomy under any reading of the hand karyotype.

  FALSIFIED IF: GSM4552418 passes both gates and the scan simply returns nothing on it. That
  would move the cause into the scan's window minimum or its null, which is a different repair
  from a gate, and this file would say so.

  WHAT IT DECIDES: whether nullisomy can reach the contested flag at all. If A1 and A2 hold, the
  review's Section 0 is wrong by mechanism rather than by coincidence, and reporting
  "both copies absent" on a contested window would be reporting it on the one population where
  nullisomy has been excluded by construction.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEST B. WHETHER THE CONFLICT BRANCH HAS EVER HAD THE CHANCE TO FIRE

23 of 69 segments carry a window allele share. defects.ts joins the share to the segment on a
string built from `segmentCoords(sg)` rounded to 0.1 Mb, so a share computed at edges that round
differently never reaches the segment and the row goes `unclear` without the conflict test running.

  B1. Of the 46 segments with no share on their own key, the number that have a share annotation
      on the SAME CHROMOSOME at a different key is reported. If it is above zero the join is
      lossy and "the branch has never fired" is partly an artefact of the key.
  B2. If it is zero, no share was computed for those windows at all, and the branch is untested
      because the second channel is silent rather than because the join dropped it.

  FALSIFIED IF: neither, which cannot happen. This test has no failure mode, only an answer. It
  is here because the answer changes what may be claimed about 23 of 23.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEST C. RECIPROCAL OVERLAP IN PLACE OF BARE INTERSECTION

The flag currently marks a pair contested on `!(a.startBp > b.endBp || b.startBp > a.endBp)`,
which at near-chromosome spans is satisfied by any two events on one chromosome. The replacement
is r = intersection / union >= 0.5, the conventional reciprocal-overlap criterion for calling two
interval calls the same event, not tuned here and not to be tuned on these cells.

The review names two false positives this would fix, GSM4472403 chr6 and GSM4472423 chr16. Neither
can trip the flag: both are same-parent pairs and the loop requires opposite parents. So the
prediction below comes from the measured pairs instead, computed on the window edges the run
logged.

  C1. 16 of the 18 pairs have r >= 0.5. The two below are GSM4552378 chr15 at 0.395 and
      GSM4552380 chr8 at 0.228. Three segments recover a parent, not four: the maternal
      0.2-135.0 Mb window on GSM4552380 chr8 is in a second pair at r = 0.668 and stays
      contested. Contested segments fall from 35 of 69 to 32 of 69, pairs from 18 to 16.
  C2. Detection is unchanged at 11 of 17 paternal and 5 of 10 maternal hand-called segmental
      events. The rule redistributes parents among regions already called and calls nothing new.

  MEASURED ON WHICH EDGES. C1 is computed on the scanning window edges, which is what the run
  printed. The test itself uses `segmentCoords`, which returns refined marker-resolution edges
  where refinement localised the event and window edges where it did not, because that is this
  repository's own answer to what a segment's coordinates are. Refinement can move an edge, so the
  two sub-threshold pairs may not be the same two. Both r values are printed per pair and whichever
  set it is will be recorded here rather than reconciled.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEST D. THE DENOMINATOR FOR AN ABSENCE SCAN

An absence scan cannot see a copy that is present twice. 5 of the 27 hand-called segmental events
are duplication-only, counted from the truth table:

  GSM4552380 chr16  dup 16q23.1-tel (mat)*
  GSM4552382 chr 6  dup(6q)(pat)
  GSM4472422 chr 6  dup (6q)(pat)
  GSM4552365 chr16  dup 16q21-tel (pat) FRA16I
  GSM4472403 chr18  dup18p (mat) FRA18E

  D1. Detection is re-denominated on the 22 deletion-bearing events. The reported figures become
      a fraction of 22 and of its paternal and maternal parts, and the five above are accounted
      for on whichever channel can see a duplication.
  D2. GSM4552365 chr16 is reported as called. Its kinds are printed. If a `parental-absence`
      segment is among them, an absence scan has fired on a duplication-only event and that is a
      defect, recorded here with the kinds that produced it.

  FALSIFIED IF: the 22 and the 5 do not sum to the 27 the table carries, which would mean the
  classification of a row as duplication-only is wrong.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEST E. THE RUN CHANNEL'S OWN ACCURACY, WHICH HAS NEVER BEEN PRINTED

The ladder puts the run that found a segment above the window allele share, on the argument that
the marker set makes the run definitional. The run's accuracy is assumed to be 1.0 and has never
been scored against anything. The share's is quoted as correct on every event the scan reaches.

  E1. On every detected hand-called segmental event where the hand names a parent, both channels
      are scored against the hand label and both counts are printed.
  E2. If the run is ever wrong where the share is right, the ladder is backwards and that single
      event outweighs the 23 agreements. If both read the same, the ordering is untestable on this
      corpus and is to be described as a convention with a stated rationale rather than as a
      validated ordering.

  FALSIFIED IF: nothing. Like B, this test has an answer rather than a failure mode, and it exists
  because "correct by construction" is an argument and not a number.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS NOT BEING DECIDED HERE

The operating point of the segment statistic. The review argues that adopting an operating point
and claiming its measured numbers are two separate acts, that a window derivable in advance from
this project's own contrast work may be adopted without a second corpus, and that only the claim
needs GSE60909. That argument is accepted as the better framing and is NOT acted on in this round:
the derivation depends on a Kullback-Leibler orientation the review could not resolve from the
record, which moves the admissible dropout from 43 percent to 76 percent, and on a null measured
on different material. Both have to be settled against the original dilution measurement first.

A1 holds. GSM4552418 reads stage `failed` at a call rate of 0.56 and returns zero segments of
every kind, gated at scoreSample.ts:826. So do the other two cells the review expected contested
pairs on, GSM4472409 and GSM4552422, both also `failed`. All three carry 10 to 21 chromosomes the
per-chromosome verdict already reads as not measured.

A2 holds in substance and not in the mechanism predicted. On the gated cells the nullisomic
chromosomes are not in `lostByAlleles` at all, they are `not_measured`: 21 of 22 on GSM4552418.
`lostByAlleles` does the excluding on the cells that are NOT gated, 8 to 12 chromosomes each.
The prediction named the wrong one of two gates and is recorded as such.

A3 holds. GSM4552385, GSM4552386 and GSM4552420 reach the scan at call rates of 0.70 to 0.80, on
stages that read blastomere and trophectoderm, and produce 4 to 7 contested segments each while
the hand karyotype reads zero nullisomic chromosomes on any of them.

WHAT TEST A DECIDES. Nullisomy cannot reach the contested flag on the cells the review's argument
rests on, because `stage === 'failed'` returns an empty segment list before the scan runs. The
coincidence of six and six was a coincidence: the cell lists overlap on three. Reporting a
contested window as "both parental copies absent" would be reporting nullisomy on the one
population where the hand karyotype excludes it.

TEST B. THE CONFLICT TEST WAS UNREACHABLE, AND BY MORE THAN THE REVIEW SUPPOSED.

  share annotation on the segment's own key            0 of 69
  annotation on the SAME CHROMOSOME, different key     6
  no annotation on that chromosome at all             63

Not a rounding problem. The annotation map in defects.ts is keyed on a coordinate string and
matched a parental-absence segment zero times in 69. The window allele share is on the segment, as
`sg.alleleGain`, and defects.ts never read it. So the conflict branch could not fire, and 5.39.0's
statement that the share supplies the confidence where the two agree was empty: no share ever
arrived, and no confidence with it.

Repaired by reading `sg.alleleGain` directly. The share is now present on the 23 of 69 segments
that carry one, the conflict test is reachable, and it fires zero times, because those 23 agree
with the run 23 of 23. Neither channel attaches a confidence: the run has no error model, and the
share's own posterior is over the tilt, which is a different question from which parent is
depleted. A parent named by the run now takes no number from any other channel either, which it
could before through a single-parent verdict that had named something else.

TEST C. PREDICTION WRONG IN THE DIRECTION THE FILE SAID TO WATCH.

C1 predicted 16 of 18 pairs at or above the threshold and three segments recovering a parent, from
r computed on the scanning-window edges. On the refined edges the test actually uses, 17 of 18 are
above it and ONE segment recovers: contested falls from 35 of 69 to 34, not to 32.

The reason is the one this file named in advance. Refinement moves the edges toward each other:
GSM4552378 chr15 reads r = 0.396 on window edges and r = 0.984 on refined edges, so it is one
region after all. 13 of the 18 pairs sit at r >= 0.98 once refined. The single pair that separates
is GSM4552380 chr8, 0.2 to 135.0 Mb maternal against 4.6 to 35.4 Mb paternal, at 0.211.

C2 holds. Detection is unchanged by the rule.

So reciprocal overlap is the right test and it changes almost nothing on this corpus: the pairs
here really are two runs describing one region, far more clearly on refined edges than on window
edges. The rule earns its place against a case this corpus does not contain rather than against
one it does, and that is what it is recorded as. The two same-parent pairs the review named as
false positives, GSM4472403 chr6 and GSM4472423 chr16, cannot reach the flag under either test:
the loop requires opposite parents.

TEST D. CONFIRMED, AND ONE DEFECT FOUND.

  hand-called segmental events                          27
  deletion-bearing, which an absence scan can see       22
  duplication-only, which it structurally cannot         5
  detected, of the deletion-bearing                     15 of 22
    paternal                                            10 of 14
    maternal                                             5 of 8
  detected, of the duplication-only                      1 of 5

The 22 and the 5 sum to 27. Every figure previously reported as "16 of 27, 11 of 17 paternal and
5 of 10 maternal" was computed on a denominator that included five events the channel cannot
detect, and counted one of them as a hit. The correct figures are 15 of 22, 10 of 14 and 5 of 8.

D2 found the defect it was written for. GSM4552365 chr16, hand-called `dup 16q21-tel (pat)
FRA16I`, is reported with kinds `copy-loss,parental-absence`. An absence scan has fired on a
duplication-only event, and the copy-number channel has called a loss where the hand reads a gain.
The other four duplication-only events return nothing, which is the expected behaviour. This is
recorded and not repaired here: it is a detection question on a different channel from the one
these tests are about.

TEST E. THE RUN CHANNEL NOW HAS A NUMBER.

  the RUN channel     right 15, wrong 0, no parental-absence segment 0
  the SHARE channel   right 11, wrong 0

On all 15 detected deletion-bearing events with a hand-named parent, the run names the parent the
hand names. The share speaks on 11 of the 15 and is right on all 11. Neither is ever wrong, and
neither is ever right where the other is wrong.

E2 therefore applies. The ladder's ordering is untestable on this corpus: there is no event that
discriminates between the two channels. It is to be described as a convention adopted for a stated
reason and not as a validated ordering. What has changed is that "correct by construction" is no
longer only an argument: the run is 15 of 15 against an independent by-eye karyotype.
