THE OPPOSITE-HOMOZYGOTE SEGMENT STATISTIC: ITS FLOOR DERIVED FROM MEASURED CONTRAST

Committed before the measurement. The rates come first, the floor follows from them, and the
statistic is then scored once against a bar fixed here.

A CORRECTION TO 5.43.1, WHICH THIS FILE EXISTS BECAUSE OF

5.43.1 concluded that the opposite-homozygote set carries the events the diluted set cannot, and
supported it with an arithmetic that put 245 markers at a likelihood ratio of 514. That used a null
of 0.021, which is the measured rate an INTACT chromosome reads on the ONE-PARENT-HOMOZYGOUS set.
It does not apply to the opposite-homozygote set and I carried it across without noticing.

On the opposite-homozygote set a marker is obligate heterozygous on an intact biparental sample, so
a single allele dropping out reads as that parent's allele being absent. At a per-allele dropout of
d the apparent absence is 2d/(1+d), split between the parents:

  d = 0.10   0.091 per parent        d = 0.30   0.231 per parent
  d = 0.20   0.167 per parent        d = 0.50   0.333 per parent

This project's own calibration says the same thing, measured on 595 autosomes: on intact
chromosomes one parent's share never reached 0.627, and on lost chromosomes the survivor's share
never fell below 0.735. So the real contrast on that set is not 0.021 against 0.90. It is somewhere
between those two figures and whatever a clean cell reaches, and the floor it implies ranges over
two orders of magnitude:

  event 0.735 against null 0.627  ->  9,535 markers for a likelihood ratio of 250
  event 0.800 against null 0.500  ->  1,297
  event 0.900 against null 0.300  ->    315
  event 0.950 against null 0.230  ->    206

chr16 carries 937 such markers whole and 245 inside 16q23.1-tel. So whether the statistic reaches a
16 Mb terminal event depends on the cell's dropout and not on a single fixed floor, which is
exactly what 5.43.1 asserted without measuring.

STEP 1. THE TWO RATES, MEASURED PER CELL AND PER CHROMOSOME

For every one of the 31 cells with both parents arrayed, over markers where the father and mother
are homozygous for OPPOSITE alleles, per autosome:

  the share reading the father's allele alone
  the share reading the mother's allele alone
  the share still reading heterozygous
  the informative count
  the hand karyotype's reading of that chromosome
  the cell's inferred dropout

  U1. On chromosomes the hand reads as INTACT the per-parent share is bounded above by 0.627, the
      figure this project measured on 490 such chromosomes, and it rises with the cell's dropout.
  U2. On chromosomes the hand reads as having lost one parent's copy the survivor's share is at or
      above 0.735, the figure measured on 105 such chromosomes.
  U3. The two populations do not overlap on any cell. If they overlap, the statistic cannot
      separate them at whole-chromosome scale either and nothing below is worth doing.

STEP 2. THE FLOOR, DERIVED PER CELL AND NOT SEARCHED

From the rates Step 1 measures, the markers a window needs to reach the calling threshold is
N = threshold / KL(event || null), with both rates taken from that cell rather than from a table.
This is the form an external review argued for and the reason it argued for it: a grid over a
quantity that is derivable is how the earlier segment grid walked to its corner.

No window minimum is proposed in this file. The floor is a function of the cell, and reporting that
function is the deliverable even where its value is too large to be useful.

STEP 3. ONE READ, AGAINST A BAR FIXED NOW

Truth is the authors' by-eye karyotype over the 595 autosomes. Scored for the shipped statistic on
the one-parent-homozygous set and for this one at its derived per-cell floor:

  hand-called segmental deletions detected, positionally, at reciprocal overlap 0.5
  chromosomes the hand reads as normal that receive a call
  chromosomes where the derived floor exceeds the markers available, which is the coverage endpoint
    and is reported rather than priced

  objective, one number, k committed here at 2.0 for the reason given in
  audit/null-replacement-preregistration.txt:

      detections  -  2.0 x (calls on hand-normal chromosomes)

THE BAR. It ships only if it scores higher on that objective AND scans at least as many
chromosomes. The precedent in this directory is a statistic that missed a two-part bar by one
chromosome and did not ship, and that is the standard here.

WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS A NEGATIVE RESULT, WHICH IS A USEFUL OUTCOME

If U1 shows the intact share rising with dropout to the point where the derived floor exceeds the
markers available on the events being missed, then the allele channel cannot localise a segment on
amplified single cells by any route, and the three missed terminal deletions are beyond it. That
would close the segmental question for this channel rather than leaving it open, and it is the
outcome the arithmetic above makes most likely.
