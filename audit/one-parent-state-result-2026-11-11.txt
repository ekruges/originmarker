The one parent calls MORE confirmed losses than two parents (90 against 82): the two-parent reading
needs opposite-homozygote markers and the one-parent copy state does not. It says fewer wrong things
on normal chromosomes overall (25 against 45). Its remaining cost is lostParent on published-normal
chromosomes, 9 against 4: three are on GSM4472413 (45,XY,-6, trio drop-in 0.12), r 0.162 and 0.180
on chr13 and chr15, under the 0.20 bar at a base of 0.16.

AGAINST THE PERSON, NOT THE MODEL
---------------------------------
The rebuild's contamination is a model figure. Against the measured array of the same man
(GSM4472397), the share of the rebuild's homozygous calls where he is heterozygous:

                                    model     measured
  5 pronuclei, no mask (5.51.0)     2.286%    3.249%
  8 pronuclei, masked (this)        1.067%    2.081%

The mask and the three admitted products take a third off the measured error. The model figure
understates it by about one point in both: it treats markers as independent, and the error comes in
haplotype stretches.

THE ONE STAGE DIFFERENCE. GSM4472405 read blastomere with two parents and trophectoderm against the
eight-pronucleus rebuild. The stage scales its heterozygosity ceiling by the loaded parent's
heterozygosity, and a reconstruction states an estimate from product disagreement: 15.5% from five
pronuclei, 19.3% from eight, for a man whose array reads 16.7%. That raised the ceiling by 1.15. A
reconstructed parent no longer scales it. Rerun: 31 of 31.
