THE REAL EVENT THAT WAS LOST, AND WHY THE OLD GATE HAD IT RIGHT BY LUCK.

GSM4472422 carries a hand-called maternal trisomy 15. Its share says maternal at 6.8 of that
cell's own share spreads, which is not in doubt. Its intensity reads +0.32 log2, which cleared
the old absolute floor. But that cell's per-chromosome intensity is wide: its 19 hand-normal
chromosomes run -0.18 to +0.22, so +0.32 is 1.8 of its own spreads and chromosomes 17, 19 and
22 sit at +0.18 to +0.22 with nothing wrong with them. Under the old floor those three would
have passed the intensity test as readily; they were not called only because their share did
not move. So the intensity in that cell cannot confirm an extra copy, the old gate was passing
noise, and the trisomy now leaves as an imbalance carrying both numbers and naming no parent.

That is a loss of information, not only of a false positive: the share names the parent clearly
and the gate discards it. A likelihood over the pair, which would weigh a decisive share against
a weak intensity instead of refusing on either alone, is the resolution and is not built.
