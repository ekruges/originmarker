WHAT THIS SHOWS, AND IT IS NOT ALL GOOD

1. A MOSAIC GAIN IS NOT DETECTED AT ALL. At a cell fraction of 0.5 the share should move by
   +0.10, and the measured median movement is +0.012. The reason is dropout: at these markers
   an amplified cell's readings are already spread toward 0 and 1, and scaling one allele by
   1.5 barely moves a marker already reading 0.95. A full extra copy, which scales by 2, does
   move it. So this channel reads a gain present in every cell and says nothing about one
   present in half of them, which is the commoner finding in an embryo biopsy.

2. THE INTENSITY GATE FAILS ON 3 OF 60 PARTIAL LOSSES AT HALF, and that is the error the gate
   exists to prevent. All three are chromosome 3 of two cells whose untouched chromosome 3
   already reads high: after a 50 percent loss removes 0.21 log2 the intensity still sits at
   +0.16 and +0.28, over the +0.15 floor, so a partial loss of one parent is reported as an
   extra copy of the other. 8 of the 60 stay over the floor and 3 of those also clear the
   share bar. The floor is absolute and the per-chromosome intensity of an amplified cell is
   not, which is the whole of the mechanism.

   The construction is harsher than the biology in one way and softer in another. A real
   partial loss also lowers the heterozygous fraction at these markers, which would often take
   the chromosome out of the biparental class before this scan sees it, and the construction
   does not model that. It also leaves the genotype calls alone. So 3 in 60 is an upper bound
   on a cell whose intensity is otherwise undisturbed, not a rate for real mosaic embryos.

   34 of the 60 were reported as an imbalance, which names no parent and is the intended
   outcome. 23 were not called at all.
