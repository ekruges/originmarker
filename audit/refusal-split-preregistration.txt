SPLITTING THE REFUSAL: FOUR MEASUREMENTS, FIXED BEFORE THEY ARE RUN

Committed before any of them is taken. A failed prediction is recorded here, not argued away.

WHY THIS EXISTS

`stage: 'failed'` is two verdicts sharing one string, and `scoreSample` keys `uninterpretable` on
the string, which returns an empty segment list before any channel runs.

  stage.ts:331  h > MAX_DIPLOID_HET, heterozygosity over 25%. Its own caveat says "two individuals
                mixed, a contaminated reaction and a failed array are not distinguishable from each
                other here". It omits a fourth cause that reads the same way: a genome with many
                chromosomes at odd copy number, which raises the apparent heterozygous rate.
  stage.ts:343  call < QC_CALL_FLOOR, call rate under 40%. An amplification failure. Unambiguous.

All four cells refused on the public corpus call at 0.53 to 0.75, above the call floor, so every
one of them is refused on heterozygosity. The hand karyotype calls three of them real biology,
"chaotic aneuploid" and "chr6p fragment only", and one of them failed. The tool refuses all four
identically and reports nothing about any of them.

The laboratory's position is on the record, 21 October: "I think the ones that are chaotic, so
there's a lot of aneuploidies, those are still important for biology ... knowing which one is
which is still going to be important", and in the same breath, "see what log R actually is
normalizing it to". Those are one request. The allele channels need informative markers and a
call rate. The intensity channels need a stable normal majority to normalise against, which is
exactly what a chaotic genome does not have. So the gate belongs per channel and not per cell.

WHAT IS ALREADY KNOWN ABOUT THE SEGMENT SCAN'S NULL, AND WHY IT CANNOT CARRY THIS ON ITS OWN

The shipped false-positive figure is 0 false segments over 110 chromosome scans on five paternal
pronuclei of the sperm donor, maximum score 139 against a calling threshold of 250. The repository
already records three problems with it, at segments.check.ts:8 and in the application text:

  1. pMII-2 carries a real chr1 event that sat inside the null and was counted as noise. Every
     false-positive rate derived from that set is optimistic by an unknown margin.
  2. pMII-6 chr20 is flagged at 12x by the same check, so the number of affected arrays is not
     established either.
  3. The threshold is fitted on the same null it is evaluated against.

So that figure is a baseline to reproduce, not a validated rate, and it says nothing about poor
material, which is the population a gate change would admit.

THE DATA

  arrays   GSE148488, Zuccaro et al., Cell 2020;183(6):1650-1664
  the MZ pronuclei: 8 maternal and 8 paternal single pronuclei aspirated from 2PN zygotes, with
  the sperm donor and an egg donor both arrayed in bulk. The dissection label is the truth: a
  paternal pronucleus carries the father's haploid genome and no maternal genome at all. That
  truth comes from the aspiration and not from any by-eye reading of the array, so it is
  independent of the karyotype table every other measurement here uses.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEST H. THE PRONUCLEI'S PARENTS, READ FROM THE DATA RATHER THAN FROM THE PAPER

GEO's `!Sample_source_name_ch1` names an egg donor for every sample except these fifteen, so
audit/geo_trio_manifest.py gives them no row and the pairing has been taken from the paper. The
opposite-homozygote rate can establish it without that.

  H1. Each paternal pronucleus reads 0.2 to 0.8 percent against the sperm donor and 6.5 to 16.6
      against every egg donor.
  H2. Each maternal pronucleus reads 0.2 to 0.8 percent against exactly ONE egg donor and 6.5 to
      16.6 against the others and against the sperm donor. That donor is the pairing.
  H3. The donor so identified is donor B.

  FALSIFIED IF: no egg donor reads as a parent of the maternal pronuclei, or more than one does.
  Either would mean the pairing cannot be established from the arrays and must stay cited to the
  paper.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEST I. THE BASELINE, ON CLEAN MATERIAL WHOSE TRUTH IS THE DISSECTION

Scored with ONE parent loaded, the sperm donor, on his own haploid products. His alleles are
present on every autosome, so every parental-absence segment is false. Both parents cannot be
loaded for this: with the mother loaded, every autosome reads as having lost her, which puts every
chromosome into `lostByAlleles`, and the scan skips those by design. The measurement would be zero
by construction and would mean nothing.

  I1. On the five pronuclei the shipped null was fitted on, the false segment count reproduces at
      or near 0 over about 110 chromosome scans.
  I2. pMII-2 chr1 is the known real event. It is reported separately and not counted as noise,
      which is the correction segments.check.ts already carries.

  FALSIFIED IF: the count is materially above 0, which would mean the shipped figure does not
  reproduce through the current pipeline and the discrepancy is the finding.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEST J. THE MEASUREMENT THAT DECIDES THE GATE CHANGE: POOR MATERIAL, OUT OF SAMPLE

pMII-5, pMII-7 and pMII-8 are haploid products of the same sperm donor at call rates of 0.54 to
0.59, reading 6.30, 11.08 and 4.56 percent against him where a product reads 0.42 to 0.52. They
are currently declined. They are NOT in the five the null was fitted on, so they are out of sample,
and their truth is the dissection rather than a by-eye karyotype. A gate that admits poor material
admits these, and every parental-absence segment on them against the sperm donor is false.

  J1. The false segment count on these three, with the stage declared so they are admitted, is
      reported per array and per chromosome scan. No prediction is made about its value: that
      number is the point of the test and guessing it first would invite reading the result to
      match.
  J2. Whatever it is, it is the specificity cost of admitting poor material, measured on truth
      that does not come from the same reading being tested. If it is high, the per-channel gate
      ships with a quality condition on the allele channel too, and that condition is derived from
      this measurement rather than chosen.

  FALSIFIED IF: nothing. This test has a value, not a failure mode.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEST K. WHETHER HETEROZYGOSITY SPREAD SEPARATES A CHAOTIC GENOME FROM A MIXTURE

The four causes behind one verdict differ physically. A chaotic genome is elevated on the
chromosomes at odd copy number and normal on the rest. Two individuals mixed, or a contaminated
reaction, is elevated at every marker on every chromosome, because two genomes differ everywhere.
So the SPREAD of heterozygosity across chromosomes should separate them where the genome-wide rate
cannot. The same reasoning has been used once in this project, on uniform absence at CV 0.20
reading as two genomes blended rather than as a parental absence.

  K1. The coefficient of variation of per-chromosome heterozygosity is reported for every one of
      the 31 cells, beside its stage, its call rate and its hand karyotype.
  K2. The three cells the hand karyotype calls chaotic, GSM4472409, GSM4472405 and GSM4552418,
      read a HIGHER CV than GSM4552422, which the hand calls failed throughout.

  FALSIFIED IF: the CVs do not order that way, in which case heterozygosity spread is not the
  separator and the taxonomy row stays as one row until a separator is found. No threshold is
  proposed in this file: a threshold fitted on four cells would be a threshold fitted on four
  cells.

THE GAIN IS ONE EVENT, NOT FOUR, AND THE SHORTFALL IS INSTRUCTIVE. Declaring the stage by hand
recovers all four hand-called del(6q)(pat) events on refused cells, each naming the right parent.
The automatic rule recovers one. The other three are GSM4552387, GSM4552388 and GSM4552389, which

documents: an array at 52 percent heterozygous and a 55 percent call rate was once classified as

chromosomes whose genotypes are not measuring them, and that is the correct behaviour.
