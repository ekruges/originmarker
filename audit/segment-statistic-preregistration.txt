THE SEGMENT STATISTIC: THE SPLIT AND THE OPERATING POINT, FIXED BEFORE THE RESULT

Written and committed before the held-out half is scored. Everything here is a decision about how
to measure, made without knowing what the measurement will say.

WHAT IS BEING DECIDED

The absence scan asks, at markers where ONE parent is homozygous, whether that parent's allele is
missing. Where the other parent is heterozygous a lost copy still reads present about half the
time, so the signal is diluted. The alternative asks the same question only at markers where BOTH
parents are homozygous FOR OPPOSITE ALLELES, where a lost copy shows at every marker in the window.
That set is roughly fifteen times smaller: about 1,034 markers per chromosome against about 16,000.

Whether the undiluted signal pays for the marker count is the question. It is not settled by
arithmetic: a power calculation written before any of this said the diluted set should win, and a
first look at all 31 cells said the opposite. That first look chose its operating point on the same
cells it then reported, which is why this file exists.

THE DATA, WHICH IS REAL THROUGHOUT

  arrays        GSE148488, Zuccaro et al. 2020. Affymetrix Axiom, 825,656 markers.
                Amplified single cells and pronuclei, with both parents arrayed in bulk.
  cells         the 31 that have both parents arrayed and a file on disk
  truth         Zuccaro Table S3, the authors' own by-eye karyotype of every arrayed cell,
                committed as audit/zuccaro-table-s3.tsv: 682 chromosome calls
  no simulation anywhere in this. Every genotype is measured, every truth label is the published
                hand reading of that same array.

THE SPLIT

By cell, never by chromosome: chromosomes in one cell share that cell's amplification and dropout.
Cells sorted by GSM accession, assigned by position, even to tuning and odd to held out. No
randomisation and no reassignment after seeing a result.

THE RULE FOR PICKING THE OPERATING POINT

Over the grid minWindow in {2400, 1200, 600, 300, 150} and LRT in {250, 150, 80}, on the TUNING
half only:

  pick the point that detects the most hand-called deletions, subject to calls on
  chromosomes the hand karyotype reads as normal being no greater, as a share of the
  normal chromosomes scored, than the shipped statistic produces on that same half at
  its own shipped setting of 2400 and 250.

Ties go to the higher threshold and then the larger window, which are the two more conservative
choices.

ON WHAT A CALL ON A HAND-NORMAL CHROMOSOME MEANS. It is not proven wrong. The authors tabulated the
events they were looking for, which were losses, so a real event they did not tabulate would land
here. It is used as a relative measure between two statistics scored on the same chromosomes of the
same cells, which it can support, and not as an error rate, which it cannot.

THE PRIMARY ENDPOINT

Hand-called deletions detected on the HELD-OUT half by the new statistic at the point the rule
picked, against the shipped statistic at its shipped setting on those same cells.

SECONDARY, ALL ON HELD-OUT CELLS

  segmental deletions only, which is the subset this work is about
  calls on hand-normal chromosomes, both statistics
  the full curve, so the chosen point can be read against what was available
  whether the two halves differ in the number of hand-called events they carry

THE BAR FOR SHIPPING IT

The new statistic replaces the shipped one only if, on held-out cells, it detects AT LEAST as many
hand-called deletions AND calls no more chromosomes the hand reads as normal. Better on one axis
and worse on the other is not enough: that is a trade, and a trade should be made deliberately
rather than inside a refactor. If it fails either test, the shipped statistic stays and this file
records that it failed.

Duplications are out of scope here. No absence scan can see a copy that is present twice, and that
needs a different detector, measured separately.
