THE SEGMENT NULL'S BREAKDOWN, AND WHAT THE OBLIGATE SET CAN NAME WITHOUT IT

Committed before the run. One pass, seven predictions, and the first three are an external review's
rather than mine.

THE DIAGNOSIS BEING TESTED

`externalNull` (segments.ts:233) is the MEDIAN per-chromosome absence rate over the sample's other
autosomes. Its own comment says a median is chosen so that several events cannot collectively raise
the bar. A median's breakdown point is 50 percent. By the published karyotype, 8 of the 31 public
cells carry 11 or more abnormal autosomes of 22, so on those the median IS an affected chromosome.

`scanChromosome` then rejects before scoring: `if (rate <= nullRate) continue` (segments.ts:274),
ahead of the likelihood ratio. So on a cell whose median absence rate sits at the level a real
event produces, every window on every chromosome is discarded untested, whatever the marker count.

A real single-parent loss does not drive absence to 1.0. At markers where the mother is homozygous
and her copy is gone, the sample reads an opposite homozygote only where the father transmitted the
other allele. The review puts that at about 0.100 from a fitted allele-frequency spectrum. An
independent figure from this project agrees: paternal pronuclei read 11.6 to 12.0 percent against
the egg donors, which is the same quantity measured on real arrays.

If both halves hold, one defect explains three findings: chr16 finding nothing with 14,000 markers,
the 35 contested windows, and the segments on 8 cells resting on a reference the sample cannot
supply.

A CORRECTION TO THE REVIEW, FOUND BEFORE RUNNING

The review says the discriminator for a both-parents window already exists as the survivor share,
and gives it as `max(onlyA, onlyB) / (onlyA + onlyB)`, which is 0.5 under symmetric dropout at any
rate and 1.0 on a real single-parent loss. The symmetry argument is exact: the array cannot know
which allele came from which parent, so dropout cannot be parent-specific.

The shipped code does not compute that. `hetCall` uses `onlyA / informative`, a share of ALL
informative markers, and `SURVIVOR_MIN = 0.70` is calibrated against THAT denominator: 595
autosomes, 105 hand-called losses never under 0.735, 490 intact never reaching 0.627. The two
differ wherever heterozygous calls survive, because `informative` includes them and
`onlyA + onlyB` does not. The review's version is invariant to dropout; the shipped one is not.

So the tally exists and the calibrated threshold belongs to a different quantity. Both are computed
below and neither is assumed to be the better one.

THE DATA

  GSE148488, Zuccaro et al., Cell 2020;183(6):1650-1664. The 31 cells with both parents arrayed and
  a by-eye karyotype in Table S3. Band coordinates from the UCSC hg19 cytoBand track. No simulation.

THE PREDICTIONS

Theirs, on the diagnosis:

  P1. On GSM4472423, GSM4552385 and GSM4552386, `externalNull` for the MATERNAL run on chr16 is at
      or above about 0.10, the rate a true maternal loss produces.
  P2. On those cells the 16q23.1-tel window's maternal absence rate is near that same figure and
      fails `rate <= nullRate`, so it is discarded before the likelihood ratio is computed.
  P3. On the six contested cells both runs' nulls are raised, and the contested windows' two
      absence rates are approximately EQUAL rather than both near the event rate.

      THE FALSIFIER, AND IT IS THEIRS: if the contested windows are strongly asymmetric, the
      dropout reading is wrong, their answer to the both-parents question falls, and so does the
      case for replacing the null. Recorded, not rescued.

Mine, on what can be named without the null:

  S1. On the four cells carrying a hand-called `del(6q)(pat)` that the heterozygosity ceiling
      refuses, chr6 reads a survivor share naming PATERNAL under at least one of the two
      normalisations. These cells are refused and `contribution` is not cleared by the refusal, so
      this is a parent call available today on an array the tool reports nothing for.
  S2. Measured directly from the two parental arrays, with no spectrum assumed, the rate at which a
      sample carrying one parent's allele alone reads an opposite homozygote against the other
      parent is between 0.08 and 0.14.
  S3. The opposite-homozygote marker count inside 16q23.1-tel is reported against
      `MIN_INFORMATIVE = 200`. The review scales it to about 171 and flags the figure as an
      estimate. This decides whether the obligate set can speak at window scale at all.
  S4. The two normalisations part company on the contested windows. The review's reads near 0.5
      there, by its symmetry argument. The shipped one reads low for an unrelated reason, because
      surviving heterozygous calls sit in its denominator. If they do not part company, the
      distinction does not matter and the shipped threshold can be reused.

WHAT IS NOT BEING DECIDED HERE

The replacement null. The review declines to propose one and gives the reason: on a cell with 15 of
22 autosomes affected, no robust estimate of location over the sample's own chromosomes can work,
so the replacement may have to be parent-referenced rather than within-sample. Candidates have
different failure modes and the choice is a separate pre-registration scored against the
595-autosome truth. Nothing is changed in this round on the strength of these numbers alone.

THE RESULT, WRITTEN AFTER THE RUN

Nothing above this line was changed. NULL_FLOOR is 0.002.

P1 FAILS. P2 FAILS. The diagnosis is right in substance and wrong in its mechanism.

  cell         nullPat  nullMat  rateMat      n   rejected before scoring?
  GSM4472423    0.0208   0.0140   0.0355   3912   no, it would be scored
  GSM4552385    0.0144   0.0280   0.1096   4095   no, it would be scored
  GSM4552386    0.0161   0.0282   0.1145   3590   no, it would be scored

The null IS raised, seven to fourteen times the floor, and nowhere near the 0.10 P1 predicted. So
the window rate exceeds it in all three cases and nothing is discarded by `rate <= nullRate`. The
pre-scoring rejection is not what suppresses these events.

WHAT SUPPRESSES THEM, with the arithmetic. The likelihood ratio a true loss can reach over the
minimum window of 2,400 markers, against the null the chromosome is scored at, with the event rate
measured below at 0.1244:

  null     LRT at w=2400   clears 250?
  0.0020             958   yes
  0.0140             403   yes
  0.0280             226   NO
  0.0605              67   NO

The boundary is a null of 0.0253. GSM4552385 and GSM4552386 read 0.0280 and 0.0282 with the event
present in the data at 0.1096 and 0.1145, which is full strength. They are scored and fall short,
by 226 against 250. Six times the marker floor cannot help, because the deficit is in the ratio and
not in the count.

GSM4472423 is NOT explained this way. Its null of 0.0140 is inside the detectable range and its
window rate is 0.0355, a third of what a true maternal loss produces. The event signal itself is
weak on that cell and the cause is still unknown.

So one of the three misses stays open and two are now explained exactly.

P3 HOLDS, 17 of 17.

  every contested window reads a ratio of max to sum between 0.503 and 0.586, against 0.5 for
  perfect symmetry, with both absence rates at 0.096 to 0.234

Both parents' alleles are depleted to nearly the same degree over every contested window, on all
six cells, with no exception. Those rates are at or above the event rate on both runs at once,
which no single lost copy can produce. The review's reading is confirmed on real data: these are
dropout, and the symmetry is the signature. The falsifier did not fire.

S1 PARTLY HOLDS, and it separates the two normalisations.

  cell          inf     het   onlyA   onlyB  shipped  review
  GSM4472405   1545  0.4395  0.0447  0.5159    0.516   0.920
  GSM4552387   1832  0.5895  0.0344  0.3761    0.376   0.916
  GSM4552388   1646  0.6215  0.1549  0.2236    0.224   0.591
  GSM4552389   1763  0.6064  0.0919  0.3018    0.302   0.767

`onlyB` exceeds `onlyA` on all four, and B is the mother, so the father's copy is the missing one
on every cell. That is the direction the hand karyotype gives, `del(6q)(pat)`, on four for four.

The shipped normalisation names none of them: it divides by all informative markers, and these
cells read 0.44 to 0.62 heterozygous, so the denominator is dominated by calls that survived. The
review's normalisation divides by `onlyA + onlyB` and is therefore invariant to that: it reads
0.920, 0.916, 0.767 and 0.591. So the correction found before the run matters, and the review's
form is the one that works on this material. What it does NOT have is a threshold: SURVIVOR_MIN at
0.70 is calibrated against the shipped denominator and cannot be carried across. Calibrating the
review's form is a separate pre-registration.

S2 HOLDS and the review's estimate was low by a fifth.

  a lost maternal copy, read against the mother:  0.1244 over 667,850 markers
  a lost paternal copy, read against the father:  0.1153 over 652,047 markers

Computed from the two parental genotypes alone with no spectrum assumed. The review derived 0.100
from a fitted Beta and flagged it; the measured figure is 0.115 to 0.124. It agrees with the 11.6
to 12.0 percent that dissected paternal pronuclei read against the egg donors, which is the same
quantity on real single-parent material and was measured independently.

S3 HOLDS, and the review's estimate was low by a third.

  chr16 whole:        937 opposite-homozygote markers
  chr16 16q23.1-tel:  245  against MIN_INFORMATIVE 200, which is ENOUGH

The review scaled it to about 171 and said to count it. Counted, it is 245, so the obligate set can
speak at window scale on this event after all.

S4 HOLDS. See S1: the two normalisations differ by a factor of two to three on exactly these cells.

WHAT SHIPPED FROM THIS, AND WHAT DID NOT

Shipped: `canDetectLoss`, which asks whether a window of the minimum size could clear the threshold
at all given the null this chromosome is scored against and the rate a true loss produces, and a
limit line naming every autosome where it cannot. Silence on such a chromosome is no longer
indistinguishable from a chromosome carrying nothing. `lossSignalRate` computes the event rate per
run from the two parental genotypes, so the test references the parents rather than this sample.

NOT shipped: any change to the null itself. The review declines to propose a replacement and gives
the reason, that no robust estimate of location over the sample's own chromosomes survives past
half of them being affected, so the replacement may have to be parent-referenced. That is a
separate pre-registration scored against the 595-autosome truth. Nor is the review's survivor-share
normalisation adopted, for want of a threshold calibrated against it.
