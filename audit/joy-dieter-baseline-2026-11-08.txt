THE GAINS SCOREBOARD, 8 NOVEMBER 2026
=====================================
The fixed baseline every later change to the segment detector is scored against. Same arrays,
same bar, both arms. Produced by audit/joy-dieter.ts at version 5.47.0, commit 47e48d4, with no
constant overridden.

Per-region rows and the false-call detail carry laboratory barcodes and are therefore untracked:
audit/local/joy-dieter-regions-2026-11-08.tsv and audit/local/joy-dieter-falsecalls-2026-11-08.txt.

HOW IT WAS RUN
--------------
Dr Egli's manual calls, audit/local/features/regions-dieter.tsv: 93 regions over 21 cells, 26
gains and 67 losses. The corpus is the 93 DIETER arrays on tesla at /opt/om/data/DIETER.

The 21 arrays carrying a hand call and the 72 carrying none answer two different questions, so
they were scanned as two sets of symlinks under /root/omsplit, the first in 3 shards and the
second in 6. One process over all 93 was measured at about 1.6 minutes per array, which is two
and a half hours; the split finishes in under an hour. Recall is merged from the per-shard tables
by keeping the rows whose array that shard actually loaded, which recovers all 93 regions exactly
once. The false-call counts are per-shard sums over disjoint arrays.

Reciprocal overlap required for a match: 0.5. That is a choice, not a law, and four gain misses
sit between 0.44 and 0.49.

WHAT THIS CONFIRMS AND WHAT IT CORRECTS
---------------------------------------
Confirms, against what was presented on the 7 November call: gains 11 of 26, the found-against-
missed size split of 69 Mb to 22 Mb, the marker split of about 22,000 to about 6,400, call rate
not separating the two, and one false segment across the 72 quiet arrays.

Corrects one statement made on that call. Losses were described as very good. Against these hand
calls at 0.5 reciprocal overlap they are 44 of 67, and 13 of the 23 misses are boundary
disagreements rather than absences. The 78 of 85 figure quoted in the same conversation is a
different measurement: which parent supplied a missing chromosome, on the Zuccaro series, which
says nothing about where a boundary lies. Both numbers are real and they are not about the same
question.

Also corrected: the deviations here are re-derived through the shipped scan, not taken from the
offline 5 Mb-bin script that audit/STATE.txt warns about. Four gains sit under the 1.0 gate by
both readings, which is the one figure the two agree on.

WHAT IT IS NOT
--------------
His calls are a comparison, not a truth. Both readings come from the same arrays and neither was
confirmed by dissection. A disagreement is reported as a disagreement.
